Week 4: Ultra-Convenient Variety Plan
====================================

This week is a celebration of vibrant textures and bold flavors with minimal kitchen time. You will enjoy a cheesy single-skillet baked ziti, super quick oven-baked chicken taquitos (using store-bought rotisserie chicken for 10-minute prep), spiced pork lettuce wraps topped with sweet fresh mango and avocado, a classic stir-fried garlic shrimp and asparagus, and a 20-minute vegetarian black bean taco.

Weekly Shopping List
--------------------

**Produce:**
- 1 head fresh garlic
- 1 bulb fresh ginger root
- 2 yellow or white onions
- 1 head fresh Bibb lettuce
- 1 1/2 lbs fresh asparagus
- 1 bunch fresh green onions (scallions)
- 2 green, yellow, or poblano bell peppers
- 1 bunch fresh basil
- 1 bunch fresh mint
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro
- 1 fresh avocado
- 1 fresh mango
- 1 fresh lime
- 1/2 head fresh red cabbage (or pre-shredded cabbage mix)

**Meat, Seafood, & Dairy:**
- 2 cups shredded cooked chicken (deli-roasted/rotisserie chicken is perfect)
- 1 lb ground pork
- 1 lb large shrimp (peeled, deveined, tails removed)
- 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1 wedge or container Parmesan cheese
- 1 cup Feta cheese
- Sour cream and/or Mexican Crema
- Butter

**Pantry & Spices:**
- 12 oz Ziti pasta (or Penne)
- 22 small flour tortillas
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans
- 1 cup corn kernels (canned, fresh, or frozen)
- 1/3 cup Salsa Verde
- 1 can (7 oz) chipotle peppers in adobo sauce
- Soy sauce
- Dry sherry (or Shaoxing wine)
- Sherry vinegar
- Sriracha sauce
- Olive oil & vegetable oil
- Cornstarch & sugar
- Spices: Red pepper flakes, ground cumin, garlic powder, salt, black pepper
- Guacamole and hot sauce (optional, for serving)
