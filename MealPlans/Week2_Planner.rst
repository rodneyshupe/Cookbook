Week 2: Ultra-Convenient Variety Plan
====================================

This week features a wonderful trip around the globe, rotating between a quick Italian sausage gnocchi skillet, Chinese wok-seared savory lettuce cups, a sizzling pan of Tex-Mex steak fajitas, griddled Baja tequila-lime fish tacos, and a hands-off bubbling baked tomato basil gnocchi.

Weekly Shopping List
--------------------

**Produce:**
- 1 head fresh garlic
- 1 bulb fresh ginger root
- 3 medium onions (red and/or yellow)
- 3 red bell peppers
- 1 yellow or green bell pepper
- 1 head fresh butter lettuce
- 1 bunch fresh green onions (scallions)
- 1 bunch fresh basil
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro
- 3 fresh limes
- 1/2 head fresh red cabbage (or pre-shredded cabbage mix)

**Meat, Seafood, & Dairy:**
- 1/2 lb hot Italian sausage
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 1/2 lbs steak (flank, flat iron, skirt, sirloin, or hanger)
- 1 lb white fish fillets (tilapia, cod, or snapper)
- 12 oz shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1 wedge or container Parmesan cheese
- Shredded cheese (Jack and Cheddar mix, for fajitas)
- Sour cream and/or Mexican Crema

**Pantry & Spices:**
- 2 packages (16 oz each) vacuum-packet gnocchi
- 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 20 small flour tortillas
- Hoisin sauce
- Asian chili sauce (like Sambal)
- Soy sauce
- Sesame oil
- Tequila (1/4 cup needed)
- Olive oil and vegetable oil
- Spices: Chili powder, ground cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, red pepper flakes, salt, black pepper
- Salsa and chopped peanuts (optional, for serving/garnish)
