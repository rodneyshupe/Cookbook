Week 5: Ultra-Convenient Variety Plan
====================================

This week delivers an incredible array of vibrant, chef-inspired meals requiring minimal cleanup and quick prep times. You will enjoy a rich and spicy Roman-style vodka pasta, baked Greek chicken meatballs served with seasoned orzo and fresh salad, an amazingly succulent and rapid broiled pork tenderloin, prosciutto-wrapped crispy lingcod, and a cozy bowl of 20-minute red curry noodles with toasted coconut garlic.

Weekly Shopping List
--------------------

**Produce:**
- 1 head fresh garlic
- 1 bulb fresh ginger root
- 1 medium red onion
- 2 cups chopped zucchini or summer squash
- 1 bunch fresh dill
- 1 bunch fresh mint
- 1 bunch fresh parsley
- 1 bunch fresh basil or cilantro
- 1 fresh lemon
- 1 fresh lime
- Fresno peppers (for serving garnish)
- Salad ingredients (cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, olives for Greek Salad)

**Meat, Seafood, & Dairy:**
- 1 lb ground chicken or turkey
- 1 lb pork tenderloin (trimmed)
- 12 oz ling cod (2 x 6 oz fillets)
- 4 thin slices prosciutto
- 1 large egg
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 block butter
- 1 wedge or container Parmesan cheese
- Feta cheese & Greek yogurt or mayonnaise (for Greek salad & meatballs sauce)

**Pantry & Spices:**
- 1 lb rigatoni or penne pasta
- 1 1/2 cups orzo pasta
- 8 oz rice noodles
- 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
- 1 can (14 oz) full-fat coconut milk
- 1/4 cup vodka
- 1 1/4 cups Panko breadcrumbs (1 cup for pasta, 1/4 cup for meatballs)
- 3 tablespoons finely shredded coconut
- 2-3 tablespoons Thai red curry paste
- 1 tablespoon low-sodium soy sauce or tamari
- 2 teaspoons agave syrup or honey
- Sesame oil & olive oil
- Vegetable oil
- Kitchen twine (for pork tenderloin)
- 1 disposable aluminum roasting pan (13x9-inch)
- Spices: ground cumin, dried oregano, crushed red pepper flakes, smoked paprika, kosher salt, black pepper, flaky salt
