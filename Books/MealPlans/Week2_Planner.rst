Week 2: Ultra-Convenient Variety Plan
=====================================

This week rotates beautifully between a comforting Italian sausage gnocchi skillet, a festive Taco Tuesday featuring rich and smoky chicken tinga, a sizzling pan of Tex-Mex steak fajitas, a quick Asian-style chicken lettuce wrap, and a hands-off bubbling baked tomato basil gnocchi.

Weekly Shopping List
--------------------

**Produce:**
- 1 head fresh garlic
- 1 bulb fresh ginger root
- 3 medium onions (red and/or yellow)
- 1 large yellow onion, halved (for Tinga poaching liquid)
- 3 red bell peppers (2 for fajitas, 1 for lettuce wraps)
- 1 yellow or green bell pepper (for fajitas)
- 1 head fresh butter lettuce (for Chicken Lettuce Wraps)
- 1 bunch fresh green onions (scallions)
- 1 bunch fresh basil (for Friday gnocchi)
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro (for Chicken Tinga and Lettuce Wraps)
- 3 fresh limes (for fajitas)

**Meat, Seafood, & Dairy:**
- 1/2 lb hot Italian sausage
- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs (for Chicken Tinga Tacos)
- 1 lb ground chicken (for Chicken Lettuce Wraps)
- 1 1/2 lbs steak (flank, flat iron, skirt, sirloin, or hanger)
- 12 oz shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1 wedge or container Parmesan cheese
- 2 tablespoons cotija cheese (for Tinga garnish)
- Shredded cheese (Jack and Cheddar mix, for fajitas)
- Sour cream and/or Mexican Crema (for fajitas)

**Pantry & Spices:**
- 2 packages (16 oz each) vacuum-packet gnocchi
- 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes (for Monday gnocchi)
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes (for Friday gnocchi)
- 1 can (28 oz) peeled plum tomatoes, San Marzano preferred (for Tinga)
- 1 can (7 oz) chipotle peppers in adobo sauce (for Tinga)
- 4 cups chicken stock (for Tinga)
- 1 can (8 oz) sliced water chestnuts (for Chicken Lettuce Wraps)
- 12 small flour or corn tortillas (for fajitas and Tinga)
- Hoisin sauce (for Chicken Lettuce Wraps)
- Peanut butter (for Chicken Lettuce Wraps sauce)
- Chopped peanuts (for Chicken Lettuce Wraps garnish)
- Soy sauce
- Sesame oil
- Apple cider or rice vinegar (for Chicken Lettuce Wraps)
- Olive oil and vegetable oil
- Honey (for Chicken Lettuce Wraps sauce)
- Spices: Chili powder, ground cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, dried oregano, red pepper flakes, salt, black pepper
- Salsa (optional, for serving)
