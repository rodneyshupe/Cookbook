Week 3: Ultra-Convenient Variety Plan
=====================================

This week features a beautiful rotation of comforting, flavor-packed meals with absolute ease. You will enjoy a single-pan Italian sausage and orzo skillet, a 2-minute cook-time chipotle chicken taco (prepped up to 24h ahead), a tender slow-cooked sweet and savory Mongolian beef, a quick Tuscan shrimp and bean skillet, and a colorful hands-off Mediterranean sheet pan roasted gnocchi.

Weekly Shopping List
--------------------

**Produce:**

- 1 head fresh garlic
- 1 bulb fresh ginger root
- 2 yellow or white onions
- 1 small red onion
- 2 bell peppers (any color)
- 1 zucchini
- 1 small bunch fresh asparagus
- 1 cup shredded carrots
- 1 bunch fresh green onions (scallions)
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro
- 1 bunch fresh basil
- 1 fresh lemon
- 1/3 cup sun-dried tomatoes
- Fresh or dried oregano & rosemary

**Meat, Seafood, & Dairy:**

- 1 lb Italian sausage
- 4 chicken thighs (approx. 1 lb)
- 1 1/2 lbs flank steak
- 1 lb large shell-on shrimp (peeled, deveined, shells reserved)
- Feta cheese (crumbled, for gnocchi garnish)
- Butter
- Shredded cheese (Jack/Cheddar mix)

**Pantry & Spices:**

- Orzo pasta (at least 1 cup needed)
- 1 package (16 oz) vacuum-packet gnocchi
- 10 small tortillas (flour or corn)
- 1 can (14 oz) stewed tomatoes
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 cans (15 oz each) Cannellini beans
- 1 can (7 oz) chipotle peppers in adobo sauce
- 2 anchovy fillets (canned or jarred in oil)
- Soy sauce (reduced sodium preferred, at least 3/4 cup needed)
- Chicken stock or broth (at least 2 cups needed)
- Cornstarch (at least 1/4 cup needed)
- Brown sugar & honey
- Sriracha sauce
- Olive oil & vegetable oil
- Spices: Chipotle chili powder, garlic powder, paprika, ground cumin, red pepper flakes, salt, black pepper
- Avocado cream (optional, for tacos)
