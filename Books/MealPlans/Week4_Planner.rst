Week 4: Ultra-Convenient Variety Plan
=====================================

This week celebrates vibrant textures and bold flavors with minimal kitchen time. You will enjoy a cheesy single-skillet baked ziti, a festive Taco Tuesday with vegetarian black bean tacos, spiced pork lettuce wraps topped with sweet fresh mango and avocado, a classic stir-fried garlic shrimp and asparagus, and finish the week with a flavourful marinated Greek chicken served with naan.

Weekly Shopping List
--------------------

**Produce:**
- 1 head fresh garlic
- 1 bulb fresh ginger root
- 2 yellow or white onions
- 1 head fresh Bibb lettuce (for pork wraps)
- 1 1/2 lbs fresh asparagus
- 1 bunch fresh green onions (scallions)
- 2 green, yellow, or poblano bell peppers (for pork wraps)
- 1 bunch fresh basil
- 1 bunch fresh mint (for pork wraps)
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro (for black bean tacos & pork wraps)
- 1 fresh avocado (for pork wraps)
- 1 fresh mango (for pork wraps)
- 2 fresh limes
- 1/2 head fresh red cabbage, shredded (for black bean tacos)
- 1/4 red onion, diced (for Greek Chicken topping)
- 1 tomato, cored and diced (for Greek Chicken topping)
- 1 cup cucumber, diced (for Greek Chicken topping)
- 2 fresh lemons (for Greek Chicken marinade)

**Meat, Seafood, & Dairy:**
- 2 chicken breasts (for Greek Chicken)
- 1 lb ground pork (for pork wraps)
- 1 lb large shrimp (peeled, deveined, tails removed)
- 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese (for ziti)
- 1 wedge or container Parmesan cheese
- 1 cup Feta cheese (for pork wraps garnish)
- Sour cream and/or Mexican Crema (for black bean tacos)
- Butter
- Mayonnaise and Greek yogurt (for Greek Chicken mayo-yogurt sauce)

**Pantry & Spices:**
- 12 oz Ziti pasta (or Penne)
- 10 small flour or corn tortillas (for black bean tacos)
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes (for ziti)
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans
- 1 cup corn kernels (canned, fresh, or frozen)
- Fish sauce or anchovy paste (for Greek Chicken marinade)
- Soy sauce
- Dijon mustard (for Greek Chicken)
- Dry sherry (or Shaoxing wine) (for shrimp)
- Sherry vinegar (for pork wraps)
- Sriracha sauce
- Olive oil & vegetable oil
- Cornstarch & sugar
- Dried thyme & dried rosemary (for Greek Chicken)
- Spices: Red pepper flakes, ground cumin, garlic powder, salt, black pepper
- Naan bread or pita (for Greek Chicken)
- Guacamole and hot sauce (optional, for serving)
