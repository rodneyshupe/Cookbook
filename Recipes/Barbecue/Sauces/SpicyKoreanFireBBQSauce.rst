Spicy Korean Fire BBQ Sauce
===========================

+----------------------+----------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Yield: about 1/2 cup |
+----------------------+----------------------+

A gochujang-based fire sauce in the style of Korean buldak ("fire
chicken"), rounded out with honey and lime. The seasoning packet from a
pack of spicy instant ramen (such as Buldak) stands in for the "fire"
heat; leave it out for a milder sauce.

Ingredients
-----------

- 3 tablespoons gochujang (Korean chili paste)
- 2 tablespoons soy paste (or 2 tablespoons soy sauce)
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon lime juice (from about 1/2 lime)
- 1 teaspoon spicy "fire" sauce or seasoning (from a spicy ramen packet),
  or to taste

Directions
----------

1. Whisk the gochujang, soy paste, honey, garlic, and lime juice together
   in a small bowl until smooth.
2. Whisk in the ramen "fire" sauce a little at a time, tasting as you go,
   until the heat and saltiness are balanced to your liking. Thin with a
   teaspoon or two of water if the sauce is too thick to brush.
3. Use right away as a glaze or dipping sauce, or refrigerate in an
   airtight container for up to 1 week.

Notes
-----

Reasonable starting quantities were built for this improvised sauce
around standard gochujang-BBQ-sauce ratios; adjust the honey for sweetness
and the ramen fire sauce for heat.
