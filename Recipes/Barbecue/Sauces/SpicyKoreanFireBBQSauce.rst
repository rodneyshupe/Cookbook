Spicy Korean Fire BBQ Sauce
===========================

+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes (est.) | Yield: about 1/2 cup (est.) |
+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- gochujang - korean chili paste
- soy paste
- garlic
- lime
- spicy 'fire' sauce (from ramen packet)
- honey
