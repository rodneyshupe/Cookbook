Louisiana (Cajun or Creole) Seasoning Spice Mix
===============================================

+----------------------+----------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Yield: 1/4 cup |
+----------------------+----------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 5 teaspoons paprika
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 teaspoon celery salt
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon pepper
