Basic Pork Rub
==============

+----------------------+--------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Yield: about 1 cup |
+----------------------+--------------------+

This is a very simple basic rub for pork.

Ingredients
-----------

-  1/4 cup brown sugar
-  1/4 cup paprika
-  2 tablespoons black pepper
-  2 tablespoons salt
-  1 tablespoon cayenne
-  2 teaspoons dry mustard
