Quick Tartar Sauce
==================

+----------------------+------------------------+--------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Total Time: 15 minutes | Yield: about 1 cup |
+----------------------+------------------------+--------------------+

Source: `Cook's Illustrated <https://www.cooksillustrated.com/recipes/11526-quick-tartar-sauce>`__

Ingredients
-----------

- 3/4 cup mayonnaise
- 3 tablespoons minced cornichons plus 1 tablespoon brine
- 1 scallion, minced
- 1 small shallot, minced
- 1 tablespoon capers, rinsed and minced
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper

Directions
----------

Combine all ingredients in small bowl. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate
until flavours meld, about 15 minutes.
