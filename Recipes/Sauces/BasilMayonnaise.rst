Basil Mayonnaise
================

+----------------------+--------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Yield: about 1 cup |
+----------------------+--------------------+

Ingredients
-----------
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves
- 1 tablespoon water
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice

Directions
----------
- Combine all ingredients in small bowl.
