Tartar Sauce with Dill
======================

+----------------------+------------------------+----------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Total Time: 15 minutes | Yield: about 3/4 cup |
+----------------------+------------------------+----------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup plain yogurt
- 1 tablespoon capers, chopped
- 2 tablespoons minced cornichons
- 1 1/2 teaspoons lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon fresh dill, chopped

Directions
----------

1. Combine all ingredients in a small bowl. Cover with plastic wrap and
   refrigerate until the flavours meld, about 15 minutes.
