Gochujang Chicken Wing Sauce
============================

+----------------------+----------------------+----------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Cook Time: 2 minutes | Yield: about 1/3 cup |
+----------------------+----------------------+----------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 tablespoon butter
- 2 1/2 teaspoons gochujang
- 2 1/2 teaspoons honey
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon mirin
- 1 tablespoon vinegar

Directions
----------

1. Combine all the ingredients in a small sauce pot and cook down until
   thickened, 1-2 minutes.
2. Let it cool, then pour into a bottle to save for your chicken wings.
