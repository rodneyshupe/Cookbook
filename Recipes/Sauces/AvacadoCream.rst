Avocado Cream
=============

Ingredients
-----------
- 1 avocado
- 1/4 cup greek yogurt
- 2 tablespoons milk 
- juice from half a lime.
- 1-2 tablespoons cilantro chopped
- salt and pepper

Directions
----------
- Remove flesh from avocado and roughly chop. Add to a small blender along
  with other ingredients and blend until smooth.
