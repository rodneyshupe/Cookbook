Buffalo Sauce
=============

+----------------------+----------------------+-------------------------------------+
| Prep Time: 2 minutes | Cook Time: 5 minutes | Yield: enough for 2 pounds of wings |
+----------------------+----------------------+-------------------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 1/2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 1/2 tablespoons Frank's Hot Sauce (you can use Tabasco or Sriracha sauce as well)

Directions
----------

1. Combine the butter and hot sauce in a small pot over low heat.
