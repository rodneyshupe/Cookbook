Argentinian Chimichurri Sauce
=============================

+------------------------+-----------------------------------------------+
| Total Time: 10 minutes | Yield: Serves 4 to 6 (Makes about 1 1/2 cups) |
+------------------------+-----------------------------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1/4 cup hot water
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/3 cups fresh parsley leaves
- 2/3 cup fresh cilantro leaves
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
- 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil

Directions
----------

1. Combine hot water, oregano, and salt in a small bowl and let stand until
   the oregano softens, about 5 minutes. Pulse the parsley, cilantro, garlic,
   and pepper flakes in a food processor until coarsely chopped, about 10
   pulses.
2. Add the water mixture and vinegar and pulse briefly to combine. Transfer to
   a medium bowl and slowly whisk in the oil until incorporated and
   emulsified.
3. Cover with plastic wrap and let stand at room temperature at least 1 hour.
   Sauce can be refrigerated up to 2 days; bring to room temperature and
   rewhisk before serving.

Notes
-----

To keep the sauce from becoming bitter, whisk in the olive oil by hand.
