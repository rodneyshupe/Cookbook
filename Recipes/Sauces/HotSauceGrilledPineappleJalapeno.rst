Grilled Pineapple and Jalapeno Hot Sauce
========================================

+----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Total Time: 5 minutes | Yield: 1 3/4 cups |
+----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 cup grilled pineapple, roughly chopped
- 1/2 cup chopped jalapenos
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1/2 tablespoon salt
- 1/2 tablespoon ground pepper
- 1 cup white vinegar
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 cup sweet onions, roughly chopped

Directions
----------

1. Combine all the ingredients in a blender and puree until a smooth sauce
   forms.
