Simple Alfredo Cream Sauce
==========================

+----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
- Freshly ground pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 tablespoons parmesan cheese, grated

Directions
----------

1. Add butter to a hot pan. Once melted, add the garlic, pepper, and nutmeg.
2. Once the garlic begins to brown, add the cream and cheese, then bring to a
   simmer and shake the pan.
3. Add pasta of choice to the pan and coat thoroughly.

Variations
----------
* Mushroom Alfredo: Add 2 ounces chopped cremini or button mushrooms in step 1
  before garlic.
* Carbonara: Add 4 ounces ham or bacon in step 1. Optionally finish with
  1/8 cup peas right before adding in pasta.
* Dill and Smoked Salmon: Add 1 tablespoon finely chopped fresh dill in step 2.
  Then add 4 ounces chopped smoked salmon at the end of step 2. You can also use
  uncooked salmon; in that case add partway through step 2.
* Cajun Seafood: Replace nutmeg with cajun spice and prior to step one sear sea
  scallops and then add them back in at the end of step 2. Season with
  hot-sauce to add additional spice.
