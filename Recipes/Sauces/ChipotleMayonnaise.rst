Chipotle Mayonnaise
===================

+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes (est.) | Yield: about 1/2 cup (est.) |
+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon chipotle powder
- 1 - 2 tablespoon lime juice

Directions
----------
- Combine all ingredients in small bowl.
