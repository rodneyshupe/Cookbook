Jalapeno Hot Sauce
==================

+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Total Time: 45 minutes | Yield: 1 1/2 cups |
+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1/2 teaspoon vegetable oil (if canning do not use and use a bit of water)
- 10 jalapenos, chopped
- 1/4 cup onions, minced
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup water
- 1/4 cup white vinegar

Directions
----------

1. Saute the oil, jalapeno peppers, onions, garlic, and salt in a sauce pan
   over high heat for 4 minutes. Pour in the water and cook, stirring often,
   for 20 minutes.
2. Take the pan off the heat and let the mixture cool to room temperature.
3. Puree the mixture in a food processor until completely smooth. Keep the
   processor running and add the distilled white vinegar until completely
   mixed.
4. Place into a jar or bottle.
