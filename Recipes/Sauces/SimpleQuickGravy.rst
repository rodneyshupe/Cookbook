Simple Quick Gravy
==================

+----------------------+------------------------+---------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Total Time: 15 minutes | Yield: 2 cups |
+----------------------+------------------------+---------------+

Quick beef broth brown gravy that can be made in just 15 minutes with only
a few ingredients.

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 1/3 cups beef broth (reduced sodium)
- 2/3 cup chicken broth (reduced sodium)
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/4 teaspoon ground thyme
- 2-3 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1/4 cup cold water
- 2-3 tablespoons cornstarch
- salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon cold unsalted butter (optional)

Directions
----------

1. Bring the broth to a boil in a medium sauce pan over medium-high heat, then
   stir in the onion powder, garlic powder, thyme, and Worcestershire sauce.
2. Whisk together the cold water and cornstarch in a small bowl until
   dissolved, whisk into the boiling beef broth, reduce heat to medium-low,
   and simmer until the gravy is thickened to your liking.
3. Season with salt and pepper, then taste and add more salt and pepper if
   needed, tasting again to make sure it's just right.
4. Remove from heat and optionally whisk in the butter. Serve immediately or
   cool and refrigerate for up to 5 days.

Variations
----------

* ROUX - If you want to use a roux, start out with 2 tablespoons unsalted
  butter in the saucepan, and heat until melted. Whisk in 2 tablespoons all
  purpose flour and cook about 1 minute. Slowly add in the broths, whisking
  the mixture as you pour, until the mixture is smooth. Proceed with the
  recipe as written, omitting the water and cornstarch.
* DRIPPINGS - Substitute them for some of the broth.
* BROTH - for a beefier flavour, try using all beef broth (2 cups). For a
  lighter flavour, and colour, increase the amount of chicken broth and
  reduce the amount of beef. Just keep it at 2 cups total.
* ONION - Finely mince some onion and saute it in the saucepan with a drizzle
  of oil over MED heat for about 5 minutes. Then add the broth and continue
  with the recipe as written.
* FRESH HERBS - try adding a sprig or two of fresh thyme or rosemary to the
  saucepan with the broth. It adds a beautiful herby flavour! Just remember
  to remove the woody stems before serving.
* MUSHROOMS - Saute sliced mushrooms in the saucepan with a drizzle of oil
  over MED heat for about 5 minutes. Then add the broth and continue with the
  recipe as written.
