Pulpo a la Gallega
==================

+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 1 1/4 hours | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1.5 pounds (680 g) octopus, frozen and defrosted
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 2-3 tablespoons high-quality olive oil
- Spanish paprika, to taste
- Salt, to taste
- 1 pound yukon gold potatoes, peeled, boiled, and sliced

Directions
----------

1. Fill a large stock pot halfway with water and bring to a boil, then add
   the bay leaf and salt; once the water is boiling, rapidly dip the
   defrosted octopus three times into it.
2. Submerge the octopus fully and cook on a low boil for 60 minutes, then
   pull the pot off the heat and let the octopus cool.
3. Slice the tentacles and serve over the sliced potatoes with high-quality
   olive oil and a sprinkle of paprika and salt.
