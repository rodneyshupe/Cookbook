Chorizo, Manchego and Olive Skewers
===================================

+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Yield: 12 skewers |
+-----------------------+-------------------+

Source: `Paige Rhodes <https://www.mymoderncookery.com/chorizo-manchego-olive-skewers/>`__

Ingredients
-----------

- Spanish Chorizo
- Manchego Cheese
- Manzanilla Olives
- Honey
- Slivered Almonds

Directions
----------

1. Thread each skewer with cheese on the bottom, olive in the middle, and chorizo on top.
2. Drizzle with honey and sprinkle with slivered almonds.
