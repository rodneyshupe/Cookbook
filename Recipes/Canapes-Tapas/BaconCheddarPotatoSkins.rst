Bacon-Cheddar Potato Skins
==========================

+-------------------------+------------------------------+
| Total Time: 1 1/4 hours | Yield: Makes 16 potato skins |
+-------------------------+------------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 4 russet potato, scrubbed
- 1/2 teaspoon table salt, plus extra
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper, plus extra
- 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack cheese
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 6 slices bacon, chopped

Directions
----------

1. MICROWAVE POTATOES Set the oven rack to the upper-middle position and heat
   the oven to 475F, then slide a rimmed baking sheet inside to preheat. Prick
   the potatoes all over with a fork, set on a paper towel, and microwave until
   tender, 10 to 15 minutes, turning them over after 5 minutes.
2. FRY BACON Cook the bacon in a large skillet over medium heat until crisp,
   about 8 minutes. Reserve 2 teaspoons bacon fat, then move the bacon to a
   paper towel-lined plate and blot with paper towels to draw off excess grease.
3. CRISP SKINS Quarter the potatoes lengthwise and let cool 5 minutes, then
   scoop out most of the flesh (save it for another use), leaving a 1/4-inch
   layer. Brush the outsides with the reserved bacon fat and season with salt
   and pepper. Set the potatoes skin-side down on the preheated sheet and bake
   until golden brown and crisp, 15 to 20 minutes.
4. FILL SKINS Combine the cheeses, cornstarch, half the bacon, 1/2 teaspoon
   salt, and 1/4 teaspoon pepper in a bowl. Pull the shells from the oven and
   top with the cheese mixture. Return to the oven and bake until the cheese
   melts, 2 to 4 minutes. Move the skins to a paper towel-lined plate, sprinkle
   with the remaining bacon, and serve.

Notes
-----

The potatoes can be cooked, scooped, and refrigerated in an airtight
container for 2 days before proceeding with the recipe. Serve with sour
cream and sliced scallions, if you like.
