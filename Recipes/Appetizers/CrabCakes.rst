Crab Cakes with Fresh Herb Provencale and Roasted Pepper Coulis
===============================================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Total Time: 50 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 12 ounces crab meat
- 3 egg, lightly beaten
- 4 1/4 ounces bread crumbs
- 5 teaspoons basil, fresh, chopped
- 5 teaspoons oregano, fresh, chopped
- 5 teaspoons sage, fresh, chopped
- 5 tablespoons spanish onion, diced
- 5 teaspoons sweet chilli sauce
- 5 teaspoons mayonnaise
- kosher salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- 1/2 cup  extra virgin olive oil
- 1/4 cup  balsamic vinegar
- 2/3 cups red pepper, roasted
- 1/4 cup  butter
- arugula
- assorted crisp greens, (mesculin mix)
- lime juice, fresh
- chives, (garnish)

Directions
----------

1. Combine crab meat, eggs, bread crumbs, fresh herbs, finely diced spanish
   onion, sweet chilli sauce, mayonnaise, salt, and pepper in a bowl and mix
   well. With wet hands, form the crab cakes and set them on a plate; cover
   and refrigerate 30 minutes. Meanwhile, make a simple vinaigrette with 2
   parts olive oil to 1 part vinegar; season and set aside.
2. Drain the canned roasted peppers and puree until smooth, about 30 seconds;
   season and set aside.
3. Preheat a skillet, add the butter, and let it melt. Add the crab cakes and
   cook until golden brown on each side, about 2 to 3 minutes per side.
   Meanwhile, toss the arugula and crisp greens in a bowl with a drizzle of
   vinaigrette. Spread the greens on a platter and arrange the crab cakes
   around them; spoon a portion of red pepper coulis onto each cake.
4. Squeeze fresh lime juice over the top and garnish with fresh chives.
