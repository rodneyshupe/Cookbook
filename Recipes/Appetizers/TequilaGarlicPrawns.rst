Tequila Garlic Prawns
=====================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+----------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Yield: 8-10 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+----------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 - 2 pounds (450 g - 900 g) raw prawns peeled and deveined
- 2 tablespoons + 1 teaspoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon, fresh oregano
- salt and pepper to taste
- 1/2 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons tequila

Directions
----------

1. In a large bowl, mix the prawns with 2 tablespoons of olive oil, the
   oregano, and salt and pepper; let sit for 10 minutes.
2. Add olive oil and butter to a frying pan over medium heat; once the butter
   is melted, add the garlic and saute until fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes.
3. Add the prawns and cook until pink, 1 to 2 minutes per side.
4. Add the tequila and flambe.
