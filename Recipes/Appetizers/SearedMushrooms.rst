Seared Mushrooms with Egg Yolk
==============================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 3 tablespoons olive oil plus more for sage
- 1/4 cup (loosely packed) fresh sage leaves
- 2 pounds (900g) mix of wild mushrooms (such as chanterelle, cremini,
   portobello, shiitake, enoki, oyster, hen of the woods, and porcini),
   cleaned, stems trimmed
- 2 tablespoons Sherry
- 1/2 tablespoon Sherry Vinegar
- 4 baguette slices, toasted (french or sourdough)
- 4 eggs

Directions
----------

1. Pour oil into a small skillet to a depth of 1/8" and heat over medium heat;
   add sage and cook, turning often, until leaves just start to crisp, about
   30 seconds (do not brown), then transfer sage to paper towels to drain.
   Strain oil through a fine-mesh sieve into a small bowl and set sage oil
   aside.
2. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a medium to large skillet over medium heat;
   add mushrooms and season with salt and pepper, then sear until they start
   to release their liquid. Add Sherry and reduce, 30 to 60 seconds, then add
   Vinegar and deglaze the pan.
3. Transfer mushrooms to a large platter with toast, add reserved sage leaves,
   and drizzle with some sage oil; season to taste with salt and pepper.
4. Pour water into a small pot to a depth of 2 inches and bring to a simmer.
   Separate eggs, reserving whites for another use and keeping whole yolks in
   shells; working with 2 yolks at a time, gently slip yolks from shells into
   simmering water and poach until outside is set but inside is still runny,
   about 30 seconds. Using a slotted spoon, place yolks over mushrooms,
   spacing apart. Serve immediately.
