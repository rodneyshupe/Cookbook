Snails in Red Wine and Mushrooms
================================

+-----------------------+--------------------------+----------------------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cooking Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 4-6 servings as appetizer |
|                       |                          |        2 servings as entree      |
+-----------------------+--------------------------+----------------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 5 ounces crimini mushrooms, sliced thin
- Salt and pepper
- 1 shallot, chopped fine
- 1/2 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cups low-sodium beef broth
- 1/2 cup red wine
- 1 (125 g) cans of Snails, drained and rinsed
- 1/4 cup cream
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 tablespoon flat leaf parsley, chopped

Directions
----------

1. Heat 1 tablespoons oil in skillet until shimmering. Cook mushrooms and 1/4
   teaspoon salt until mushrooms release their liquid, about 2 minutes; add
   shallot and cook until the liquid has evaporated, about 3 minutes.
2. Stir in tomato paste, then flour; cook for 30 seconds.
3. Gradually stir in broth, then wine. Bring to simmer and reduce over low
   heat, 8 to 10 minutes.
4. Add snails and simmer for 5 minutes. Add cream and, off heat, whisk in
   butter. Season to taste, top with parsley, and serve with baguette slices.
