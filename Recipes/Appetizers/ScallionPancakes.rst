Scallion Pancakes
=================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 30 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Total Time: 1 hour | Yield: 2 pancakes |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 1/2 cups (7 1/2 ounces) plus 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup boiling water
- 7 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 4 medium scallions, sliced thin
- `Dipping Sauce <#asian-dipping-sauce>`__

Directions
----------

1. Mix 1 1/2 cups flour and boiling water in a bowl with a wooden spoon to
   form a rough dough. When cool enough to handle, transfer to a lightly
   floured counter and knead until a tacky (but not sticky) ball forms, about
   4 minutes; the dough will not be perfectly smooth. Cover loosely with
   plastic wrap and let rest for 30 minutes.
2. While the dough rests, stir together 1 tablespoon vegetable oil, the sesame
   oil, and the remaining 1 tablespoon flour; set aside.
3. Set a 10-inch cast-iron skillet over low heat to preheat. Divide the dough
   in half, cover 1 half with plastic wrap, and set aside. Roll the remaining
   dough into a 12-inch round on a lightly floured counter, drizzle with 1
   tablespoon oil-flour mixture, and brush evenly over the entire surface.
   Sprinkle with 1/2 teaspoon salt and half of the scallions, then roll the
   dough into a cylinder, coil the cylinder into a spiral, tuck the end
   underneath, and flatten the spiral with your palm. Cover with plastic and
   repeat with the remaining dough, oil-flour mixture, salt, and scallions.
4. Roll the first spiral into a 9-inch round and cut a 1/2-inch slit in the
   center; cover with plastic, then roll and cut a slit in the second pancake.
   Put 2 tablespoons vegetable oil in the skillet and increase the heat to
   medium-low. Add 1 pancake (the oil should sizzle), cover, and cook, shaking
   the skillet occasionally, until the pancake is slightly puffy and golden
   brown on the underside, 1 to 1 1/2 minutes. If the underside is not browned
   after 1 minute, turn the heat up slightly; if it is browning too quickly,
   turn the heat down slightly. Drizzle 1 tablespoon vegetable oil over the
   pancake and brush over the entire surface, then carefully flip. Cover and
   cook, shaking the skillet occasionally, until the second side is golden
   brown, 1 to 1 1/2 minutes. Uncover and continue to cook until the bottom is
   deep golden brown and crispy, 30 to 60 seconds longer, then flip and cook
   until deep golden brown and crispy, 30 to 60 seconds. Transfer to a wire
   rack and repeat with the remaining 3 tablespoons vegetable oil and the
   remaining pancake. Cut each pancake into 8 wedges and serve, passing `dipping sauce <#asian-dipping-sauce>`__
   separately.

Make Ahead
----------

Stack uncooked pancakes between layers of parchment paper, wrap tightly
in plastic wrap, and refrigerate for up to 24 hours or freeze for up to
1 month. If frozen, thaw pancakes in single layer for 15 minutes before
cooking.
