Ginger Prawns with Sweet Chilli Sauce and Shredded Daikon
=========================================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Yield: 2 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 2 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon ginger, chopped
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- 1/2 cup celery, sliced
- 1 tablespoon walnut oil
- 1/2 cup daikon, shredded
- 2 tablespoon sweet chilli sauce
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 3/4 pound (340 g) prawns, deveined
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1/4 cup white wine
- 2 tablespoon flat leaf parsley, chopped
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil

Directions
----------

1. Heat the olive oil in a wok or saute pan over medium-high heat. Add the
   garlic and ginger; stir fry until fragrant, 30 seconds, and season.
2. Add the celery, walnut oil, daikon, sweet chilli sauce, and soy sauce;
   season and saute for 2 minutes. Add the prawns and saute until pink, 2 to 3
   minutes, then add the lime juice and white wine and reduce for 1 to 2
   minutes.
3. Correct the seasoning if required, add the parsley, and saute for 30
   seconds.
