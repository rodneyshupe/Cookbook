Prawns Hara (Green Prawns)
==========================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 4 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 4 clove garlic, minced
- 3 tablespoon ginger, chopped
- 1 teaspoon cumin powder
- 3 tablespoon green chili, chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- 16 large prawns, (peeled)
- 1/2 spinach, chopped
- 2 tablespoon cilantro, fresh, chopped
- 3 tablespoon mint, fresh, chopped
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- 1 teaspoon Garam masala

Directions
----------

1. Heat the olive oil in a skillet; add the onions, season, and sear for 2
   minutes. Add garlic, ginger, cumin, green chili, and turmeric powder; cook
   for 1 minute.
2. Add the prawns, season, and cook for approx. 4-5 minutes. Add the spinach,
   cilantro, and mint, season, and cook for an additional 2-3 minutes.
3. Before serving, add garam masala.
