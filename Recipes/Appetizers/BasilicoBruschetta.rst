Basilico Bruschetta
===================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 baguette, sliced in half
- 1 red pepper
- 1 yellow pepper
- 4 ounces Butter, softened
- 1 bunch basil, chopped
- fresh cracked pepper
- 2 clove garlic
- 1 can artichokes, drained & quartered
- 2 chorizo sausage, sliced
- kosher salt
- 12 slices provolone cheese

Directions
----------

1. Set the oven to broil. Put the peppers on a flat baking sheet and into the
   oven, turning them for an even roast until the outer skin is burnt. Bag
   them in a brown paper bag and let sit for 5 minutes, then peel off the
   outer skin, cut in half, and remove the seeds. Finely julienne and set
   aside.
2. Make the basilico butter: in a bowl blend the butter, pepper, and chopped
   basil until well combined.
3. Rub garlic cloves onto the baguettes, then spread the butter compound on
   each. Toast under the broiler until lightly golden, then layer the peppers
   evenly across.
4. Add artichokes, chorizo, and season, then top with provolone cheese. Broil
   until the cheese has melted and is lightly golden. Cut into wedges.
   Serves 4. If desired, drizzle a good balsamic vinegar over top.
