Poached Figs served with Greek Manouri Cheese
=============================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 45 minutes | Total Time: 1 3/4 hours | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------
- 12 ounces Dried greek figs
- 2/3 cup mavrodaphne wine
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 2-3 whole cloves
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 1-2 pounds manouri cheese
- mint, fresh for garnish

Directions
----------

1. Place figs in saucepan, cover with hot water, and steep for 1 hour.
2. Pour in the wine and spices; bring to a boil, then turn down to a simmer
   for 30 minutes, until figs are plump and tender.
3. Remove the fruit and strain the poaching liquid; return liquid to stove,
   add sugar, and boil until syrup consistency.
4. Slice cheese into 1/2 inch slices and lay in a row on a platter; top with
   figs, pour syrup over, and garnish with fresh mint. Serve.
