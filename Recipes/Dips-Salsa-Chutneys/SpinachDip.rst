Spinach Dip
===========

+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Yield: 6-8 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------
- 1 cup Mayonnaise
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 pkg knorr Vegetable Soup Mix
- 1/4 cup red onion
- 1/3 cup cheddar cheese, finely grated
- 1/4 cup water chestnuts, chopped (optional)
- 1 pkg frozen diced spinach leaves
- Round loaf of bread (Sourdough, Pumpernickel, etc)

Directions
----------
1. Boil the spinach leaves, then pat dry; combine the mayonnaise and sour
   cream in a bowl, then fold in the remaining dried items until evenly
   mixed.
2. Hollow out the loaf, leaving 1/2 inch around the edge and bottom, and
   reserve the removed bread; dice the bread into bite-size pieces, then
   fill the hollowed loaf with the mixture and serve with the bread pieces.
