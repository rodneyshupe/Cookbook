Guacamole
=========

+----------------------+-----------------------+---------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Yield: 2 cups |
+----------------------+-----------------------+---------------+

I got this recipe from my friend Scott. According to him: *This recipe
is the best guacamole ever.*

Ingredients
-----------

- 3 medium avocados, ripe
- 2 tablespoons minced onion
- 1 medium clove garlic, minced
- 1 small jalapeno chili, minced (1 to 1 1/2 teaspoons)
- 1/4 cup minced fresh cilantro leaves
- 1/4 teaspoon table salt
- 1/2 - 3/4 teaspoon ground cumin
- 2 tablespoons lime juice

Directions
----------

1. Halve one avocado, remove the pit, and scoop the flesh into a medium
   bowl. Mash lightly with the onion, garlic, jalapeno, cilantro, salt,
   and cumin (if using), using the tines of a fork until just combined.
   *To minimize discolouration, mince the ingredients first so they are
   ready to mix in the moment the avocados are cut.*
2. Halve and pit the remaining two avocados. Scoop them gently into the
   bowl with the mashed mixture.
3. Sprinkle the lime juice over the avocado and mix everything lightly
   with the fork until combined but still chunky. Adjust the seasoning
   with salt if needed, then serve.

Notes
-----

Can be covered with plastic wrap pressed directly onto the surface and
refrigerated up to one day. Return the guacamole to room temperature,
removing the wrap at the last moment, before serving.
