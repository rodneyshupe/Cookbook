Chutney
=======

+-----------------------+--------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Yield: about 1 cup |
+-----------------------+--------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 cup cilantro, leaves
- 2 tablespoon Ginger
- 2 green chili
- 2 tablespoon lemon juice, fresh
- Sea salt

Directions
----------

1. Grind the cilantro leaves, ginger, and green chili together into a paste.
2. If the paste is too thick, add a little water.
3. Stir in the lemon juice and salt and mix well.
