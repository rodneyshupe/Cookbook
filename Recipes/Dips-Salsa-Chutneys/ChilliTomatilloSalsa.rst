Chilli Tomatillo Salsa
======================

+------------------------+------------------------------------------+
| Total Time: 25 minutes | Yield: Serves 6 (Makes about 1 1/2 cups) |
+------------------------+------------------------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 2 Dried Kashmiri red chillies
- 2 Dried Bedgi red chillies
- 1/2 pound tomatillos, washed
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tablespoon pepitas or pumpkin seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 2 tablespoons Vinegar
- Salt

Directions
----------

1. Heat the oil in a wide frying pan or cast iron skillet. Add the dried
   chillies and toast until aromatic and red, expanded. Bash the garlic,
   then add it to the pan with the cumin seeds, pumpkin seeds, and finally
   the tomatillos. Cook the tomatillos until squishy soft and starting to
   fall apart when you stir them around. Tip everything into a blender and
   whizz with 2 tablespoons vinegar and salt. Taste and adjust the salt to
   your liking.

Notes
-----

To make this salsa spicier, reserve and add the jalapeno seeds to the
blender before processing.
