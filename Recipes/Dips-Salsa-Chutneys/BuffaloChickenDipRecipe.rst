Buffalo Chicken Dip Recipe
==========================

+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Total Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+

Here's what you need: shredded chicken, cream cheese, ranch dressing,
buffalo sauce, cheddar cheese, scallion, chip

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 cup shredded chicken, leftover
- 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
- 1/2 cup buffalo sauce
- 1/2 cup ranch dressing
- cheddar cheese
- scallion, to serve
- chip, to serve

Directions
----------

1. Preheat the oven to 350F (180C), then mix the chicken, cream cheese,
   and buffalo sauce in a bowl; pour into a cast iron skillet and smooth
   with a spoon.
2. Cover with cheddar cheese and bake until the cheese is melted and
   bubbly, about 20 minutes; top with scallions and serve with your
   favorite chips.
