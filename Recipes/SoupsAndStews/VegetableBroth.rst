Vegetable Broth
===============

+-----------------------+------------------------------+---------------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 1 hour 40 minutes | Yield: about 5 cups broth |
+-----------------------+------------------------------+---------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- 1 large carrot, chopped
- 1 celery rib, chopped
- 2 quarts water
- 5-6 sprigs fresh thyme, tied into a bundle

Directions
----------

1. Heat oil in a large sauce pan or stock pot over medium high heat. Add
   onion, carrot and celery and saute until onions start to brown.
2. Add water, thyme (or other herb) bundle and bring to a boil. Reduce heat
   and simmer for approx. 90 minutes and reduce to 5 cups.
