Ham Hock Vegetable Soup
=======================

+-----------------------+-------------------------------+----------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 2 hours 40 minutes | Yield: 8-10 servings |
+-----------------------+-------------------------------+----------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 pounds (900 g) smoked pork ham hocks
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 5 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 large carrots, peeled and diced
- 4 stalks celery, diced
- 8 ounces frozen corn
- 8 ounces frozen green beans
- 8 ounces green peas
- 1 (15 ounce / 425 ml) can black beans, drained and rinsed (you can use any bean you choose)
- 1 (13.5 fluid ounces / 400 ml) can diced tomatoes
- 1 teaspoon dried chili flakes (optional)
- 2 quarts chicken stock or broth

Directions
----------

1. In a large pot over medium-high heat, add the oil and cook the onions,
   celery, carrots, and garlic for 5 minutes, stirring continuously.
2. Stir in all other vegetables and the diced tomatoes, then heat and stir for
   5 minutes.
3. Add the pork hocks along with all other ingredients and reduce the
   temperature to a simmer.
4. Simmer the soup for 2 to 2 1/2 hours. Add more water occasionally as the
   soup reduces.
5. When the pork hocks are tender, remove them from the pot, take the lean
   meat off the bones, and shred it. Discard the fat and bones.
6. Add the shredded lean meat back to the soup and adjust the seasoning to
   taste.
