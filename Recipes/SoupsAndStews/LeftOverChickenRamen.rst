Left Over Chicken Ramen
=======================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 6 cups chicken stock
- 3 cups water
- 1 1/2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1 1/2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 4 inch piece of ginger, crushed
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped fine
- 1 1/2 teaspoons five spice
- Left over roast chicken, shredded
- 1 large carrot, cut into match sticks
- 3 green onions, cut thin diagonally
- 1/2 cup cilantro, roughly chopped
- 10 baby spinach leaves
- dried seaweed
- sriracha, to taste

Directions
----------

1. Bring the chicken stock, water, Worcestershire sauce, soy sauce, ginger,
   garlic, and five-spice powder to a boil in a Dutch oven. Reduce the heat to
   medium-low and simmer until the flavours meld, about 10-15 minutes.
2. While the broth simmers, soak the seaweed in 2 cups water for 10 minutes.
   Once rehydrated, squeeze out the water and slice.
3. Cook the noodles and set aside.
4. Build the ramen bowl: start with some chicken, then add layers of carrots,
   green onion, cilantro, baby spinach, and seaweed. Add the noodles last.
5. Remove the ginger from the broth and ladle the broth over the bowl
   contents. Add some more carrots, green onion, and cilantro.
