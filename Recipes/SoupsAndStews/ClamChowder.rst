New England Clam Chowder
========================

+---------------------+
| Yield: 3-4 servings |
+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 8 pounds cherrystone clams, scrubbed
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 4 ounces bacon, cut into 1/2-inch pieces
- 1 celery stalk, minced
- 1/2 large onion, minced
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 1/4 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled, cut into 1/2-inch pieces
- 1/2 tablespoon chopped fresh thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1 cup heavy cream
- Kosher salt, freshly ground pepper
- Chopped fresh chives
- Oyster crackers or Vermont Common Crackers

Directions
----------

1. Bring the clams and 2 cups water to a boil in a large pot over high heat.
   Cook until the clams just open, 8-10 minutes; discard any that do not open.
   Using a large slotted spoon, transfer the clams to a large rimmed baking
   sheet and set the broth aside. Let the clams cool slightly, then pull the
   meat from the shells and discard the shells.
2. Chop the clams into bite-size pieces. Strain the broth through a fine-mesh
   sieve set over a large bowl. Add water if needed to measure 3 cups. DO
   AHEAD: The clams and broth can be made 1 day ahead; cover separately and
   chill.
3. Melt the butter in a large heavy pot over medium heat. Add the bacon and
   cook, stirring occasionally, until the fat is rendered and the bacon begins
   to brown, about 8 minutes. Add the celery, onion, and garlic and cook,
   stirring often, until the onion is translucent, about 10 minutes. Add the
   reserved broth (or 3 cups bottled clam juice), potatoes, thyme, and bay
   leaf. Bring the chowder base to a simmer and cook until the potatoes are
   tender, 20-25 minutes. Stir the cornstarch and 1 tablespoons water in a
   small bowl to form a slurry. Stir the slurry into the chowder base and
   return to a boil to thicken.
4. Remove the base from the heat. Discard the bay leaf. Stir in the reserved
   clams (or two 10-ounce cans baby clams) and the cream. Season with salt, if
   needed (clams' brininess varies), and pepper.
5. Divide the chowder among bowls. Garnish with chives and oyster crackers. DO
   AHEAD: Base can be made 1 day ahead. Let cool; cover and chill. Keep clams
   chilled. Bring base to a simmer before continuing.
