Butternut Squash Soup
=====================

+-----------------------+------------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 1 1/4 hours | Yield: 6-8 servings |
+-----------------------+------------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 1/2 pounds butternut squash, peeled, seeded, and cut into 2-inch
  chunks (about 7 cups)
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 leek, white and green parts only, quartered lengthwise, sliced
  thin, and washed thoroughly (about 1 1/2 cups)
- Salt and pepper
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 - 2 cups water
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- pinch cayenne pepper
- Sour cream

Directions
----------

1. Put squash in a bowl, cover, and microwave until a paring knife glides
   easily through the flesh, 14 to 18 minutes, stirring halfway through.
   Carefully move squash to a colander set in a bowl (it will be very hot) and
   drain 5 minutes; reserve the liquid.
2. Melt butter in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add squash, leek, and 1
   teaspoon salt; cook, stirring now and then, until the pieces start to break
   down and a brown fond forms on the bottom, 10 to 13 minutes.
3. Add 2 cups broth and scrape the bottom to loosen and dissolve the fond. Add
   remaining 2 cups broth, reserved squash liquid, 1 cup water, thyme sprigs,
   bay leaf, and cayenne. Crank heat to high and bring to a simmer, then drop
   to medium and simmer until the leeks are fully tender, 6 to 7 minutes.
4. Fish out and discard the bay leaf and thyme sprigs. Working in batches,
   blend the soup until smooth, 1 to 2 minutes. Return it to the clean pot and
   bring to a simmer, thinning with up to 1 cup water to the consistency you
   want. Season with salt and pepper to taste; serve with a dollop of sour
   cream. (Soup keeps up to 2 days in advance.)
