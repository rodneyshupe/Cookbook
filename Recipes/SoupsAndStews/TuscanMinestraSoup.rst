Tuscan Minestra Soup
====================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Yield: 2 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 1/2 cups chicken stock, or vegetable stock
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- 2 tablespoons zucchini, diced
- 2 tablespoons onion, diced
- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 1 clove garlic, chopped
- 1 tablespoon oregano, fresh, chopped
- 1 tablespoon basil, fresh, chopped
- 1 tablespoon parmesan cheese, freshly grated
- 1 plum tomato, chopped
- 1 carrot, 1/4 inch dice
- 3 tablespoons Romano beans
- 1 tablespoon red pepper, diced
- 1 tablespoon flat leaf parsley, chopped
- 1 tablespoon celery, diced
- 2 tablespoons pasta of choice, pre-cooked
- 2 tablespoons tomato puree

Directions
----------

1. Cook the pasta and set it aside. In a soup pot, heat the olive oil.
2. Add the onions, season, and sear for 2 minutes. Add the garlic and sear for
   1 minute. Add the celery, carrots, and red peppers and sear for 1 minute,
   then season. Add the tomatoes, stock, and beans. Bring to a boil, then turn
   down to a simmer. Add the zucchini, pasta, fresh herbs, and parsley.
   Correct the seasoning and simmer for 1-2 minutes.
3. Place in soup bowls and grate fresh parmesan over the top.
