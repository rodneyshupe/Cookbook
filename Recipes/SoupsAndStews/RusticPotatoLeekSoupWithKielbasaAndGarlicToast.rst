Rustic Potato-Leek Soup with Kielbasa and Garlic Toast
======================================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 45 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 (12-inch) baguette, sliced 1 inch thick on bias
- 1 garlic clove, peeled
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 8 ounces kielbasa sausage, halved lengthwise and sliced 1/2 inch
  thick
- 2 pounds leeks
- 5 cups chicken broth
- 1/2 pound red potatoes, unpeeled, cut into 3/4-inch pieces
- Salt and pepper

Directions
----------

1. Prep the leeks. Trim and discard the root and dark leaves, then slice the
   trimmed leek in half lengthwise. Slice the halves into 1 inch pieces as
   directed. Rinse the cut leeks thoroughly to remove dirt and sand.
2. Adjust the oven rack to the middle position and heat the oven to 400F.
   Arrange the bread in a single layer in a rimmed baking sheet and bake until
   dry and crisp, about 10 minutes, turning the slices over halfway through
   baking. While still hot, rub each slice with garlic and drizzle with oil,
   then set aside.
3. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a Dutch oven over medium heat. Cook the
   kielbasa until lightly browned, 2 to 3 minutes. Stir in the leeks, cover,
   and cook, stirring occasionally, until the leeks are tender but not mushy,
   10 to 15 minutes.
4. Stir in the broth, scraping up any browned bits. Stir in the potatoes and
   bring to a simmer. Cover, reduce the heat to medium-low, and simmer gently
   until the potatoes are almost tender, 5 to 7 minutes.
5. Off heat, let sit until the potatoes are tender and the flavours meld, 10
   to 15 minutes. Season with salt and pepper to taste and serve with the
   garlic toast.
