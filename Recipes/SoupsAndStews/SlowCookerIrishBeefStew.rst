Slow Cooker Irish Beef Stew
===========================

+-----------------------+--------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 7 hours | Yield: 4-6 servings |
+-----------------------+--------------------+---------------------+

Equipment
---------

Slow Cooker

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1.5 pounds (680 g) beef stew meat in 1-2 inch cubes
- 2 small-medium onions, peeled and cut into slim wedges
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 pound waxy potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
- 4 medium carrots, peeled and cut into chunks
- 3 sticks celery, sliced
- 4 cups beef broth (see notes if you want to use beer)
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce (can use soy sauce in place)
- 4 tablespoons white flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- ground black pepper to taste
- 1 sprig thyme OR 1/4 teaspoon dried
- 1 sprig rosemary OR 1/4 teaspoon dried
- 1 bay leaf (skip if you don't have)
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch, stirred into 3 tablespoons cool water to make a
  slurry (use more cornstarch for an extra-thick gravy, but don't overdo it
  - it will thicken more as it cools slightly)

Directions
----------

1. Brown meat: Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the
   beef and brown it, then remove it and add it to a 4-6 quart slow cooker.
2. Saute onions: Add the onions to the hot skillet and saute over medium heat
   until softened. Pour in about 1/2 cup of the beef broth, scraping any
   browned bits off the bottom of the pan (see notes for using dark beer in
   this step). Take off the heat and carefully pour into the slow cooker over
   the meat.
3. Cook stew: Place the potatoes, carrots and celery in the slow cooker. Whisk
   together the broth, Worcestershire sauce and flour in a measuring jug. Pour
   over the meat and vegetables in the slow cooker, then carefully stir. Tuck
   in the herbs, close the lid and cook on LOW for 7-8 hours or on HIGH for 4
   hours.
4. Thicken: Open the slow cooker and carefully stir. Pour in the cornstarch
   slurry, stirring to combine well. Simmer WITHOUT the lid for 15-30 minutes
   on HIGH, until thickened.
