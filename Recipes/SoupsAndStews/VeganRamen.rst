Vegan Ramen
===========

+-------------------+
| Yield: 4 servings |
+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

Broth
^^^^^

- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 4 scallions
- 1 2" piece ginger, peeled, thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 5 cups `Vegetable Broth <#vegetable-broth>`__
- 5 cups water
- 8 dried shiitake mushrooms
- 1 4x3" piece dried kombu (if not using kombu, replace with an additional 1/2 tablespoon miso)
- 1 tablespoon miso
- 1 tablespoon low-sodium soy sauce

Ramen
^^^^^

- 4 baby bok choy (about 12 ounces total), quartered lengthwise
- 4 5-ounce packages fresh ramen noodles
- toasted nori sheets, and/or cilantro (for serving; optional)
- 1 cup corn kernels
- `Garlic Chilli Oil <#garlic-chili-oil>`__ or Sriracha to taste

Directions
----------

1. Trim the dark green parts from the scallions and thinly slice them; set
   aside for serving. Coarsely chop the white and pale green parts. Heat the
   remaining 2 tablespoons oil in the reserved pot over medium-high. Cook the
   chopped scallions and ginger, stirring often, until the scallions are
   charred in spots, about 4 minutes. Add the tomato paste and cook, stirring
   occasionally, until it begins to stick to the bottom of the pot and darkens
   slightly, about 2 minutes. Add the mushrooms and kombu, then stir in 5 cups
   cold water. Bring to a boil, then remove from the heat and let sit until
   the mushrooms soften, about 10 minutes. Remove and discard the kombu if
   using.
2. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the solids to a blender. Add a ladleful or
   2 of broth to the blender and puree until smooth. Stir the puree back into
   the broth in the pot and bring to a simmer over medium heat. Stir in the
   soy sauce; season with salt. Reduce the heat to low and keep warm until
   ready to serve.
3. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add the bok choy and cook
   until bright green and tender, about 2 minutes. Using a slotted spoon,
   transfer the bok choy to a plate. Return the water to a boil and cook the
   noodles according to the package directions. Drain and divide among bowls.
4. To serve, ladle the broth over the noodles, then top with the bok choy and
   reserved garlic oil. Top with nori, cilantro, and corn if desired.
