Turkey Noodle Soup
==================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+----------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 8-10 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+----------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 8-10 cups `Turkey Stock <#basic-turkey-stock>`__
- 1 medium onion, diced medium
- 2 medium carrots, peeled and cut into 1/4-inch-thick rounds
- 1 large rib celery, sliced 1/4 inch thick
- 1 tablespoon minced fresh thyme leaves
- salt and pepper
- 2-3 cups pasta shells (medium), or other medium-sized pasta shape
- 2 tablespoons minced fresh flat leaf parsley leaves

Directions
----------

1. Bring the turkey stock to a simmer in a large stockpot or Dutch oven over
   medium-high heat. Add the onion, carrot, celery, thyme, and 1 teaspoon
   salt; cover and simmer until the vegetables are just tender, about 10
   minutes. Add the pasta and reserved shredded turkey meat from stock; simmer
   until the pasta is al dente, 10 to 12 minutes. Stir in the parsley, adjust
   the seasonings with salt and pepper, and serve.
