Spicy Chicken Breast, Chilli, Chard and Egg Noodle Soup
=======================================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Yield: 2-4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 7 ounces chicken breast, sliced
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 medium red chili, chopped
- 6 cups chicken stock
- 5 1/4 ounces swiss chard, chopped
- 9 ounces egg noodles
- 1 ounce parmesan cheese, grated

Directions
----------

1. Cook the egg noodles per the package directions, drain, and set aside
2. Heat the oil and saute the onions, then season
3. Add the chicken breast, season, and saute for 3 minutes
4. Add the chillies and saute, then add the stock, bring to a boil, and reduce
   to a simmer for 10 minutes
5. Add the swiss chard and egg noodles to the soup
6. Adjust the seasoning if needed and add freshly grated parmesan
