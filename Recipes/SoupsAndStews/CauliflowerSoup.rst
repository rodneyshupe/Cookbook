Cauliflower Soup
================

+-----------------------+------------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 1 1/2 hours | Yield: 4-6 servings |
+-----------------------+------------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 head cauliflower (2 pounds)
- 8 tablespoons unsalted butter, cut into 8 pieces
- 1 leek, white and light green parts only, halved lengthwise, sliced
  thin, and washed thoroughly
- 1 small onion, halved and sliced thin
- Salt and pepper
- 4 1/2 - 5 cups water
- 1/2 teaspoon sherry vinegar (or white wine vinegar)
- 3 tablespoons minced fresh chives

Directions
----------

1. Pull off outer leaves of cauliflower and trim stem. Cut around core with
   paring knife to remove; slice core thin and reserve. Cut heaping 1 cup of
   1/2-inch florets from head; set aside. Cut remaining cauliflower crosswise
   into 1/2-inch thick slices.
2. Melt 3 tablespoons butter in large saucepan over medium-low heat. Add leek,
   onion, and 1 1/2 teaspoons salt; cook, stirring frequently, until softened
   but not browned, about 7 minutes.
3. Increase heat to medium-high; add 4 1/2 cups water, sliced core, and half
   of sliced cauliflower; bring to simmer. Reduce heat to medium-low and
   simmer gently 15 minutes. Add remaining sliced cauliflower; return to
   simmer and cook until tender and crumbling easily, 15 to 20 minutes longer.
4. Meanwhile, melt remaining 5 tablespoons butter in 8-inch skillet over
   medium heat. Add reserved florets and cook, stirring frequently, until
   golden brown and butter is browned and nutty, 6 to 8 minutes. Pull skillet
   from heat; transfer florets to small bowl with slotted spoon. Toss florets
   with vinegar and season with salt to taste. Pour browned butter into small
   bowl and reserve for garnish.
5. Process soup in blender until smooth, about 45 seconds. Rinse out pan.
   Return pureed soup to pan and bring back to simmer over medium heat,
   adjusting consistency with remaining water as needed (soup should be thick
   and velvety but thin enough to settle flat after stirring) and seasoning
   with salt to taste. Serve, garnishing bowls with browned florets, a drizzle
   of browned butter, and chives, then season with pepper to taste.
