Turkey Orzo Soup with Kale and Chickpeas
========================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Yield: 6 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 tablespoons turkey fat
- 1 onion, chopped fine
- 1 pinch red pepper flakes
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon ground coriander
- 8 cups `Turkey Stock <#simple-turkey-stock>`__
- 3 ounces curly kale, stemmed and cut into 1/2-inch pieces (6 cups)
- 1 (15-ounce / 425 ml) can chickpeas, rinsed
- 1/2 cup orzo
- 2 cups shredded turkey
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- Salt and pepper

Directions
----------

1. Heat the fat in a Dutch oven over medium heat until shimmering. Add the
   onion and pepper flakes and cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion is
   softened and translucent, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic, cumin, and
   coriander and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute. Add the stock, increase
   the heat to high, and bring to a simmer. Stir in the kale, chickpeas, and
   orzo, reduce the heat to medium-low, and simmer, partially covered, for 10
   minutes.
2. Add the turkey and cook until the orzo and kale are tender, about 2
   minutes. Off heat, stir in the lemon juice and season with salt and pepper
   to taste. Serve.
