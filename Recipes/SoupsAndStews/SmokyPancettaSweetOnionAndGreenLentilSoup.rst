Smoky Pancetta, Sweet Onion, And Green Lentil Soup
==================================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 25 minutes | Yield: 2-4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- 1 onion, chopped
- 6 cups chicken stock
- 10 1/2 ounces green lentils, canned
- 9 ounces pancetta, diced
- 2 tablespoons flat leaf parsley, chopped

Directions
----------

1. Heat 3 tablespoons olive oil in a large saucepan, add the onions, saute,
   and season.
2. Add the stock and bring to a boil, add the lentils, then turn down to a
   simmer and season.
3. In a separate saucepan, fry the pancetta until crispy and add it to the
   soup. Garnish with parsley.
