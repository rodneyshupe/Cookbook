Chicken and Black Bean Soup
===========================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 4-6 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 small onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 jalapeno pepper, seeded and minced (optional)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (13.5 fluid ounces / 400 ml) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (15 ounces / 425 ml) black beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup frozen corn kernels
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 1 tablespoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cups shredded chicken
- Sour cream, cheese, and cilantro for garnish (optional)

Directions
----------

1. Heat a large pot over medium heat; add a splash of oil or a pat of butter.
2. Add the onion, bell pepper, jalapeno (if using), and garlic and saute until
   the vegetables are softened, 5 minutes.
3. Add the diced tomatoes, black beans, corn, chicken broth, chili powder,
   cumin, oregano, paprika, and a pinch of salt and pepper; bring to a simmer.
4. Reduce the heat to low and simmer until the vegetables are tender, 20
   minutes.
5. Stir in the shredded chicken and cook until heated through, 5 minutes.
6. Taste and adjust the seasoning as needed.
7. Ladle into bowls and garnish with a dollop of sour cream, a sprinkle of
   cheese, and some chopped cilantro, if desired.
