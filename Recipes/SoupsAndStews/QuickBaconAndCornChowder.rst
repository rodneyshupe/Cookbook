Quick Bacon and Corn Chowder
============================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 8 cups frozen corn kernels (about 2 pounds), thawed (see note)
- 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 6 slices bacon, chopped fine
- 1 onion, chopped fine
- 2 pounds russet potatoes, peeled and cut into 1/2-inch chunks
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1 teaspoon minced fresh thyme
- 1/8 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- Salt and pepper

Directions
----------

1. Puree 4 cups corn and 2 cups broth in a blender or food processor until
   smooth. Cook the bacon in a large pot over medium-high heat until crisp,
   about 5 minutes, then transfer to a paper towel-lined plate. Reduce the
   heat to medium and cook the onion and potatoes in the bacon fat until the
   onion is softened, about 5 minutes.
2. Whisk in the pureed corn mixture, cream, thyme, cayenne, and remaining
   broth, and simmer until the potatoes are tender, about 15 minutes. Stir in
   the remaining corn and cook until the corn is heated through, about 2
   minutes. Season with salt and pepper. Sprinkle with the crisp bacon and
   serve.

Notes
-----

Frozen corn can be quickly defrosted in a bowl in the microwave. If
using fresh corn, you'll need about 1 dozen medium ears; use a chef's
knife to cut the kernels off the husked and silked ears.
