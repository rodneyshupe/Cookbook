Instant Pot Chicken and Dumpling Soup
=====================================

+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Total Time: 40 minutes | Yield: 6 servings |
+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+

Equipment
---------

Electric Pressure Cooker

Ingredients
-----------

The Chicken
^^^^^^^^^^^

- 4 tablespoons of butter
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 large carrots, peeled and diced
- 3 stalks of celery, sliced into 1/4" pieces (with leafy tops reserved)
- 1 tablespoon of crushed garlic
- 6 cups of reduced-sodium chicken broth (I used 2 tablespoons of Chicken Better Than Bouillon + 6 cups of water)
- Whole chicken (3-4 pounds / 1.35 kg - 1.8 kg), chopped into pieces with skin on
- 3 bay leaves
- 1/2 cup of Sherry wine (if you leave it out, add in another 1/2 cup of broth)
- 1/2 tablespoon of seasoned salt
- 1/2 tablespoon of poultry seasoning (salt-free)
- 1/2 tablespoon of black pepper
- 1/2 tablespoon of rubbed sage
- 1 teaspoon of dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon of dried parsley
- 3 tablespoons of cornstarch + 3 tablespoons of water to form a slurry
- 1/2 cup of heavy cream

The Dumplings
^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- 1 1/4 cups of all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons of baking powder (NOT to be confused with baking soda!)
- 1 teaspoon of salt
- 2 tablespoons (1/4 stick) of salted butter
- 1/2 cup of whole milk

Directions
----------

1. Hit "Saute" and set it to "More" or "High". Add the butter and once melted,
   add the onion, carrots and celery; saute for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and
   saute for 1 minute longer.
2. Pour in the broth and stir well, then add the chicken and make sure it's
   all covered. Toss in the bay leaves and secure the lid.
3. Hit "Keep Warm/Cancel", then hit "Manual" or "Pressure Cook" on High
   Pressure for 10 minutes.
4. When the chicken is done, do a quick release. Discard the bay leaves and
   pull the chicken out with tongs; set it aside in a bowl to cool.
5. Add the sherry, seasoned salt, poultry seasoning, pepper, rubbed sage,
   thyme, parsley and the reserved leafy celery tops; stir well and let the
   pot rest for now.
6. While the chicken cools, make the dumplings. Whisk the flour, baking powder
   and salt together in a mixing bowl. Put the butter and milk in a pyrex and
   microwave for 40 seconds, then pour it into the flour and lightly mix with
   a fork until totally blended into a warm dough. (NOTE: Don't overdo the
   mixing as it can make for a tough dumpling.) Let your hands take over and
   lightly knead the dough. On a clean floured surface, roll it with a
   flour-coated rolling pin to 1/8" thick max (it's very important it's no
   thicker than 1/8" so it cooks properly). Using a pizza cutter or knife,
   slice the dough vertically into strips about 1" wide, then horizontally
   about 2" wide, forming little rectangular strips. Dust with more flour so
   they don't stick.
7. Give the pot heat again by hitting "Saute" set to "More" or "High". Once it
   bubbles, stir in the cornstarch slurry, then add the dumpling strips
   one-by-one and give them a good stir so none stick together. Let them
   simmer for 10 minutes with the glass lid on.
8. After 10 minutes of the dumplings boiling, hit "Keep Warm/Cancel" so the
   bubbles die down.
9. The chicken should now be cool enough to handle. Pick the bones clean,
   discarding the bones, skin and cartilage. Rip the chicken by hand into
   robust pieces and add to the pot.
10. Add the heavy cream and give everything a final stir until well combined.
