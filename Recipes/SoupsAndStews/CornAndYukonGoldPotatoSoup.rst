Corn and Yukon Gold Potato Soup
===============================

+-----------------------+-------------------+-----------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 1 hour | Yield: 4 - 6 servings |
+-----------------------+-------------------+-----------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1/4 large yellow onion, chopped (about 1/3 cup)
- 1/4 cup small diced red bell pepper
- 1/4 cup small diced celery
- 2 1/2 cups corn nibbles
- 1 bay leaf
- 5 cups vegetable stock
- 1 medium Yukon Gold potato, peeled and 1-inch diced
- 1 1/2 teaspoons Kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground white pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
- 1 1/2 cups heavy whipping cream
- 2 threads of saffron
- 3 ounces butter

Directions
----------

1. Place the butter, chopped onions, red bell pepper, celery, salt and pepper
   in a pot and cook at medium-low until the vegetables soften. Add the
   potatoes and saute for 10 more minutes.
2. Add the vegetable stock, saffron and bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then reduce
   the heat to a bare simmer. Cover the pot and cook for 20 minutes. Keep the
   heat as low as it can go while still maintaining a gentle simmer.
3. After 20 minutes, add the cream and thyme, and check the seasoning.
   Increase the heat to return the soup to a simmer, then lower the heat to
   maintain the simmer and cook for another 10 minutes.
4. Discard the bay leaf. Blend the corn chowder in a high speed blender until
   smooth. Pour into a heated bowl and garnish with chive sour cream and
   smoked sunflower oil.
