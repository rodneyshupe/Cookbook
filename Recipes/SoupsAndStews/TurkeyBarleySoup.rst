Turkey Barley Soup
==================

+-----------------------+-------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 1 hour | Yield: 6 servings |
+-----------------------+-------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 tablespoons turkey fat
- 1 onion, chopped fine
- 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 pinch red pepper flakes
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 8 cups `Turkey Stock <#simple-turkey-stock>`__
- 3/4 cup pearled barley
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 celery ribs, cut into 1/4-inch pieces
- 2 carrots, peeled and cut into 1/4-inch pieces
- 2 cups shredded turkey
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- Salt and pepper

Directions
----------

1. Heat the fat in a Dutch oven over medium heat until shimmering. Add the
   onion, thyme, and pepper flakes and cook, stirring occasionally, until the
   onion is softened and translucent, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cook
   until fragrant, about 1 minute. Add the stock, barley, and bay leaf,
   increase the heat to high, and bring to a simmer. Reduce the heat to
   medium-low and simmer, partially covered, for 15 minutes.
2. Add the celery and carrots and simmer, partially covered, until the
   vegetables start to soften, about 15 minutes.
3. Add the turkey and cook until the barley and vegetables are tender, about
   10 minutes. Off heat, stir in the lemon juice and season with salt and
   pepper to taste. Serve.
