Spiced Apples On A Bed Of Vodka Infused Wild Berries
====================================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Total Time: 1 hour | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 300 ml water
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 1/4 cup berry sugar
- 5 teaspoons marsala wine
- 4 whole cloves
- 2 ml ground nutmeg
- 1 lemon, rind
- 1 vanilla bean, split
- Seasonal berries, washed patted dry
- 1/2 cup vodka
- milk chocolate
- Vanilla cream
- 4 apple, cored, sliced
- several sprig mint, fresh
- whipping cream

Directions
----------

1. In a sauce pan add water, cinnamon, sugar, marsala, cloves, nutmeg, lemon
   rind, and vanilla bean; bring to a boil, cut heat, and put aside, let cool.
2. Meanwhile marinate fruit with vodka, toss gently, refrigerate for 1 hour.
3. Using a double boiler melt chocolate and blend into vanilla cream.
4. Place apple on plate, layer choc., vanilla cream over and place fruit over
   cream, garnish with mint leaf and a dollop of whipped cream.
