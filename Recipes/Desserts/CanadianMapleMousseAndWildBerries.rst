Canadian Maple Mousse and Wild Berries
======================================

+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Yield: 8 servings |
+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 cups whipping cream
- 5 teaspoons icing sugar
- 1/2 cup maple syrup
- 1 cup vanilla cream
- 1 cup fresh berries
- Chocolate baskets
- mint, for garnish
- 1 vanilla bean

Directions
----------

1. Whip whipping cream to a stiff peak with the icing sugar
2. Fold maple syrup into the vanilla cream, then fold in the whipped cream and
   the fresh berries
3. Pipe into the chocolate baskets and garnish with fruit and mint leaf
