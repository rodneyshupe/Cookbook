Lemon Curd
==========

+----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Yield: about 2 cups |
+----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 3 large eggs
- 1 cup sugar
- Zest from 2 lemons
- 3/4 cup lemon juice (juice from the two lemons + Santa Cruz Organic
  Lemon Juice)
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter

Directions
----------

1. Beat eggs and add to heavy pot, then add the rest of the ingredients to the
   pot.
2. Whisk over medium to medium high heat for 20 minutes, careful not to let
   the mixture boil.

Make Ahead
----------

May be stored in the refrigerator in a sealed container for 1-2 weeks.
