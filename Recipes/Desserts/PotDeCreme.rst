Pot de Creme
============

+-----------------------+-------------------+-------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 1 hour | Total Time: 1 1/2 hours | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-------------------+-------------------------+-------------------+

Equipment
---------
Sous-Vide (immersion circulator) set to 175F

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 1/4 cups heavy whipping cream
- seeds from 1/4 vanilla bean (you can substitute 1/2 teaspoon pure vanilla
  extract or 1/2 teaspoon ground vanilla)
- 8 egg yolk
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- Maple syrup for drizzle

Directions
----------

1. Heat cream, vanilla seeds and pod over medium heat to 158F / 70 C.
2. Whisk yolks and sugar until smooth, then slowly pour cream into the egg
   mixture, tempering the eggs; strain to remove any cooked egg and the
   vanilla pod, then rest at room temp 20 minutes so any bubbles can rise to
   the top.
3. Heat sous vide water bath to 175F (80 C).
4. Skim away any remaining bubbles, divide the mixture between 4 sealable 4
   ounce jars, then place jars into the sous vide bath and cook for one hour.
5. Remove the jars and place in an ice bath to chill.
6. Serve with a drizzle of maple syrup at the table.
