"Fried" Ice Cream
=================

+----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Total Time: 45 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 pint ice cream (such as Breyers Delights Vanilla Cupcake Ice Cream)
- 1 1/2 tablespoons butter
- 1 1/4 cups corn cereal, crushed
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 2 teaspoons granulated sugar
- Whipped cream, for garnish
- Sprinkles, for garnish
- 4 maraschino cherries, for garnish

Directions
----------

1. Line a small sheet tray with parchment paper and place in freezer, then
   scoop ice cream into 4 balls and set on the prepared tray. Keep in freezer
   while making the cereal mixture, at least 30 minutes.
2. Melt butter in a medium skillet over medium heat; add corn cereal and
   cinnamon and cook, stirring occasionally, until the cereal turns golden, 5
   to 7 minutes. Remove from heat and stir in sugar, then spread on a shallow
   bowl and let cool.
3. Remove ice cream balls from freezer and roll in cereal mixture. Top with
   whipped cream, sprinkles, and a cherry; serve immediately.
