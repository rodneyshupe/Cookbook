Caramel and Fresh Mango Mousse
==============================

+-----------------------+----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 30 minutes | Cook Time: 5 minutes | Total Time: 45 minutes | Yield: 8 servings |
+-----------------------+----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

Caramel sauce
^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- 1 recipe pastry cream
- 2 cups whipping cream
- 3 tablespoon icing sugar
- 4 mango, peeled, diced in small pieces
- 5 1/4 ounces butter, (unsalted)
- 1 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 1/3 cup golden syrup
- 3/4 cup heavy cream
- Chocolate baskets

Directions
----------

1. Prepare pastry cream as per recipe; whip cream and icing sugar together to
   stiff peaks, then fold in whipped cream gently.
2. Fold mango into finished pastry cream, then cover and refrigerate.
3. Meanwhile make the caramel sauce: add all the ingredients to a pot and stir
   over low-medium heat until the sugar dissolves; simmer 3 minutes, then
   chill in fridge 30 minutes and fold into mousse.
4. Pipe into chocolate baskets with a round piping tube large enough not to
   catch the pieces of mango.
5. Garnish with fresh mint leaf.
