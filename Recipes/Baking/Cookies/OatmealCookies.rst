Oatmeal Cookies
===============

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Yield: 20 cookies |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 cup (5 ounces) all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 3/4 cup (5 1/4 ounces) dark brown sugar
- 1/2 cup (3 1/2 ounces) granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup Vegetable oil
- 1 large egg plus 1 large yolk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3 cups (9 ounces) old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1/2 cup raisins (optional)

Directions
----------

1. Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 375F. Line 2 rimmed
   baking sheets with parchment paper; whisk flour, salt, and baking soda
   together in medium bowl and set aside.
2. Melt butter in 8-inch skillet over medium-high heat, swirling pan
   occasionally, until foaming subsides. Keep cooking, stirring and scraping
   the bottom of the pan with a heat-resistant spatula, until milk solids are
   dark golden brown and butter smells nutty, 1 to 2 minutes; immediately
   transfer the browned butter to a large heatproof bowl, scraping the
   skillet, then stir in cinnamon.
3. Add brown sugar, granulated sugar, and oil to the bowl with the butter and
   whisk until combined; add egg, yolk, and vanilla and whisk until smooth.
   Using a wooden spoon or spatula, stir in the flour mixture until fully
   combined, about 1 minute, then add oats and raisins, if using, and stir
   until evenly distributed (mixture will be stiff).
4. Divide dough into 20 portions, each about 3 tablespoons (or use a #24
   cookie scoop); arrange dough balls 2 inches apart on the prepared sheets,
   10 per sheet, and press each ball into a 2 1/2-inch disk with a damp hand.
5. Bake, 1 sheet at a time, rotating halfway through, until edges are set and
   lightly browned and centers are still soft but not wet, 8 to 10 minutes.
   Let cookies cool on the sheet on a wire rack for 5 minutes, then transfer
   with a wide metal spatula to the wire rack and let cool completely.
