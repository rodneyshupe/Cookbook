Vegan Brownies
==============

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 45 minutes | Yield: 8 brownies |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 1/2 cups black beans (if using canned beans rinse well)
- 1/2 cup of quick oats, ground
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
- 1/2 chocolate chips
- salt
- 1/3 cup maple syrup
- 1/4 cup coconut oil
- 2 tablespoons of vanilla extract

Directions
----------

1. Preheat oven to 350F and line a loaf pan with parchment paper.
2. Place all ingredients into a food processor and puree.
3. Bake until a toothpick inserted comes out dry, 30 minutes to 1 hour; let
   cool and serve.
