Peanut Butter Icebox Cookies
============================

+-----------------------+----------------------+-------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 8 minutes | Total Time: 3 1/2 hours | Yield: 24 cookies |
+-----------------------+----------------------+-------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- 3 tablespoon margarine, softened
- 2 tablespoon peanut butter
- 1/2 cup brown sugar, firmly packed
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1 large egg white

Directions
----------

1. Combine flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl; stir well and set aside.
2. Combine margarine and peanut butter in a large bowl; beat at medium speed
   of a mixer until light and fluffy, then gradually add sugars and beat at
   medium speed until well blended.
3. Add vanilla and egg white; beat well, then stir in flour mixture.
4. Turn dough out onto wax paper and shape into a 6" log; wrap log in wax
   paper and freeze 3 hours.
5. Preheat oven to 350F.
6. Cut log into 24 (1/4" thick) slices; place slices 1" apart on a baking
   sheet coated with cooking spray, then bake at 350F for 8 minutes.
7. Remove cookies from pan and let cool on wire rack.
