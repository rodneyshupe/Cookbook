Six Minute Brownies
===================

+-----------------------+----------------------+--------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 6 minutes | Yield: 16 brownies |
+-----------------------+----------------------+--------------------+

These brownies are best served warm!

Ingredients
-----------

- 1/3 cup of cocoa
- 3/4 cup of sugar
- 1/2 cup of flour
- 1 teaspoon of baking powder
- 2 eggs
- 2/3 cup of melted butter
- 1 teaspoon of vanilla
- 1 cup of chocolate chips
- 1 cup of chopped nuts (optional)
- Vanilla ice cream

Directions
----------

1. Combine the cocoa, sugar, flour, and baking powder in one of the mixing
   bowls.
2. In the other mixing bowl, beat the 2 eggs; then add the melted butter and
   whisk until light and foamy, and stir in the vanilla once the butter and
   eggs are mixed well together.
3. Add the wet ingredients into the dry, and mix until they reach the
   consistency of a batter; once the bowl is rid of dry ingredients, fold in
   the chocolate chips and chopped nuts (optional) and mix again until spread
   throughout the batter.
4. Spray the 9x9 glass pan with cooking spray, add the brownie batter, and
   smooth out with the spatula.
5. Cook the brownies in the microwave for 6 minutes on high.
6. Remove from the microwave, cut into the desired amount of pieces, and serve
   with a generous amount of vanilla ice cream.
