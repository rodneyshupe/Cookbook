Simple Crusty Bread
===================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 30 minutes | Total Time: 3 1/2 hours | Yield: 3-4 loaves |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 1/2 tablespoons yeast
- 1 1/2 tablespoons kosher salt
- 6 1/2 cups unbleached, all-purpose flour, more for dusting dough
- Cornmeal

Directions
----------

1. In a large bowl or plastic container, mix yeast and salt into 3 cups
   lukewarm water (about 100F); stir in flour until no dry patches remain.
   Dough will be quite loose. Cover, but not with an airtight lid, and let
   rise at room temperature 2 hours (or up to 5 hours).
2. Bake now or refrigerate, covered, for as long as two weeks. When ready,
   sprinkle a little flour on the dough and cut off a grapefruit-size piece
   with a serrated knife; turn it in your hands to lightly stretch the
   surface, creating a rounded top and a lumpy bottom. Set on a pizza peel
   sprinkled with cornmeal and let rest 40 minutes, then repeat with the
   remaining dough or refrigerate it.
3. Place a broiler pan on the bottom of the oven and a baking stone on the
   middle rack; turn oven to 450F and heat the stone at that temperature for
   20 minutes.
4. Dust the dough with flour and slash the top three times with a serrated or
   very sharp knife, then slide it onto the stone. Pour one cup hot water into
   the broiler pan and shut the oven quickly to trap the steam. Bake until
   well browned, about 30 minutes. Cool completely.

Variation
---------

If not using a stone, stretch the rounded dough into an oval and place in a
greased, nonstick loaf pan. Let rest 40 minutes if fresh, an extra hour if
refrigerated. Heat oven to 450F for 5 minutes, then place the pan on the
middle rack.
