Almost No-Knead Sourdough Bread
===============================

+-----------------------+-------------------+----------------------+---------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 1 hour | Total Time: 16 hours | Yield: 1 loaf |
+-----------------------+-------------------+----------------------+---------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 3 2/3 cups (18 1/3 ounces) all-purpose flour or bread flour
- 1 3/4 teaspoons salt
- 1 1/2 cups plus 4 teaspoons (12 2/3 ounces) water, room temperature
- 1/3 cup (3 ounces) mature `Sourdough Starter <#sourdough-starter>`__

Directions
----------

1. Whisk flour and salt in a medium bowl. In a large bowl, whisk the
   room-temperature water and starter until smooth; add the flour mixture and
   stir with a wooden spoon, scraping up dry flour from the bottom, until the
   dough comes together, then knead by hand in the bowl until a shaggy ball
   forms and no dry flour remains. Cover with plastic wrap and let sit at room
   temperature at least 12 hours or up to 18 hours.
2. Lay a 12 by 12-inch sheet of parchment on the counter and spray generously
   with vegetable oil spray. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured counter and
   knead 10 to 15 times, then shape into a ball by pulling the edges into the
   middle. Set the dough seam side down in the center of the parchment, lift
   by the parchment edges, and lower into a heavy-bottomed Dutch oven; cover
   with plastic wrap.
3. Adjust the oven rack to the middle position and set a loaf or cake pan on
   the bottom of the oven. Place the pot on the middle rack and pour 3 cups
   boiling water into the pan below, then close the door and let the dough
   rise until doubled and it does not readily spring back when poked with a
   floured finger, 2 to 3 hours.
4. Remove the pot and water pan; discard the plastic from the pot. Lightly
   flour the top and, using a razor blade or sharp knife, make one
   7-inch-long, 1/2-inch-deep slit along the top. Cover the pot and set on the
   middle rack. Heat the oven to 425F and bake 30 minutes, starting the timing
   as soon as you turn on the oven.
5. Remove the lid and continue baking until the loaf is deep brown and
   registers 210F, 20 to 30 minutes longer. Carefully remove the bread from
   the pot, transfer to a wire rack, and let cool completely before serving.
