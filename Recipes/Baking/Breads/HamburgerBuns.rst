Hamburger Buns
==============

+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+----------------+
| Prep Time: 30 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Total Time: 3 hours | Yield: 12 buns |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+----------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 463g All Purpose Flour
- 14g Yeast
- 220g Lukewarm Water
- 2 Large Eggs, one beaten
- 31g Sugar
- 9g Salt
- 41g Melted Butter
- Sesame Seeds
- 30g Nido (Milk Powder)

Directions
----------

1. Whisk flour and yeast in the bowl of a stand mixer, then add
   room-temperature water and 2 eggs. Fit the mixer with the dough hook and
   mix on low until the dough comes together and no dry flour remains, about 2
   minutes, scraping down the bowl and hook frequently. Turn off the mixer,
   cover with a dish towel or plastic wrap, and let stand 15 minutes.
2. Add sugar and salt and knead on medium-low until incorporated, about 30
   seconds. Increase to medium and, with the mixer running, add the butter 1
   piece at a time, letting each piece incorporate before the next, about 3
   minutes total, scraping down the bowl and hook as needed. Keep kneading on
   medium until the dough is elastic and pulls cleanly from the sides of the
   bowl, about 10 minutes longer; then transfer to a greased large bowl, cover
   tightly with plastic, and let rise at room temperature until doubled, about
   1 hour.
3. Line 2 rimmed baking sheets with parchment. Turn the dough onto the counter
   and divide into twelve 3-ounce portions, dividing any remaining dough
   evenly among them. Working with 1 portion at a time, cup it under your palm
   and roll against the counter into a smooth, tight ball. Space 6 balls on
   each sheet, then press with the greased bottom of a dry measuring cup to
   3-inch diameter, about 3/4 inch thick, poking any air bubbles with the tip
   of a paring knife.
4. Cover loosely with plastic and let rise at room temperature until doubled,
   about 1 hour. Adjust the oven racks to the upper-middle and lower-middle
   positions and heat the oven to 350F.
5. Discard the plastic and brush the tops and sides with beaten egg (you need
   not use all of it), then sprinkle with sesame seeds, if using.
6. Bake until the buns are deep golden brown and register 205 to 210F in the
   center, 18 to 20 minutes, switching and rotating the sheets halfway
   through. Transfer to wire racks and let cool completely, about 30 minutes;
   serve. (Buns keep in zipper-lock bags at room temperature for up to 2 days
   or frozen for up to 1 month.) TO MAKE AHEAD: Before the second rise, cover
   with plastic wrap and refrigerate for up to 24 hours. Let the dough sit at
   room temperature for 2 hours before baking, increasing the baking time by 5
   minutes.
