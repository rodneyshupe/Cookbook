60-Minute Bread
===============

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------------+---------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 40 minutes | Total Time: 1 1/2 hours | Yield: 1 loaf |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------------+---------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 3 cups all-purpose flour, plus more as needed
- 2 teaspoons instant yeast, such as SAF
- 2 teaspoons salt

Directions
----------

1. Combine the flour, yeast, and salt in a bowl or food processor; add 1 1/4
   cups warm water all at once and stir with a wooden spoon, or run the
   machine. Keep mixing a minute or two longer by hand, about 30 seconds total
   in the food processor, adding water by the tablespoon if needed, until a
   ball forms.
2. Shape into a flat round or long loaf, using only enough flour to handle the
   dough; set it on a baking sheet or a well-floured pizza peel. Cover and let
   rise in the warmest spot in your kitchen while the oven preheats to 425F.
   (If you have time, let it rise for an hour or so.)
3. Bake on the sheet, or slide onto a baking stone, until the crust is
   golden-brown, crisp, and firm, 30 to 45 minutes.
