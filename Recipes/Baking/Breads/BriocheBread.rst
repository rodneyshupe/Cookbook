Brioche Bread
=============

+-----------------------+-----------------------+----------------------+----------------------------------------+
| Prep Time: 30 minutes | Cook Time: 35 minutes | Total Time: 14 hours | Yield: 2 medium loaves or 1 large loaf |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+----------------------+----------------------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

The sponge
^^^^^^^^^^

- 1/3 cup whole milk, warm (100F)
- 1 1/8 teaspoon active dry yeast
- 1 pinch sugar
- 1 1/4 cup bread flour, (unsifted)

The dough
^^^^^^^^^

- 6 ounces butter, (unsalted) cold
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 1/4 teaspoon Salt
- 4 large egg
- 1 large egg, for egg wash

Directions
----------

1. Make the sponge: pour the milk into a small bowl and sprinkle the yeast
   over it, then add the sugar and stir. Mix in 1/2 cup of flour until well
   blended, then scatter the remaining 3/4 cup flour over the top to cover the
   sponge. Let stand at room temp. until spongy and doubled in volume, about
   30-35 minutes; the flour on top will crack.
2. Start the dough: remove the cold butter from the fridge. Lay a strip of
   plastic wrap on a work surface, unwrap the butter and set it on the strip.
   Set the wrappings on top of the butter and hit with a rolling pin to
   flatten and soften, then set nearby at room temp.
3. Pour the flour, sugar and salt into a 14 cup capacity food processor bowl
   fitted with the steel blade; pulse briefly to blend. Pour the sponge over
   the dry ingredients and pulse 3-5 times to incorporate the yeast mixture,
   then add the eggs on top and process for 15-20 seconds. Continue until most
   of the dough wraps around the blade, then remove it. Turn out onto a
   lightly floured work surface and gently knead until a smooth, cohesive
   dough forms, about 1 minute.
4. Put the dough into a large bowl and cover with plastic wrap, then cover the
   bowl securely with another piece. Let rise in a cool place until doubled in
   volume, about 2-2 1/2 hours. Gently punch down and place in a clean bowl,
   set a piece of plastic wrap directly on the dough, cover the bowl securely,
   and refrigerate overnight.
5. Form the loaves: lightly grease two pans and shape the dough into loaves.
6. Cover the pan with plastic wrap and leave at room temp. for 3 hours.
   Preheat the oven to 375F. Beat the egg with a teaspoon of water and brush
   the top of the loaves, then bake until golden, 35 minutes. Remove and cool
   on a wire rack.
