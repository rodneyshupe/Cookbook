Bao Buns
========

+--------------------+-------------------------------+-----------------+
| Prep Time: 2 hours | Total Time: 2 hours 8 minutes | Yield: 14 to 16 |
+--------------------+-------------------------------+-----------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1/3 cup warm water
- 1/2 cup warm milk
- 1 tablespoon active dry yeast
- 4 tablespoons sugar, divided half
- 2 tablespoons avocado, vegetable or canola oil
- 2 1/2 cups all purpose flour (use blenched flour if you want complete white buns)
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

Directions
----------

1. Combine warm water, milk, yeast, sugar, and oil; whisk to dissolve the
   yeast and sugar, then let sit until the yeast activates, about 5 to 10
   minutes.
2. Meanwhile, combine flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a stand mixer,
   or mix by hand if you prefer.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry mixture. Start on low speed to slowly
   incorporate everything, then move to medium speed until it takes a dough
   shape; knead 3 to 4 minutes on medium speed. The dough should be elastic
   and really soft but not stick to your fingers or the bowl.
4. Take the dough off the hook and form a ball; place it back in the bowl,
   cover with plastic wrap, and let rise in a warm place until tripled in
   size, about 2 hours. Let it sit longer if needed.
5. Turn the raised dough onto a work surface and roll out 1/4-inch thick.
   Sprinkling flour should not be necessary, but if you do, use as little as
   possible. Cut into 3 1/2-inch circles with a ring mold or glass; this makes
   14 to 16 baos.
6. Lightly brush or spray oil on one surface of each bao and fold in half into
   a half-moon shape. Gently press each with a roller and place on a
   parchment- or coffee-filter-lined steamer; cover with a lid and rest 30
   minutes.
7. Meanwhile, bring water to a boil in a wok or steamer pot. Carefully set the
   steamer of baos over the boiling water, making sure the water is not
   touching the baos. Cover and steam until done, 8 to 12 minutes. Tilt the
   lid a tiny bit for slow air circulation, about 2 to 3 minutes, before
   opening.
