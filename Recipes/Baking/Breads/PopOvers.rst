Pop Overs
=========

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 40 minutes | Yield: 6 popovers |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter, melted and cooled, plus 1 teaspoon for
  the pan
- 4 3/4 ounces all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 1 cup whole milk, at room temperature

Directions
----------

1. Heat the oven to 400F, then grease a 6-cup popover pan with the 1 teaspoon
   of butter.
2. Combine the 1 tablespoon of butter, the flour, salt, eggs, and milk in a
   food processor or blender; process for 30 seconds.
3. Divide the batter evenly among the cups of the popover pan, filling each
   one-third to one-half full; bake on the middle rack for 40 minutes, taking
   care not to open the oven door. Remove the popovers to a cooling rack and
   pierce each one in the top with a knife to let steam escape. Serve warm.
