Creamy Potato Salad with Jalapeno
=================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 4 - 6 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes (about 4 large), peeled, and cut into
  1-inch by 1-inch cubes
- Kosher salt
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1 teaspoon dijon mustard (made with white wine)
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 jalapeno, sliced into rounds (remove seeds for less spice)
- 2 teaspoons chopped dill
- 2 teaspoons chopped parsley
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

Directions
----------

1. Cover the potatoes with cold water in a large pot, season with salt, and
   bring to a boil. Cook until just under fork tender, about 25 minutes, so
   the potatoes still have a bite. Drain in a colander in the sink, cover with
   a kitchen towel, and let cool until mostly cooled, about 10 minutes.
2. Toss the potatoes with the mayonnaise, mustard, lemon juice, jalapeno,
   dill, parsley, and black pepper. Taste and adjust the seasonings if
   necessary with more salt and pepper.
3. Serve at room temperature, or make in advance and keep cold in the
   refrigerator.
