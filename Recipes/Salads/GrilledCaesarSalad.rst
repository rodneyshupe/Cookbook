Grilled Caesar Salad
====================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Yield: 12 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 3 cups cubed French bread, about 1 1/2-inch cubes
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 heads (or 3 hearts) romaine lettuce, quartered through the core
- 4 lemons, halved
- 2 teaspoons anchovy paste
- 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
- 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- Parmigiano Reggiano shavings
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper

Directions
----------

1. Preheat your grill or a griddle pan to medium and toss the bread into a
   bowl. Melt together the butter, 1 tablespoon of olive oil, and garlic
   powder in a small microwave safe dish, 15 to 20 seconds. Drizzle over the
   bread, season with salt and pepper, and toss well to combine. Transfer the
   bread cubes to the grill or griddle pan and toast, turning frequently,
   until the croutons are golden and just starting to char, then transfer to a
   bowl and set aside.
2. Drizzle the remaining tablespoon of oil evenly over the quartered romaine
   heads and season with a small pinch of salt. Place the romaine quarters and
   lemon halves cut side down onto the grill and cook until the greens and
   lemons are starting to char, 2 minutes or so. Remove the lemons and lettuce
   from the heat, transfer the romaine to a serving platter grilled side up,
   and set aside.
3. Whisk the anchovy paste, mustard, and garlic together in the bottom of a
   large mixing bowl. Whisk in the red wine vinegar and 3 to 4 tablespoons of
   juice from one of the lemon halves you just grilled. Continue whisking and
   slowly drizzle in the extra virgin olive oil, then season with salt and
   pepper to taste.
4. Drizzle the romaine with dressing, making sure some gets in between the
   layers of leaves. Scatter the croutons across the top along with some
   Parmigiano Reggiano shavings, season with a bit more pepper, and arrange
   the grilled lemons around the platter for serving.
