All Canadian Potato Salad
=========================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Total Time: 45 minutes | Yield: 6 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1.5kg (3 pounds) Yukon gold potatoes, cut into 1/2 inch pieces or baby potatoes
- 1/4 cup + 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1/3 cup grainy mustard
- 2 tablespoons of liquid honey
- 1/3 cup canola oil
- 3 green onions, sliced

Directions
----------

1. Place a large pot of water on the stove, add the potatoes, and salt the
   water generously. Bring to a boil and cook until fork tender but still
   slightly firm, about 8 minutes.
2. Drain the potatoes and transfer to a large mixing bowl. Pour the
   tablespoons of apple cider vinegar over the hot potatoes, toss gently, and
   set aside to cool completely.
3. Whisk together the 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar, grain mustard, honey, and
   canola oil in a separate bowl.
4. Once the potatoes are cool, add the green onions. Add the dressing and toss
   to coat all the potatoes, then refrigerate until ready to serve.
