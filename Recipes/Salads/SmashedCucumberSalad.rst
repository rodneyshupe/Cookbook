Smashed Cucumber Salad
======================

+-----------------------+-------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Total Time: 1 1/2 hours | Yield: 6 servings |
+-----------------------+-------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 English cucumbers
- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt, plus more as needed
- 1 teaspoon white sugar
- 2 cloves garlic, finely crushed
- 2 tablespoons seasoned rice vinegar
- 1 teaspoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- red pepper flakes to taste
- 2 teaspoons toasted sesame seeds

Directions
----------

1. Wrap each cucumber in plastic wrap to minimize splattering, then place on a
   work surface and pound with a flat object (like a meat pounder) until
   cucumbers crack and are slightly flattened. Remove from plastic, cut
   cucumbers in half, and halve each side lengthwise. Slice into 1- to 1 1/2
   -inch wide slices and transfer to a strainer set over a bowl.
2. Sprinkle cucumber with salt and sugar and mix until well combined.
   Refrigerate the bowl and strainer until the cucumber pieces drain into the
   bowl, 30 to 60 minutes.
3. Whisk garlic, seasoned rice vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, and red pepper
   flakes together thoroughly in a large bowl.
4. Transfer drained cucumbers into the bowl with dressing and toss to coat.
   Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate until the flavours mingle, about 30
   minutes.
5. Place cucumbers in a serving bowl and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Notes
-----

- In place of seasoned rice vinegar, you can use plain rice vinegar and
  add an extra pinch of salt and sugar to the dressing
