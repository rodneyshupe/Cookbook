Grapefruit, Avocado, Raspberries and Watercress Salad
=====================================================

+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 bunch watercress
- 2 pink grapefruit, segmented
- 2 avocado, sliced
- kosher salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- extra virgin olive oil
- flat leaf parsley, chopped
- 1/2 cup raspberries
- Balsamic vinegar
- 2 cup spring salad mix
- 1 red onion, sliced finely

Directions
----------

1. Simply place all ingredients in a bowl, season, and add 2 parts extra
   virgin olive oil and 1 part balsamic vinegar.
2. Toss gently, and serve on a platter or individually.
