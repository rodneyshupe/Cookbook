Simple Potato Salad
===================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Yield: 6 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1.5 pounds baby or pee wee or small potatoes
- 3 celery stalks, diced
- 1/4 cup chopped curly parsley
- 2 tablespoons fresh dill, finely chopped
- 10 ounces bacon, cooked and chopped
- 3 tablespoons honey
- 3 tablespoons dijon mustard
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- juice of one lemon
- Kosher salt & pepper

Directions
----------

1. Cook the potatoes in boiling water until soft enough for a fork to pierce
   easily. Drain, run cool water over them, and when cool enough to handle,
   halve the smaller ones and 1/4 the larger ones.
2. Put the potato pieces in a large bowl and add the celery, parsley, dill and
   bacon.
3. Combine the honey, mustard, garlic, lemon juice, and salt and pepper to
   taste. Mix well, add about half to the salad, and stir through to combine.
   Add more dressing if necessary.
