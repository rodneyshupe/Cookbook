Moroccan Chicken, Crispy Romaine Salad
======================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 25 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Total Time: 1 hour 5 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 chicken breast
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- pinch red pepper flakes
- 1 lime, juiced
- 2 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 2 cup romaine lettuce, cubed
- 2 cup radicchio lettuce, sliced
- 1 avocado, peeled, sliced
- 2 tablespoon cilantro
- 8 cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 small yellow pepper, julienne
- 2 tablespoon Flat leaf parsley

Mango , Lemon, Cumin Dressing
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- 1-2 mango, cleaned, cubed
- 1 tablespoon Ginger, minced
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- pinch ground coriander
- 1 teaspoon Ground cumin
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoon White wine vinegar
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 tablespoon cilantro

Directions
----------

1. Marinade the chicken first. Cut the chicken into strips and place in a
   bowl. Add sea salt, pepper, red pepper flakes, lime juice, garlic, and
   olive oil. Cover and refrigerate at least 30 minutes. Grill the chicken or
   sear it in a skillet, then chill.
2. Meanwhile, prepare the dressing. In a food processor or blender, combine
   mango, ginger, sea salt, pepper, coriander, and ground cumin. Puree for 1
   minute. Slowly add the olive oil while blending, then blend in vinegar,
   lemon, and cilantro. Set aside and correct the seasoning if needed.
3. In a large bowl, combine the rest of the ingredients (cool the cooked
   chicken first if desired). Slowly add the dressing and toss gently.

Notes
-----

Carb count 5g. per serving
