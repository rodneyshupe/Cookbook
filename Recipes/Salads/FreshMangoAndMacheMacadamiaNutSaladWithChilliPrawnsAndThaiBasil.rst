Fresh Mango and Mache Macadamia Nut Salad with Chilli Prawns and Thai Basil
===========================================================================

+-----------------------+----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 25 minutes | Cook Time: 5 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 tablespoon Sesame oil
- 16 tiger prawns, shelled, de-veined
- 1 tablespoon Sambal Oelek
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- 1/4 cup white wine
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- 1/2 red onion, sliced finely
- 1/2 cup macadamia nuts
- 1/2 cup grape tomatoes
- 1/2 yellow pepper, diced
- 2 mango, sliced
- 4 cups spring salad mix
- 2 tablespoon Thai basil, chopped
- 1 lime, juiced
- 4 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoon Red wine vinegar

Directions
----------

1. Heat a skillet and add the sesame oil. Season the prawns in a bowl with
   sambal, salt, and pepper, then sear until they have turned colour and
   firmed up, approx. 2 minutes.
2. Add the white wine and lemon juice, and reduce for 1 minute. Set aside to
   cool.
3. Place the spring salad mix, onions, nuts, tomatoes, yellow peppers, mangoes,
   and Thai basil in a large bowl, then season.
4. Whisk together the lime juice, olive oil, and red wine vinegar in a small
   bowl.
5. Drizzle the vinaigrette slowly into the salad and toss gently.
6. Portion onto 4 plates and arrange the chilli prawns around the salad.
