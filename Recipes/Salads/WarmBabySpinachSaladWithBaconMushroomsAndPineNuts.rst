Warm Baby Spinach Salad with Bacon, Mushrooms and Pine Nuts
===========================================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 cup french bread, cubed
- 2 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon parsley, chopped
- 1 tablespoon basil, chopped
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- extra virgin olive oil
- 4 cup baby spinach leaves
- 1/2 cup pine nuts, toasted
- 7 ounces double smoked bacon, diced
- 1 red onion, sliced finely
- 1 cup button mushrooms, sliced
- 8 cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 small yellow pepper, julienne
- 3 tablespoon red wine vinegar
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 7 ounces baby bocconcini balls

Directions
----------

1. Preheat oven to 400F.
2. Make the croutons: cut bread into oversize cubes, add garlic, parsley,
   basil, salt, pepper, and approx 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil, then
   mix and toss well. Spread onto a baking sheet and bake until lightly golden
   and crisp. Do not over bake, since you want the croutons slightly tender in
   the centre. Put aside.
3. Wash and pat dry the baby spinach leaves, keep cool, and put aside. Toast
   the pine nuts in the oven on a tray until golden, approx 7-10 minutes, then
   put aside.
4. Cook the bacon in a skillet until crisp, drain excess fat or set on a paper
   towel, and put aside.
5. Add 6 tablespoons olive oil to the same skillet and heat. Add the onions,
   season, and saute until golden. Add the mushrooms, season, and saute for 3
   minutes. Add the bacon, tomatoes, and peppers, season, and saute for 1-2
   minutes. Turn off the heat, add 3 tablespoons red wine vinegar and the
   juice of 1 lemon, and stir.
6. Place the spinach, croutons, pine nuts, and bocconcini in a large bowl. Add
   the warm mixture and toss gently.
