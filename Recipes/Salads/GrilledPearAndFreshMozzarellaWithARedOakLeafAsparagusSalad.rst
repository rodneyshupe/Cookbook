Grilled Pear and Fresh Mozzarella with a Red Oak Leaf Asparagus Salad
=====================================================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 pears, sliced, quartered
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed
- kosher salt
- 1/4 cup basil grapeseed oil
- 2 tablespoon red wine vinegar
- 1 teaspoon grainy dijon mustard
- fresh cracked pepper
- 2 ball mozzarella, sliced
- 1 head red oak leaf lettuce, chopped
- 1 red pepper, julienne
- 2 tablespoon mint, fresh, chopped

Directions
----------

1. Preheat the char-broiler. Place the pears in a bowl and toss with the lemon
   juice, then set them on a seasoned, oiled grill and cook until golden on
   both sides. Move to a plate and set aside.
2. Meanwhile, bring a small saucepan of water with a pinch of salt to a boil.
   Add the asparagus and blanch for 45 seconds, then remove and submerge in
   ice cold water to stop the cooking. Once cooled, drain.
3. Whisk the grapeseed oil, red wine vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper in a
   bowl for the vinaigrette.
4. Combine all the ingredients in a large bowl, season, and toss, then serve
   onto individual plates or a platter.

Notes
-----

Carb count 7g. per serving
