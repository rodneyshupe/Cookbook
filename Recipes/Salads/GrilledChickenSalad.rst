Grilled Chicken Salad
=====================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 3 Chicken breasts
- kosher salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- 4 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 1/4 cup soy sauce, (light)
- 1 teaspoon garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon ginger, grated
- 2 tablespoon white wine vinegar
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 tablespoon grainy dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon flat leaf parsley
- 1/2 cup red seedless grapes
- 1/2 red pepper, julienne
- 4 cups spring salad mix
- 1/4 cup pecan, toasted

Directions
----------

1. Preheat and season the grill, then season both sides of the chicken.
2. Grill the chicken until done.
3. Meanwhile, whisk the dressing in a bowl: combine oil, soy sauce, garlic,
   vinegar, ginger, lemon juice, dijon, parsley and seasoning.
4. Slice the cooked chicken into strips and place in a large bowl with grapes,
   peppers, greens and pecans, then drizzle the dressing over top and toss
   gently.
5. Serve on a platter or individual plates.

Notes
-----

Carb count 9g per serving
