Grilled Jumbo Tiger Prawns on a bed of Crisp Greens
===================================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 25 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 4 skewers
- 3 tablespoon butter
- 1 tablespoon Ginger, chopped
- 2 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoon Flat leaf parsley, chopped
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- 6 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tablespoon Sherry vinegar
- 1 tablespoon grainy dijon mustard
- 24 jumbo prawns, peeled and de-veined
- 4 cup assorted greens
- 1 small red onion, sliced
- 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes
- 1 small yellow pepper, julienne
- 1-2 lemon
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper

Directions
----------

1. Soak the skewers in water.
2. Make the sauce for the prawns: melt the butter, then add the ginger,
   garlic, parsley, and seasoning. Set aside.
3. Make the vinaigrette: in a bowl, whisk the oil, vinegar, Dijon, and
   seasoning. Set aside.
4. Wash and dry the salad greens. Set aside.
5. Thread 6 prawns onto each skewer and season.
6. Preheat the grill, lay the skewers on, and baste continuously with the
   sauce. Cook until firm, approx. 3-4 minutes per side.
7. Place the salad ingredients in a bowl, season, add the vinaigrette, and
   toss.
8. Portion the greens onto 4 plates and top with the skewered prawns.
