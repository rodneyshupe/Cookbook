Shrimp, Avocado, And Artichoke Salad
====================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 12 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 7 ounces Baby shrimp
- 2 avocado, diced
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 2 tablespoon dill, fresh, chopped
- 300 ml Artichokes, sliced
- 1 teaspoon jalapeno pepper, chopped
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 2 tablespoon Flat leaf parsley, chopped
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper

Roasted Peppers
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- 4 bell pepper
- 2 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoon sage, fresh, chopped
- 2 clove garlic, minced
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper

Directions
----------

1. Preheat oven to 375F. Remove the top part of the pepper (core side), making
   a slice across by a 1/2 " down, then carefully remove the seeds so as not
   to break the peppers.
2. Line a baking dish with parchment paper and place the peppers and tops onto
   it. Season the inside with salt and pepper.
3. Blend the oil, sage, garlic, and seasoning together in a small bowl, then
   brush the inside of the peppers liberally with the sage oil.
4. Roast in the oven until tender, approximately 10 - 12 minutes. Remove and
   let cool.
5. Add the ingredients to a bowl and blend well, then place the mixture inside
   the peppers.
6. Place the tops of the peppers on an angle to garnish. If the peppers are
   over roasted they will collapse, which is why the outer skin is not removed
   and they are lightly roasted.

Notes
-----

Carb count 8g. per serving
