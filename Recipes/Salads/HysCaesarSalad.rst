Hy's Caesar Salad
=================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 30 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

Dressing
^^^^^^^^

- 1 teaspoon crushed garlic
- 1/2 teaspoon minced anchovies
- 1/2 teaspoon Hy's seasoning salt
- 1/2 teaspoon fresh crushed black pepper
- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1/4 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 egg yolk
- 1/2 teaspoon Tabasco
- 1/2 tablespoon red wine vinegar
- 2 tablespoons Canola oil
- juice of half lemon

Salad
^^^^^

- 2 good handfuls of Romaine lettuce
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1/2 cup Homemade Croutons (see below)

Croutons
^^^^^^^^

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, peeled
- 1 cup day-old bread, cubed
- salt and pepper

Directions
----------

1. For the croutons: Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cut
   the cloves of garlic into quarters and add to the hot oil. Cook and stir
   until brown, then remove the garlic from the pan. Add the bread cubes to
   the hot oil and cook, turning frequently, until lightly browned. Remove the
   bread cubes from the oil and season with salt and pepper.
2. For the dressing: Combine the garlic, anchovies, seasoning salt, and black
   pepper in a large mixing bowl. Mix thoroughly with the back of a salad
   spoon until a paste forms.
3. Add the Worcestershire sauce, Dijon, egg yolk, and Tabasco to the bowl and
   mix well. Add the red wine vinegar. Slowly add the canola oil, stirring
   continuously until the mixture thickens slightly. Add the lemon juice and
   mix well.
4. To assemble: Add the lettuce and toss until all the leaves are coated with
   dressing. Add half the Parmesan cheese and toss again until the cheese
   sticks to the dressing on the leaves. Add the croutons and toss lightly.
5. Divide the salad onto plates and finish by sprinkling the rest of the
   cheese on top. Add more black pepper to taste if desired.
