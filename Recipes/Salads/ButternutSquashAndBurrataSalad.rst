Butternut Squash and Burrata Salad
==================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 Butternut squash, cut in half
- 2 4-ounce (118 ml) container Burrata (2 ounces per person)
- 4 cups packed mixed greens
- `Honey Vinaigrette <#honey-vinaigrette>`__
- Pumpkin seeds or pine nuts

Directions
----------

1. Preheat oven to 350F on convection or 375F if not convection, and line a
   sheet pan with parchment paper.
2. Season the butternut squash with salt and sprinkle with vegetable oil.
   Place cut side down on the parchment and bake until fork tender, about 30
   minutes. (this can be done ahead of time)
3. Scoop the cooked squash out and place it in a bowl.
4. Assemble the salad by placing burrata on a plate, then add squash, the
   mixed greens, 1-2 tablespoon vinaigrette, and some pumpkin seeds.
