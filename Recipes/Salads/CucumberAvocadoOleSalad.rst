Cucumber Avocado Ole Salad
==========================

+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 25 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 cucumber, peeled, seeded
- 2 avocado, diced
- 2 roma tomatoes, diced
- 2 lime, juiced
- 2 tablespoon Cilantro, chopped
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- extra virgin olive oil
- Red wine vinegar
- 1 tablespoon grainy dijon mustard
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 4 cup crisp greens
- 200 ml red pepper, roasted and julienne
- 1 cup blue corn tortillas chips

Directions
----------

1. Combine the cucumber, avocado, roma tomatoes, lime juice, cilantro,
   seasoning, and 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil in a bowl and toss.
2. Whisk 2 parts olive oil to 1 part vinegar in a small bowl, then add the
   grainy Dijon, lemon juice, and garlic and set the vinaigrette aside.
3. Place the greens and roasted peppers in a salad bowl, season, drizzle with
   vinaigrette, and toss.
4. Portion the salad onto four plates and top with the cool cucumber avocado
   salad.
5. Arrange the corn chips around the salad.
