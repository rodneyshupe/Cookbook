Green Onion Jam
===============

+-----------------------+----------------------+--------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Total Time: 1 minute | Yield: 1 cup |
+-----------------------+----------------------+--------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
- 1 pound green onions, thinly sliced
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1 tablespoon sherry vinegar
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons flake kosher sea salt

Directions
----------

1. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat, then add the onions
   and 1 tablespoon of the oil and reduce the heat to low. Cook uncovered
   until the onions soften, about 10 minutes. Add the vinegar, sugar, salt and
   remaining 2 tablespoons of oil and stir to incorporate, then cook until the
   onions turn deeply golden and fragrant, about 40 minutes, stirring every 10
   minutes.
2. Remove from the heat and cool to room temperature, then transfer the jam to
   a mason jar. Seal and refrigerate; keep for up to 1 month.
