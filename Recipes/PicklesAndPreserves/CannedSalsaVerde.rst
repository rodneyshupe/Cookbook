Canned Salsa Verde
==================

+-----------------------+------------------------------+----------+
| Prep Time: 25 minutes | Total Time: 1 hour 5 minutes | Yield: 4 |
+-----------------------+------------------------------+----------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 5 1/2 cups (about 2 pounds) chopped tomatillos, husked and cores removed
- 1 cup chopped onion
- 1 cup chopped peppers (I used a mix of green bell, jalapenos, and thai chilies)
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked Spanish paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 cup white vinegar
- 4 tablespoons lime juice
- 1/3 cup minced cilantro

Directions
----------

1. Have jars ready for canning, sterilized and hot.
2. Preheat oven to 500F. Place 3/4 of the chopped tomatillos on a baking
   sheet, reserve remaining 1/4; roast until slightly charred and juices are
   seeping out, about 20 minutes.
3. While they roast, chop the onion and peppers in a food processor, then put
   into the sauce pot you will be using to cook.
4. Remove tomatillos from oven, let cool a few minutes, and pulse in the food
   processor along with the 1/4 raw tomatillos you reserved; add all
   tomatillos to the sauce pot with the onions/peppers, along with the garlic,
   cumin, paprika, salt, vinegar, and lime juice. Bring to a boil, then turn
   down to a simmer for 12 minutes; during the last minute add the minced
   cilantro.
5. Ladle hot salsa into hot sterilized jars, leaving 1/2 inch head space, then
   place sterilized rings and lids on top and return to a boiling water bath
   for 15 minutes. Remove and let cool, listen for the pop!
