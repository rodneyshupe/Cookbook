Mustard Pickles
===============

+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+---------------------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Total Time: 8 hours | Yield: about four 16 ounce jars |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+---------------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 4 large cucumbers, sliced
- 2 cups sliced onion
- 1 tablespoon pickling salt
- 1 quarts cold water, or as needed
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- 1 cup white vinegar
- 1/2 tablespoon ground dried turmeric
- 1/2 tablespoon dry mustard powder
- 1/4 teaspoon celery seed

Directions
----------

1. Place the sliced cucumbers and onions into a large bowl and sprinkle the
   salt over the top; fill the bowl with enough water to cover the cucumbers
   and let stand for 8 to 10 hours.
2. Drain and rinse the cucumbers and onion with fresh water, then sterilize 4
   (pint) jars and lids.
3. In a 6 quart pot, stir together the sugar and flour, then whisk in the
   vinegar, turmeric, mustard powder and celery seed to make a smooth paste;
   add the cucumbers and onions and fill with enough water to cover, then stir
   and bring to a boil and cook until thickened, about 10 minutes, stirring
   frequently.
4. Pour into hot sterilized jars, filling to within 1/2 inch of the top, then
   seal with lids and rings and process for 10 minutes in a simmering water
   bath; refrigerate any jars that fail to seal properly.
