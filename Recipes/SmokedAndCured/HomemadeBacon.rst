Homemade Bacon
==============

+-----------------------+-------------------+--------------------+---------------------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cure Time: 7 days | Cook Time: 2 hours | Yield: about 2 1/2 pounds bacon |
+-----------------------+-------------------+--------------------+---------------------------------+

Equipment
---------
Smoker

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 1/2 pounds (1.1 kg) pork belly, squared off, rind removed
- 2 1/2 tablespoons kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon instacure no. 1 (prague powder no. 1)
- 1/4 cup brown sugar (possible substitutions maple syrup, honey, white
  sugar or molasses)
- 2 tablespoons bourbon (possible substitution apple cider)

Directions
----------

1. Place the pork belly in a large Ziploc bag; add the salt, pink salt, and
   cure additions, then rub the cure into the belly, turning the bag over and
   over and pressing it into the flesh.
2. Close the bag, squeezing out all the air, and refrigerate for seven days,
   flipping the bag each day; some liquid will gather in the bag.
3. After seven days, wash the cure off the meat, rinsing thoroughly, then pat
   dry with paper towels and set on a rack over a baking sheet; air-dry in the
   refrigerator for 6 to 24 hours.
4. Preheat the smoker to 200F, then smoke for about 2 hours (or to an internal
   temperature of 150F) with Apple (or Maple) wood.
5. Chill the bacon well, then slice thick or thin, to preference; cut any
   bacon that doesn't easily slice into chunks for starting a pot of beans or
   soup.
6. Wrapped in parchment paper, then in plastic wrap or foil and placed in a
   Ziploc bag, the bacon keeps for three weeks in the refrigerator and three
   months in the freezer.

Notes
-----

If not using a smoker, perform step 3 in an oven and add 1/2 tablespoon of
liquid smoke to the cure.
