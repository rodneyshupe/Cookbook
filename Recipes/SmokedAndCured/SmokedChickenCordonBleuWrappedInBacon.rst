Smoked Chicken Cordon Bleu wrapped in Bacon
===========================================

+-----------------------+--------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 2 hours | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+--------------------+-------------------+

Equipment
---------
Smoker

Ingredients
-----------

*(per cordon bleu)*

- 5 pieces of bacon
- 1 boneless/skinless chicken breast, sliced in half and pounded flat.
- 2 slices of ham
- 2 slices of provolone cheese

Directions
----------

1. Lay out 4 slices of bacon side by side, then lay 1 slice across the middle
   of the 4 slices.
2. Lay 1 slice of chicken on the bacon, then add ham and cheese; finally add
   the second slice of chicken, ham and cheese.
3. Roll the chicken, ham and cheese into a tight roll.
4. Wrap the 4 bacon slices around the roll, then close off the end with the
   single bacon slice and pin with toothpicks.
5. Preheat smoker to 250F and smoke for 2 hours with Hickory.

Make Ahead
----------

Can be prepared in advance (and even frozen) then re-heated in a 200F oven
for 30 minutes.

Notes
-----

Can also be enjoyed served cold, sliced with salad or makes a delicious
sandwich.
