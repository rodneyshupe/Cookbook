"Cold Smoked" Salmon a la Montreal
==================================

+-----------------------+-------------------+-------------------+--------------------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cure Time: 7 days | Dry Time: 12 days | Yield: about 2-3 pounds salmon |
+-----------------------+-------------------+-------------------+--------------------------------+

Equipment
---------
UMAi Dry Bag, Vacuum Sealer

Ingredients
-----------

Curing Mixture
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- 500g Turbinado sugar
- 500g Kosher salt
- 20g `Montreal seasoning <#montreal-seasoning>`__
- 10g Freshly ground black pepper

Salmon
^^^^^^

- 1-1 1/2kg (approx. 2-3 pounds) fresh skin on salmon
- Paprika to coat
- Liquid smoke, 5ml per 500g of fish

Directions
----------

1. Mix curing ingredients together and keep in a Ziploc bag or Mason jar.
2. Remove bones from salmon fillet, sprinkle on paprika, and rub into the
   surface to provide a deep red colour; place fillet into a resealable bag or
   container so it can rest flat, cutting into two or four parts as you wish.
3. Add curing mixture at 50% the weight of the fish along with the appropriate
   amount of liquid smoke, then toss to coat the surface well.
4. Refrigerate 7 days, turning to redistribute the cure each day.
5. After 7 days, remove fish and rinse lightly under fresh cold water, then
   pat dry with clean paper towel; vacuum seal into a UMAi Dry bag of the
   appropriate size and refrigerate about 12 days. Slice and serve.
