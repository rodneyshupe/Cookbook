Smoked Competition Ribs
=======================

+-----------------------+------------------------+----------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 3 3/4 hours | Yield: 2 racks |
+-----------------------+------------------------+----------------+

Equipment
---------
Smoker

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 racks pork ribs (I prefer to use side ribs)
- 1 cup `Basic Barbecue Rub <#basic-barbecue-rub>`__
- 2 cups wood chips, preferably apple wood, soaked in 2 cups apple
  cider for 1 hour then drained
- 4 tablespoons butter, softened
- 3 tablespoons brown sugar
- 3 tablespoons agave syrup (may substitute with honey)
- 1 cup `Kansas City Style Sweet and Smoky Barbecue Sauce <#kansas-city-style-sweet-and-smoky-barbecue-sauce>`__

Mop Sauce
^^^^^^^^^

- 1/2 cup Apple Cider Vinegar
- 1/2 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1 tablespoon `Basic Barbecue Rub <#basic-barbecue-rub>`__
- 1/4 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1/2 cup Coke
- 1/2 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce

Directions
----------

1. Pull the papery skin off the back of each rack in a sheet with your fingers
   or a pair of pliers.
2. Spread mustard all over the ribs, then sprinkle rub on both sides, patting
   it in with your fingers; cover and marinate in the refrigerator for 1 - 4
   hours.
3. Mix together Mop Sauce ingredients.
4. Set up the smoker to 240F with enough wood for 2 hours of smoke; once up to
   temperature, place the ribs on racks and set in the smoker for 2 hours,
   basting with mop sauce every 30 minutes.
5. After the 2 hours, remove ribs from the smoker and baste one last time with
   mop sauce, then spread 2 tablespoons of butter on each rack and sprinkle
   with 1 1/2 tablespoons brown sugar and 1 1/2 tablespoons agave syrup; wrap
   ribs in foil and return to the smoker for 1 1/2 hours, until the meat is
   very tender and pulled back from the ends of the bones.
6. Unwrap ribs and brush with half of the cup of barbecue sauce (set aside the
   other half for serving at the table).
7. Return ribs to the smoker and continue cooking while the sauce sets, about
   15 minutes.
8. Transfer the ribs to a platter, sprinkle with the remaining rub, and serve
   with the other half cup of barbecue sauce.
