Air Fryer Bacon
===============

+----------------------+-------------------------+-----------------+
| Prep Time: 2 minutes | Cook Time: 9-12 minutes | Yield: 5 slices |
+----------------------+-------------------------+-----------------+

Equipment
---------
Air Fryer

Ingredients
-----------

- 5 slices bacon (regular bacon or thick-cut)

Directions
----------

1. Lay the bacon strips in a single layer in the air fryer basket, cutting
   strips in half if needed to fit more.
2. Air fry regular cut bacon at 350F until browned and at your desired
   crispiness, 7-9 minutes; for thick-cut, air fry 10-12 minutes.
3. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate and serve. If cooking in batches,
   discard any oil in the drip pan before repeating.
