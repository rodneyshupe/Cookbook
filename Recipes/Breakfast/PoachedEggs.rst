Poached Eggs
============

+----------------------+----------------------+---------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Cook Time: 5 minutes | Yield: 4 eggs |
+----------------------+----------------------+---------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 4 large eggs
- 1 tablespoon distilled white vinegar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- Salt and pepper (to taste)

Directions
----------

1. Bring 6 cups water to boil in Dutch oven over high heat. Meanwhile, crack
   eggs one at a time into colander and let stand until loose, watery whites
   drain away, 20 to 30 seconds; gently transfer eggs to 2-cup liquid
   measuring cup.
2. Add vinegar and 1 teaspoon salt to boiling water. Hold lip of measuring cup
   just above the surface and gently tip eggs into the water one at a time,
   leaving space between them. Cover pot, remove from heat, and let stand
   until whites closest to yolks are just set and opaque, about 3 minutes. If
   whites are not set after 3 minutes, let stand in water, checking every 30
   seconds, until eggs reach desired doneness. (For medium-cooked yolks, let
   eggs sit in the covered pot for 4 minutes, then begin checking for
   doneness.)
3. Using slotted spoon, carefully lift and drain each egg over the Dutch oven.
   Season with salt and pepper to taste, and serve.
