Almond Granola with Dried Fruit
===============================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+---------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 45 minutes | Total Time: 2 hours | Yield: 9 cups |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+---------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1/3 cup maple syrup
- 1/3 cup packed (2 1/3 ounces) light brown sugar
- 4 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 5 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
- 2 cups (10 ounces) raw almonds, chopped coarse
- 2 cups raisins or other dried fruit, chopped

Directions
----------

1. Adjust oven rack to upper-middle position and heat oven to 325F; line
   rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Whisk maple syrup, brown sugar, vanilla, and salt in large bowl, then whisk
   in oil; fold in oats and almonds until thoroughly coated.
3. Transfer oat mixture to prepared baking sheet and spread into thin, even
   layer (about 3/8 inch thick), then compress with stiff metal spatula until
   very compact. Bake until lightly browned, 40 to 45 minutes, rotating pan
   once halfway through. Cool on wire rack to room temperature, about 1 hour,
   then break into pieces of desired size and stir in dried fruit. *Granola
   can be stored in airtight container for up to 2 weeks.*
