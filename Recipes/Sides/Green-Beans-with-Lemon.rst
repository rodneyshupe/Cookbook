Green Beans with Lemon
======================

+----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Cook Time: 90 minutes | Total Time: 95 minutes | Yield: 4-6 servings |
+----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+---------------------+

Equipment
---------
Sous-Vide (immersion circulator) set to 180F, Vacuum Sealer

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 pound fresh slender green beans, washed and trimmed
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon lemon zest
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup toasted almonds, roughly chopped

Directions
----------

1. Preheat the sous-vide cooker to 180F; toss the green beans with oil and
   lemon zest, then transfer to a food-safe bag and vacuum seal.
2. Place the green beans in the water bath and cook for 90 minutes.
3. Remove the green beans from the bag and put them on a serving plate;
   drizzle with lemon juice and sprinkle with salt, then top with chopped
   almonds and serve.

Make Ahead
----------

Quick chill the pouch, submerged in ice water (half ice/half water) for
15 to 20 minutes and refrigerate until ready to reheat alongside meat or
fish in the water bath.
