Smashed Fingerling Potatoes
===========================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Total Time: overnight | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 pound Fingerling potatoes
- 1/4 cup oil
- salt

Directions
----------

1. Cook potatoes in water until fork tender; let cool in the water. When cool,
   remove and let cool overnight.
2. Next day, smash them flat. Get a pan on high heat with oil. Place smashed
   potatoes in the pan and sear. When golden brown, place in a single layer on
   a baking sheet and place in 200F oven.
