Braised Cabbage with Bacon and Apples
=====================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 45 minutes | Yield: 4 portions |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 8 slices thick sliced bacon, cut into 1/2 inch pieces
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 small Red cabbage, sliced thin, about a 1 1/4 pounds
- 2 apples, cut around the core and thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
- 2 sprigs thyme

Directions
----------

1. Heat a large lidded frying pan or pot over medium heat; cook the bacon
   until crisp, then drain off.
2. Add the olive oil and onion to the bacon; cook until the onions are soft,
   then add the garlic and cook until fragrant. Add the cabbage, apple,
   vinegar, thyme, and a healthy pinch of salt.
3. Stir and cover; reduce the heat to low and simmer until the cabbage is
   melting tender, 30-45 minutes. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
