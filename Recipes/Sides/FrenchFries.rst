French Fries
============

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 1/2 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes (about 6 medium), scrubbed, dried, sides squared off, and cut lengthwise into 1/4-inch by 1/4-inch batons
- 6 cups peanut oil
- 1/4 cup bacon fat, strained (optional)
- Kosher salt

Directions
----------

1. Combine potatoes, oil, and bacon fat (if using) in a large Dutch oven, then
   cook over high heat until oil has reached a rolling boil, about 5 minutes.
   Keep cooking, without stirring, until potatoes are limp but exteriors are
   beginning to firm, about 15 minutes.
2. Stir potatoes with tongs, gently scraping up any that stick, and keep
   cooking, stirring occasionally, until golden and crisp, 5 to 10 minutes
   longer. Transfer fries with a skimmer or slotted spoon to a thick paper bag
   or paper towels; season with salt and serve immediately.

Notes
-----

Flavouring the oil with bacon fat (optional) gives the fries a mild meaty
flavour. Peanut oil is best for frying, but vegetable or canola oil can be
substituted.
