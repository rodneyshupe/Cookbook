Pressure-Cooker Parmesan Risotto
================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 25 minutes | Yield: 4-6 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 small onion, chopped fine
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 1/2 cups Arborio rice
- 1/2 cup white wine
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth, warmed
- 1 ounce Parmesan cheese, grated (1/2 cup), plus extra for serving
- Salt and pepper

Directions
----------

1. BUILD FLAVOUR: Melt butter in pressure-cooker pot over medium-high heat.
   Add onion; cook until softened, about 5 minutes. Stir in garlic; cook until
   fragrant, about 30 seconds. Stir in rice and toast lightly, about 3
   minutes. Stir in wine; cook until almost evaporated, about 1 minute. Stir
   in 3 1/4 cups broth, then scrape up any rice stuck to the bottom of the pot
   with a wooden spoon.
2. HIGH PRESSURE FOR 6 MINUTES: Lock the lid and bring to high pressure over
   medium-high heat. As soon as the pot reaches high pressure, reduce heat to
   medium-low and cook for 6 minutes, adjusting heat as needed to maintain
   high pressure.
3. QUICK RELEASE PRESSURE: Remove pot from heat. Quick release the pressure,
   then carefully remove the lid, letting steam escape away from you.
4. BEFORE SERVING: Keep cooking over medium heat, stirring constantly, until
   rice is tender and liquid has thickened, about 6 minutes. Stir in Parmesan;
   season with salt and pepper to taste. Add the remaining 3/4 cup broth as
   needed to loosen the risotto before serving.
