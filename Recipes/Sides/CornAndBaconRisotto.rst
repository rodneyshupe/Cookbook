Corn and Bacon Risotto
======================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Yield: 1 serving |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+------------------+

Ingredients are per person for a side and can be scaled up to double
per person for a main.

Ingredients
-----------

- 1/2 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/4 cup Arborio rice
- 1 cup `Corn Stock <#corn-stock>`__ (can substitute vegetable stock)
- 1/4 cup corn kernels
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 ounces parmesan grated
- 2 tablespoons corn puree
- 1/2 to 1 tablespoon miso (the amount will be to taste)
- 1 tablespoon crispy bacon (may substitute sauteed mushrooms)

Directions
----------

1. Heat a pan with olive oil, add the rice, and toast until fragrant.
2. Turn the heat to medium, add 3/4 stock, and let it reduce; when all the
   stock is gone, if the rice is still hard add extra stock or water. It
   should be thick and clumpy.
3. To finish, add the corn and bring back to temperature.
4. Add butter, parmesan, salt, corn puree, and miso; stir until all
   incorporated.
5. Garnish with bacon.
