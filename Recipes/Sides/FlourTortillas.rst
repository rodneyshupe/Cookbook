Flour Tortillas
===============

+-----------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+
| Prep Time: 25 minutes | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Yield: 8 tortillas |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+--------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 3/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/4 cup vegetable shortening
- 1/2 cup hot water
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour for dusting

Directions
----------

1. Whisk flour, salt, and baking powder in a large bowl. Add vegetable
   shortening and work it in with a pastry cutter until it resembles coarse
   crumbs.
2. Form a well in the middle and mix in the hot water. Work it together with a
   fork, then transfer to a cutting board.
3. Knead until a smooth, soft dough forms, about 3 minutes; add more flour if
   it gets too sticky.
4. Dust the cutting board with more flour, cover the dough with the bowl, and
   let rest for 15 minutes.
5. Cut the dough into eight equal parts and cover until needed.
6. Roll out the balls of dough by hand or with a tortilla press.
7. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add a flattened tortilla and
   cook until it begins to bubble, about 1 minute. Flip and cook an additional
   minute, then flip again and cook 1 more minute. Repeat with the remaining
   dough.
