Skillet-Roasted Brussels Sprouts with Mustard and Brown Sugar
=============================================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+----------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Yield: 4 |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+----------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 pound small (1 to 1 1/2 inches in diameter) Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
- 5 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon packed brown sugar
- 2 teaspoons white wine vinegar
- 1/8 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- Salt

Directions
----------

1. Arrange Brussels sprouts cut sides down in a single layer in a 12-inch
   nonstick skillet, then drizzle oil evenly over them. Cover, set over
   medium-high heat, and cook until sprouts are bright green and cut sides
   have started to brown, about 5 minutes.
2. Uncover and keep cooking until cut sides are deeply and evenly browned and
   a paring knife slides in with little to no resistance, 2 to 3 minutes
   longer; adjust heat and move sprouts as needed to keep them from
   overbrowning. Meanwhile, combine mustard, sugar, vinegar, cayenne, and 1/4
   teaspoon salt in a small bowl.
3. Off heat, add the mustard mixture and stir to coat sprouts evenly. Season
   with salt to taste, then transfer to a large plate and serve.

Variations
----------

**Skillet-Roasted Brussels Sprouts with Maple Syrup and Smoked Almonds**
\* In step 2, swap the mustard, sugar, vinegar, and cayenne for 1 tablespoon
maple syrup and 1 tablespoon sherry vinegar. \* In step 3 once plated,
sprinkle with 1/4 cup smoked almonds (chopped fine).

**Skillet-Roasted Brussels Sprouts with Pomegranate and Pistachios** \*
In step 2, swap the mustard, sugar, vinegar, and cayenne for 1 tablespoon
pomegranate molasses and 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin. \* In step 3 once
plated, sprinkle with 1/4 cup shelled pistachios (toasted and chopped fine)
and 2 tablespoons pomegranate seeds.

**Skillet-Roasted Brussels Sprouts with Lemon and Pecorino Romano** \*
In step 2, swap the mustard, sugar, vinegar, and cayenne for 1 tablespoon
lemon juice. \* In step 3 once plated, sprinkle with 1/4 cup shredded
Pecorino Romano cheese.
