Jackson Potatoes
================

+-----------------------+-------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 1 hour | Yield: 2 servings |
+-----------------------+-------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 russet potatoes, washed, scrubbed
- 2 ounces bacon, diced
- 2 tablespoons onion, diced
- 1 tablespoon butter
- sea salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- 1-2 tablespoons sour cream
- 1 tablespoon flat leaf parsley, chopped
- 1 egg yolk

Directions
----------

1. Preheat oven to 400F. Put potatoes on a baking sheet and into the oven;
   cook until fork tender. Remove and let cool slightly, 5 minutes.
2. Meanwhile heat a skillet, add bacon, and saute until golden. Add onions,
   season, and saute until golden; remove any excess oil and set aside.
3. Slice the potatoes across the top about a 1/4 down and remove most of the
   centre. Place in a bowl, add onions, bacon, butter, seasoning, sour cream,
   and parsley, then mix.
4. Pipe the mixture back into the potato using a piping bag and star tip.
5. Brush the top with a beaten egg yolk.
6. Return to the oven until golden on top, 10 minutes.
