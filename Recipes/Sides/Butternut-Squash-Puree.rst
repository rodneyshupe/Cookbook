Sous Vide Butternut Squash Puree
================================

+-----------------------+----------------------------+---------------------+----------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 1 - 1 1/2 hours | Total Time: 2 hours | Yield: 8-10 servings |
+-----------------------+----------------------------+---------------------+----------------------+

Equipment
---------
Sous-Vide (immersion circulator) set to 200F

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 pounds butternut squash, peeled, seeded, and sliced 1/4 inch thick
- 1/4 cup water, plus extra as needed
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
- Salt

Directions
----------

1. Set your sous-vide to 200F (93C).
2. Put squash, water, butter, and 1/2 teaspoon salt in a 1-gallon zipper-lock
   freezer bag; seal, pressing out as much air as possible. Slip that bag into
   a second 1-gallon zipper-lock freezer bag and seal. Lower gently into the
   water bath and weight it until the squash is fully submerged, then clip the
   top corner to the side of the container so the last air bubbles rise to the
   top. Reopen 1 corner of the zipper, release the remaining bubbles, and
   reseal. Cover and cook 1 hour to 1 1/2 hours.
3. Transfer the squash and cooking liquid to a blender and process until
   smooth, about 2 minutes, scraping down the sides as needed. If it won't
   blend easily, loosen with up to 2 tablespoons extra water. Season with salt
   to taste and serve.

Note:
-----

- Be sure to double bag the squash to protect against seam failure. Squash
  has a tendency to float when placed in a sous vide water bath, which can
  lead to uneven cooking. Use weights to make sure the squash is fully
  immersed during cooking.
