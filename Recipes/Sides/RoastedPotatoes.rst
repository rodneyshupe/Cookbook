Roasted Potatoes
================

+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 1 1/4 hours | Yield: 6 servings |
+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 3 pounds White potatoes, peeled
- Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Olive oil
- 1 bulb of garlic, broken into cloves
- Red wine vinegar

Flavour Combo 1
---------------

- A good couple of lugs of olive oil
- A bunch of fresh rosemary, leaves picked

Flavour Combo 2
---------------

- 50g butter, cut into little cubes
- A bunch of fresh sage, roughly torn
- 1 clementine

Flavour Combo 3
---------------

- 2 tablespoons goose fat
- A bunch of fresh thyme, leaves picked
- A couple of fresh bay leaves

Directions
----------

1. Preheat the oven to 375F. Peel the potatoes with a knife or speed peeler
   and cut any larger ones to an even size - twice the size of a squash ball
   is about right. Wash the potatoes in cold water to remove extra starch,
   then tip into a large pot, cover with cold water and season well. Bring to
   the boil and cook for about 6 to 7 minutes, until parboiled, then drain in
   a colander and leave to steam dry for 3 minutes. Shake the colander a bit
   to chuff up the potatoes for extra crispness later.
2. Decide which flavour combo to use. Tip the potatoes into a tray or pan in
   one layer, add the fat - olive oil, butter or goose fat - then season
   really well with salt and pepper. Toss the potatoes in the fat, or use a
   spoon or fish slice to mix it all up. To make ahead, prepare up to this
   stage the day before, cover with cling film or tin foil and keep in the
   bottom of the fridge or a cool place until needed. Roast for 30 minutes
   until lightly golden and three quarters cooked.
3. Gently squash each potato with a potato masher to increase the surface
   area - the more potato in contact with the pan, the crispier it will be.
   Prepare the rest of the flavourings. Add a good lug of olive oil to a small
   bowl with the herbs, garlic and a splash of red wine vinegar, then scrunch
   and mix a bit. For butter, peel in a good few strips of clementine zest
   with a speed peeler - these are not eaten but add amazing flavour. Add
   the flavourings to the potatoes, give the pan a good shake, then roast for
   40 to 45 minutes until done to your liking - gnarly, crispy, bubbly and
   delicious.
4. Transfer to a plate lined with kitchen paper to drain off excess fat, then
   serve.
