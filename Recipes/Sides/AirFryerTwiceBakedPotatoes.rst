Air Fryer Twice Baked Potatoes
==============================

+----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+-----------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Total Time: 15 minutes | Yield: 6 halves |
+----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+-----------------+

Equipment
---------
Air Fryer

Ingredients
-----------

- 3 cooked `baked potatoes <#air-fryer-baked-potatoes>`__
- 3 tablespoons sour cream
- 2/3 cup cheddar cheese
- 1 1/2 tablespoons butter
- 3 slices `cooked bacon <#air-fryer-bacon>`__

Directions
----------

1. Cut the baked potatoes in half and scoop the insides into a bowl.
2. Add the sour cream, 1/3 cup of cheddar cheese, and the butter; mash with a
   potato masher until it reaches the consistency you want.
3. Spoon the filling back into the shells, mounding to fit as needed;
   refrigerate until ready to serve.
4. When ready, set the potatoes in the air fryer basket and cook at 400F for 8
   minutes.
5. Top with the remaining 1/3 cup of cheddar cheese and bacon bits, taking
   care not to touch the hot sides of the basket; return to the air fryer and
   cook 2 more minutes at 400F to melt the cheese and crisp the bacon.
