Com Do (Vietnamese Red Rice)
============================

+------------------------+----------------------+
| Total Time: 45 minutes | Yield: Serves 4 to 6 |
+------------------------+----------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 1/2 cups jasmine rice
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 4 garlic clove, minced
- 3 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 3/4 cups water
- 2 teaspoons maggi sauce (substitute half soy sauce and half worcestershire sauce)
- 1/2 teaspoon table salt

Directions
----------

1. Rinse rice in fine-mesh strainer under cold running water until water runs
   clear; drain well. Melt butter in medium saucepan over medium-high heat,
   then add rice and cook, stirring constantly, until grains become chalky and
   opaque, 1 to 3 minutes. Add garlic and cook, stirring constantly, about 30
   seconds. Add tomato paste and cook, stirring constantly, until evenly
   distributed, about 1 minute.
2. Add water, maggi sauce, and salt; bring to boil. Cover, reduce heat to low,
   and cook until liquid is absorbed and rice is tender, about 20 minutes. Let
   stand off heat, covered, 10 minutes. Fluff rice with fork and stir to
   combine; serve.
