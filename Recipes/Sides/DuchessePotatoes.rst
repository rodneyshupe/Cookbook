Duchesse Potatoes
=================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 35 minutes | Yield: 6 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 pounds russet potatoes, peeled
- kosher salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- 1 pinch nutmeg
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 2 egg yolks
- 1 tablespoon Butter
- 1 egg yolk, beaten

Directions
----------

1. Place potatoes in a pot of salted boiling water and cook until tender;
   drain and let sit 2 minutes to make sure all the water is drained. Place in
   a large bowl and mash, then add salt, pepper, nutmeg, cream, egg yolks, and
   butter and blend well.
2. Preheat oven to 400F. Pipe the potatoes onto a baking sheet into a crown
   shape using a piping bag with a star tube, then brush the piped potatoes
   with the beaten egg yolk.
3. Bake for approx. 15 minutes, until golden brown.
4. You can put various ingredients into the potatoes, such as herbs, bacon
   (cooked), diced ham, truffles, sour cream, sliced truffle, etc.
