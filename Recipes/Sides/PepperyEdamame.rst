Peppery Edamame
===============

+----------------------+----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Cook Time: 5 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+----------------------+----------------------+-------------------+

Source: `Bon Appetit <https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/peppery-edamame>`__

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 14-16 ounce (414-473 ml) bag frozen unshelled edamame (soybeans)
- 3 tablespoons toasted sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon sugar

Directions
----------

1. Cook edamame in a large pot of boiling water 2 minutes; drain and transfer
   to a large bowl.
2. Add oil, pepper, salt, and sugar and toss to coat.
3. Serve immediately.
