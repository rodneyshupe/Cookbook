Refried Beans
=============

+----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2-3 rashers bacon
- 1 large can pinto beans, drained and canning liquid reserved
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin
- Salt and pepper

Directions
----------

1. Render bacon in a medium skillet over medium heat; remove bacon and reserve
   for another purpose, keeping the fat in the pan.
2. Add pinto beans to the skillet, then stir in garlic powder, cumin, salt and
   pepper. Heat for 1-2 minutes, then mash with a potato masher until smooth,
   adding 1/2 cup of the canning juice if the mixture is too dry. Cook until
   they look like refried beans.

Notes
-----

Skip the bacon and use 1 tablespoon vegetable oil to make this vegan
friendly.
