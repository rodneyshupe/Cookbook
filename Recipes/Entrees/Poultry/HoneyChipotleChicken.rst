Honey Chipotle Chicken
======================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 12 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts, or chicken tenderloins
- extra virgin olive oil
- salt and pepper to taste
- garlic powder
- 2 Tablespoon chipotle powder grill seasoning, divided
  (1 tablespoon for sauce & 1 tablespoon for rubbing into chicken)
- juice of 1 lime
- 2 Tablespoon honey

Directions
----------

1. Heat oil in a large skillet over high heat.
2. Rub the chicken with olive oil, then season with salt, freshly ground black
   pepper, garlic powder, and 1 tablespoon Chipotle powder seasoning. The oil
   helps keep the juices in so the chicken stays tender and juicy.
3. Reduce heat to medium and add the chicken, working in batches so as not to
   overcrowd the skillet. Cook until done, about 5 - 6 minutes on each side.
4. While the chicken cooks, mix the lime juice, honey, and the other
   tablespoon of chipotle powder seasoning.
5. Plate the chicken and brush with the honey chipotle mixture. Serve with a
   favorite side of vegetables, over mashed potatoes, rice, or quinoa, or atop
   a big green salad.
