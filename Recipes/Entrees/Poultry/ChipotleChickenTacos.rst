Chipotle Chicken Tacos
======================

+-----------------------+----------------------+--------------------------------------+-----------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 5 minutes | Marinate Time: 10 minutes - 24 hours | Yield: 10 tacos |
+-----------------------+----------------------+--------------------------------------+-----------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 4 chicken thighs, chopped into 1/2 inch cubes
- 2 teaspoon chipotle chili powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon avocado oil (can sub vegetable oil)
- 10 small tortilla
- `avocado cream <#avocado-cream>`__
- adobo sauce (from chipotle peppers can)
- sliced cabbage (optional)
- onion, small dice
- cilantro.

Directions
----------

1. Combine the ingredients and let sit for at least 10 minutes, or refrigerate
   up to 24 hours.
2. Heat a non-stick pan over medium high heat. Add the chicken and saute until
   fully cooked, 2-5 minutes. Heat the tortilla shells.
3. Assemble the tacos: to each tortilla add avocado cream, adobo sauce,
   cabbage (if using), cooked chicken, onion (if using), and cilantro.
