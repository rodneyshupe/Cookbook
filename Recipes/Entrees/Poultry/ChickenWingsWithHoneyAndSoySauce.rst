Chicken Wings with Honey and Soy Sauce
======================================

+------------------------------+-------------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 1 hour 10 minutes | Total Time: 1 hour 30 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+------------------------------+-------------------------------+-------------------+

I like the glaze that comes from baking these wings at a nice high
temperature. As the cooking liquid from the chicken reduces with the sugars
in the honey and the umami in the soy sauce, it coats the wings in a burnt,
sweet and succulent glaze.

Ingredients
-----------

- 5 pounds (2.25 kg) chicken wings
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons white wine vinegar
- 1 tablespoon honey
- Kosher salt
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 1/2 shallot, cut into julienne
- 1/3 cup chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- Kosher salt
- Lime wedges, for serving

Directions
----------

1. Preheat the oven to 400F. Allow the wings to come to room temperature for
   15 minutes and line a sheet tray with foil.
2. Toss the wings in the olive oil, soy sauce, vinegar, honey and a healthy
   pinch of salt in a large bowl, then arrange them in a single layer on the
   prepared sheet tray (use 2 sheet trays if necessary). Bake for 30 minutes,
   then remove from the oven and carefully flip over.
3. Increase the oven temperature to 425F and cook until golden brown and the
   meat is falling off the bone with the glaze reduced to a syrup, about 30
   minutes more. Cool for 10 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, mix the yogurt, shallots, parsley, lemon juice and some salt in
   a small bowl with a rubber spatula and set aside.
5. Toss the cooled wings with the yogurt sauce in a large bowl. Serve with
   lime wedges.
