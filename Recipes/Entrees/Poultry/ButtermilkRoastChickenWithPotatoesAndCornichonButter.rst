Buttermilk Roast Chicken with Potatoes and Cornichon Butter
===========================================================

+--------------------------------------+-------------------+-----------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes + 6 - 24 hours | Cook Time: 1 hour | Yield: 3 - 4 servings |
+--------------------------------------+-------------------+-----------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 whole chicken, about 4 pounds (1.8 kg)
- 1 cup buttermilk (substitute 1 1/2 teaspoons of cream of tartar and 1 cup regular milk)
- 1 cup pickle juice, from any type of pickles (dill, bread and butter, spicy garlic, etc.)
- 1 pinch kosher salt, to taste
- 1 1/2 pounds (680g) baby potatoes, halved, or small golden potatoes, peeled and sliced 1/4-inch thick (up to 2 pounds of potatoes)
- 1 splash olive oil
- 1 tablespoon butter, softened
- 4 cornichons, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped flat-leaf parsley

Directions
----------

1. Mix together buttermilk, pickle juice, and 1 teaspoon kosher salt. Place
   chicken in a gallon-sized plastic zipper-lock bag, pour in the brine, and
   seal, pressing out as much air as possible. Refrigerate 6 to 24 hours; a
   longer brine yields a more flavourful bird.
2. About an hour before roasting, remove chicken from the bag and discard the
   brine. Pat dry with paper towels. Rub a little olive oil on the bottom of a
   large baking dish or ovenproof skillet, one large enough to hold the
   potatoes. Set the chicken breast-side up in the middle and let it come to
   room temperature.
3. Heat oven to 425F. Toss potatoes with 1 tablespoon olive oil and kosher
   salt, to taste, and arrange them around the chicken. Rub the chicken evenly
   with 1/2 to 1 tablespoon olive oil, then season chicken and potatoes with
   freshly cracked black pepper.
4. Roast until the juices run clear or the thickest part of the thigh
   registers 165F, about 50 to 70 minutes, rotating once. If the chicken
   browns too quickly relative to its internal temperature, reduce the oven to
   375F to 400F.
5. Meanwhile, mash together the butter and cornichons with a fork; don't worry
   if they aren't completely integrated.
6. Remove the chicken from the pan as soon as it's done and rest on a cutting
   board for 10 to 15 minutes before carving. Add the cornichon butter and
   parsley to the hot potatoes and, once it starts to melt, toss to integrate
   evenly.
