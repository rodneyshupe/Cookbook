Greek Chicken Meatballs
=======================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+----------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 4 |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+----------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1/4 cup panko
- 1 egg
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- pinch of pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/4 teaspoon chili flakes
- 1 pound (450 g) ground chicken or turkey
- 2 tablespoons dill, chopped
- 2 tablespoons mint, chopped
- 1 lemon
- 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup (plus more to drizzle) olive oil
- 1 1/2 cups orzo
- Greek Salad
- `Mayo Yogurt Sauce <#mayo-yogurt-sauce>`__ or `Tzatziki Sauce <#tzatziki-sauce>`__

Directions
----------

1. Heat the oven to 400F and line a sheet pan with parchment paper or lightly
   grease with non-stick cooking spray.
2. In a medium bowl, mix the panko and egg. Add the salt, pepper, cumin,
   oregano, and chili flakes and mix. Add the chicken, dill, mint, garlic, and
   zest from 1/2 the lemon, then gently toss without overworking.
3. Scoop the mixture onto the sheet pan using a 2 tablespoon scoop, drizzle
   with olive oil, and bake until set, 15-18 minutes, then broil for 2
   minutes.
4. While the meatballs cook, boil the orzo until tender, then drain and toss
   with the zest from the other half of the lemon and the juice from 1/2 the
   lemon.
5. Serve the meatballs with the orzo, Greek salad, and some sauce.
