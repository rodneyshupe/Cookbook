Greek Chicken
=============

+-----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Marinate Time: 2 hours | Yield: 2 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

Marinade
^^^^^^^^

- 3/4 cup olive oil
- 2 tablespoons fish sauce (or the same of anchovy paste)
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 2 tablespoons garlic paste, or 2 large cloves, minced
- 2 teaspoons dried thyme
- 2 teaspoons dried rosemary
- 1/2 teaspoon each Kosher salt and fresh ground pepper
- Juice of 2 lemons
- 2 chicken breasts

Toppings
^^^^^^^^

- `Mayo Yogurt Sauce <#mayo-yogurt-sauce>`__
- 1/4 Red Onion, diced
- 1 tomato, cored, seeded, and diced
- 1 cup Cucumber, diced
- `Naan <#simple-naan-bread>`__, for serving

Directions
----------

1. Combine all ingredients except the chicken in a measuring cup or deli
   container, shaking or mixing well, and set aside.
2. Flatten the chicken breasts to an even thickness and place in a ziplock bag
   with about 3/4 of the marinade. Mush around to coat the chicken well, seal
   the bag, and refrigerate a couple hours.
3. Remove from the fridge at least 30 minutes before cooking.
4. Grill on a very hot grill, turning often and brushing with the reserved
   marinade once grilled on both sides, until the chicken hits 165F. Remove,
   brush again, and use.

Notes
-----

Substitute fresh Naan with Pita.  Warm pita by wrapping in foil and place in
a hot oven.
