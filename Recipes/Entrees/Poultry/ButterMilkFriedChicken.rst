Butter Milk Fried Chicken
=========================

+----------------------+--------------------------+-----------------------+------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Marinate Time: 12+ hours | Cook Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 3-4 |
+----------------------+--------------------------+-----------------------+------------+

Ingredients:
------------

- 1 broiler/fryer chicken, cut into 8 pieces
- 2 cups low fat buttermilk
- Vegetable shortening, for frying
- 2 tablespoons kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons Hungarian paprika
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- Flour, for dredging

Directions
----------

1. Place the chicken pieces into a plastic container, cover with buttermilk,
   then cover and refrigerate for 12 to 24 hours.
2. Melt enough shortening over low heat to come just 1/3-inch up the side of a
   12-inch cast iron skillet or heavy fry pan. Once the shortening liquefies,
   raise the heat to 325F and do not allow the oil to go over 325F.
3. Drain the chicken in a colander. Combine the salt, paprika, garlic powder,
   and cayenne pepper, then season the chicken liberally with the mixture.
   Dredge the chicken in flour and shake off the excess.
4. Place the chicken skin side down into the pan, putting the thighs in the
   centre and the breast and legs around the edge; the oil should come half
   way up the pan. Cook until golden brown on each side and the internal
   temperature is right around 180F, approximately 10 to 12 minutes per side,
   monitoring the shortening temperature every few minutes.
5. Drain the chicken on a rack over a sheet pan rather than directly on paper
   towels or brown paper bags. If holding before serving, cover loosely with
   foil but avoid a warm oven, especially a gas oven. Serve with homemade `French Fries <#french-fries>`__.
