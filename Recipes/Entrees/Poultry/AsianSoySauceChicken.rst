Asian Soy Sauce Chicken
=======================

+----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Equipment
---------
Air Fryer

Ingredients
-----------

- 4 chicken thighs (bone in, skin on)
- 2 tablespoons of regular soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons of dark soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon five spice
- 2 teaspoon sugar
- 2 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
- 1 green onions sliced thin, chopped

Directions
----------

1. Debone the chicken thighs, then pat them dry. Combine the chicken in a bowl
   with the soy sauce, five spice, sugar, minced garlic and ginger, mixing
   well. Let sit for at least 30 minutes, making rice in the meantime.
2. Spray the air fryer container with oil and place the chicken thighs skin
   side up. Air fry at 380F for 16 minutes.
3. Serve with rice and chopped green onion.
