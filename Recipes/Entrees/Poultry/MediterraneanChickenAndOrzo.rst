One Pot Mediterranean Chicken and Orzo
======================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 40 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 pounds (908g) boneless skinless chicken thighs
- salt and pepper to taste
- 4 tablespoons (60ml) extra virgin olive oil
- 1 large onion diced
- 6 cloves garlic minced
- 1 teaspoon crushed hot red pepper flakes
- 1 1/2 pounds (680g) cherry tomatoes halved
- 1 pound (454g) orzo
- 3 1/2 cups (840ml) low-sodium chicken stock
- 1 tablespoon (22g) honey
- 1 tablespoon dried greek oregano
- 1/2 cup (70g) kalamata or gaeta olives pitted, rinsed, and chopped
- 2 tablespoons (30ml) lemon juice
- 1/2 cup minced flat-leaf Italian parsley
- 1/2 cup (57g) crumbled feta

Directions
----------

1. Pat the chicken thighs very dry with paper towels and season both sides
   with salt and pepper.
2. Heat a large pan to medium heat and add the olive oil. Once shimmering, add
   the chicken thighs and cook until golden brown, 4-5 minutes per side.
   Transfer to a plate and tent with foil.
3. Add the onion to the pan with a pinch of salt and cook until soft and
   translucent, about 5-7 minutes. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant,
   another 1-2 minutes. Add the hot red pepper flakes and tomatoes and cook
   until the tomatoes begin to burst, 2 minutes.
4. Add the orzo and mix well to coat with the olive oil, then toast for 3
   minutes. Add the chicken stock, honey, and oregano and bring to a boil.
   Turn the heat down to low, nestle the chicken pieces into the orzo, cover,
   and cook until the orzo is tender and most of the liquid has absorbed,
   about 12-15 minutes.
5. Uncover and mix in the olives and lemon juice. Remove the pan from the heat
   and add the parsley and feta. If the orzo is too dry, add a touch more
   chicken stock and olive oil. Taste and adjust salt and pepper if required.
   Enjoy!
