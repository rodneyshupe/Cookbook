Greek Lemon Chicken and Potatoes
================================

+-----------------------+-------------------+------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 1 hour | Marinate Time: 2 hours | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-------------------+------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

Marinade
^^^^^^^^

- 8 cloves garlic
- 3/4 cup (180ml) olive oil
- 1/2 cup (120ml) lemon juice
- 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 tablespoon Greek dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 2 teaspoons fine sea salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper

Chicken and Potatoes
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- 8 large chicken thighs, overlapping skin trimmed
- 5 medium Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled, cut into large chunks, and soaked in water then dried
- 1 sprig fresh oregano, optional, for garnish
- 5 slices lemon, optional, for garnish

Directions
----------

1. Blend the marinade ingredients until smooth. Place the chicken with all of
   the marinade in a sealable bag and refrigerate for 2 hours.
2. Preheat the oven to 425F and set the rack to the middle level.
3. Add the potatoes to a baking dish and pour half of the used marinade over
   them. Coat the potatoes thoroughly with your hands, then spread them into
   an even layer.
4. Place the chicken thighs on top of the potatoes, skin side up, and drizzle
   with the remaining marinade. Bake for 10 minutes, then turn the heat down
   to 375F and continue until the chicken reaches at least 185F internal temp
   on a thermometer, another 45-55 minutes. *Note: If the potatoes are still a
   bit hard, remove the chicken pieces to a plate and tent with foil. Raise
   the oven temp to 425F and return the potatoes to the oven, cooking until
   tender and brown.*
5. Let the chicken sit lightly tented so the chicken and potatoes reabsorb the
   juices, 10-15 minutes, before serving. Serve with a garnish of fresh
   oregano and lemon wedges.

Notes
-----

- Use two baking dishes if you cannot fit everything in one. Avoid overcrowding
  to more effectively roast the chicken.
- If needed, broil the chicken during the last few minutes to crisp the skin.
