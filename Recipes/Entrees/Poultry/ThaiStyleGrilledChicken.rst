Thai-Style Grilled Chicken (Gai Yang)
=====================================

+-----------------------+---------------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Marinate Time: 6-24 hours | Cook Time: 45 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+---------------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

Hens
^^^^

- 4 Cornish game hens, 1 1/4 to 1 1/2 pounds (570g to 680g) each, giblets discarded
- 1 cup fresh cilantro leaves and stems, chopped coarse
- 12 garlic cloves, peeled
- 1/4 cup packed light brown sugar
- 2 teaspoons ground white pepper
- 2 teaspoons ground coriander
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1/4 cup fish sauce

Dipping Sauce
^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- 1/2 cup distilled white vinegar
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon minced Thai chiles
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

Directions
----------

1. Working with 1 hen at a time, place hens breast side down on a cutting
   board and use kitchen shears to cut through the bones on either side of the
   backbones; discard the backbones. Flip the hens and press on the
   breastbones to flatten, then trim any excess fat and skin.
2. Pulse the cilantro leaves and stems, garlic, sugar, pepper, coriander, and
   salt in a food processor until finely chopped, 10 to 15 pulses, and
   transfer to a small bowl. Add the fish sauce and stir until the marinade
   has the consistency of a loose paste.
3. Rub the hens all over with the marinade, then transfer the hens and any
   excess marinade to a 1-gallon zipper-lock bag and refrigerate for at least
   6 hours or up to 24 hours, flipping the bag halfway through marinating.
4. For the dipping sauce, bring the vinegar to a boil in a small saucepan, add
   the sugar, and stir to dissolve. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer
   until the vinegar mixture is slightly thickened, 5 minutes, then remove
   from the heat and let cool completely. Stir in the chiles, garlic, and salt
   until combined, then transfer the sauce to an airtight container and
   refrigerate until ready to use. (The sauce can be refrigerated for up to 2
   weeks; bring to room temperature before serving.)
5. Turn all burners to high, cover, and heat the grill until hot, about 15
   minutes. Leave the primary burner on high and turn off the other burner(s),
   adjusting the primary burner (or, if using a three-burner grill, the
   primary burner and second burner) as needed to maintain a grill temperature
   between 400 and 450F.
6. Clean and oil the cooking grate. Remove the hens from the bag, leaving any
   marinade that sticks to them in place, tuck the wingtips behind the backs,
   and turn the legs so the drumsticks face inward toward the breasts. Place
   the hens skin side up on the cooler side of the grill (if using charcoal,
   arrange them so the legs and thighs face the coals), cover, and cook until
   the skin is browned and the breasts register 145 to 150F, 30 to 35 minutes,
   rotating the hens halfway through cooking.
7. Using tongs, carefully flip the hens skin side down and move them to the
   hotter side of the grill. Cover and cook until the skin is crisp, deeply
   browned, and charred in spots and the breasts register 160F, 3 to 5
   minutes, being careful to avoid burning.
8. Transfer the hens skin side up to a cutting board, tent with aluminum foil,
   and let rest for 10 minutes. Slice each hen in half or into 4 pieces and
   serve, passing the dipping sauce separately.
