Barbecued Chicken Balls
=======================

This is a great chicken recipe that creates the most tender and
flavourful pieces of chicken.

+----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Yield: 2 servings |
+----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Equipment
---------

Disposable muffin tin

Ingredients
-----------

- 6 chicken thighs
- 1/4 cup `Basic Barbecue Rub <#basic-barbecue-rub>`__
- 6 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup `Kansas City Style Sweet and Smoky Barbecue Sauce <#kansas-city-style-sweet-and-smoky-barbecue-sauce>`__

Directions
----------

1. Season both sides of the chicken thighs generously with Rub. Set aside for
   15 minutes.
2. Add 1 tablespoon of butter to each cup of a disposable muffin tin. Roll
   each thigh into a ball and place on top of the butter in each cup.
3. Place on the grill over indirect heat with some (preferably apple) wood
   chips in a foil pack. Cook, basting with melted butter every 3 minutes, for
   approximately 12 minutes.
4. Remove from the muffin tin and place the thighs over high heat for 1 minute
   per side. Toss with 1 cup sauce and season with salt and pepper. Serve,
   passing remaining sauce separately.
5. Move back to indirect heat and baste with barbecue sauce. Cook until the
   sauce begins to caramelise, approximately 2 minutes per side.
