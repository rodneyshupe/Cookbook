Chicken Curry
=============  

+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cooking Time: 20 mins | Yield: 4 servings   |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 1/2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, minced
- 2 tablespoons curry powder
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro and lime wedges for garnish

Directions
----------

1. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season chicken with 
   salt and pepper and brown on all sides, about 6 minutes. Remove chicken 
   from pan and set aside.
2. Reduce heat to medium. Add onion to the same skillet and cook until 
   softened, about 5 minutes. Stir in garlic and ginger and cook for 1 
   minute. 
3. Stir in curry powder and turmeric, cooking for 30 seconds until 
   fragrant. Add coconut milk and diced tomatoes, scraping up any 
   browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
4. Return chicken to the skillet. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook 
   uncovered for 10-12 minutes until the sauce thickens and chicken is 
   cooked through.
5. Stir in frozen peas and cook for 2 minutes. Garnish with cilantro and 
   a squeeze of lime. Serve over rice or with flatbread.

Note
----

For a creamier, thicker sauce, use full-fat coconut milk. If you prefer 
extra heat, add 1/4 teaspoon of cayenne pepper or a chopped serrano 
chili during step 2.
