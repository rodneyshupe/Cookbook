Chicken Curry
=============

+-----------------------+--------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 20 mins | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+--------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 1/2 pounds (680 g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, minced
- 2 tablespoons curry powder
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 can (13.5 fluid ounces / 400 ml) coconut milk
- 1 can (13.5 fluid ounces / 400 ml) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup frozen peas
- Fresh cilantro and lime wedges for garnish

Directions
----------

1. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season chicken with salt
   and pepper, then brown on all sides until golden, about 6 minutes. Remove
   chicken from pan and set aside.
2. Reduce heat to medium. Add onion to the same skillet and cook until
   softened, about 5 minutes. Stir in garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute.
3. Stir in curry powder and turmeric and cook until fragrant, 30 seconds. Add
   coconut milk and diced tomatoes, scraping up any browned bits from the
   bottom of the pan.
4. Return chicken to the skillet. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered
   until the sauce thickens and chicken is cooked through, 10-12 minutes.
5. Stir in frozen peas and cook for 2 minutes. Garnish with cilantro and a
   squeeze of lime. Serve over rice or with flatbread.

Notes
-----

For a creamier, thicker sauce, use full-fat coconut milk. If you prefer
extra heat, add 1/4 teaspoon of cayenne pepper or a chopped serrano
chili during step 2.
