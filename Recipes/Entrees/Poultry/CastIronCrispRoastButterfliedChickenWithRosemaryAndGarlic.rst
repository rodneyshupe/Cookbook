Cast Iron Crisp Roast Butterflied Chicken with Rosemary and Garlic
==================================================================

+-----------------------+-------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 1 hour | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 teaspoon minced fresh rosemary
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 3 1/2 to 4 pound (1.6kg to 1.8kg) whole chicken, giblets discarded
- Salt and pepper

Directions
----------

1. Adjust oven rack to lowest position, place 12-inch cast-iron skillet on
   rack, and heat oven to 500F. Combine 1 tablespoon oil, rosemary, and garlic
   in a bowl and set aside.
2. With chicken breast side down, cut through the bones on either side of the
   backbone with kitchen shears and discard the backbone. Flip the chicken
   over, tuck the wingtips behind the back, and press firmly on the breastbone
   to flatten. Pat dry with paper towels, then rub with the remaining 1
   tablespoon oil and season with salt and pepper.
3. When the oven reaches 500F, place the chicken breast side down in the hot
   skillet. Reduce oven temperature to 450F and roast until well browned,
   about 30 minutes.
4. Remove the skillet from the oven with potholders and, being careful of the
   hot handle, gently flip the chicken breast side up. Brush with the oil
   mixture, return the skillet to the oven, and continue roasting until the
   breast registers 160F and the thighs register 175F, about 10 minutes.
   Transfer to a carving board, tent loosely with aluminum foil, and let rest
   15 minutes. Carve and serve.
