Dave's BBQ Chicken Wings
========================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 50 minutes | Total Time: 3-4 hours | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 6 tablespoons table salt (or 12 tablespoons kosher salt)
- 6 tablespoons sugar
- 12 whole chicken wings
- `Basic Barbecue Rub <#basic-barbecue-rub>`__
- `Cajun Spice Mix <#louisiana-cajun-or-creole-seasoning-spice-mix>`__
- Bacon Fat

Directions
----------

1. Dissolve sugar and salt in 4 cups of water in a one gallon-size zip lock
   freezer bag. Add chicken, press out as much air as possible, and seal.
   Refrigerate 30 minutes.
2. Remove chicken from brine and pat dry with paper towel. Apply equal amounts
   of Basic Barbecue Rub and Cajun Spice Mix, or to taste, then lay out on a
   cookie sheet with a wire rack so they are not overlapping. Refrigerate to
   dry, at least 3-4 hours.
3. Get the smoke going (or not) and put the wings on one side of the grill,
   that side on the lowest setting, the smoking side low to medium heat. Flip
   wings after 15 minutes, or more frequently if the heat is high, to get them
   smokey and dry. After about 30 minutes baste with melted bacon fat, flip,
   and baste again, a couple of times if you like. There should be no flare
   ups, or little on the wing side, but the low heat applies extra cooking and
   drying and prevents the worst of the fat build up.
4. Keep smoking and cooking until the skin is getting crispy and greyish from
   the spices and smoke. Raise the heat and switch them to the smoking side to
   crisp up more; if you do it on the wings side get ready for lots of flames.
   When done to your liking take them off and pour on as much room temperature
   BBQ sauce as you like; we coated about half of each wing, drizzling with a
   table spoon. Don't cook them too long, 40-50 minutes total time from when
   they went on the grill.

Variation
---------

Alternate brine: Substitute sugar with 1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce.
