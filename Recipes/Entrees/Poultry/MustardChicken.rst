Mustard Chicken
===============

+-----------------------+-----------------------+--------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 30 minutes | Marinate Time: overnight | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+--------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1/4 cup yellow mustard
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon chili flakes
- 4 chicken legs (skin on), split into thighs and drumsticks
- salt and pepper

Directions
----------

1. Mix the mustard, garlic, paprika, and chili flakes in a small bowl.
2. Season the chicken legs with salt and pepper, then rub with the mustard
   mixture and marinate for several hours, preferably overnight.
3. Preheat the oven to 400F (convection). An air fryer works well here.
4. Cook the chicken until it registers 165F, about 30 minutes.
