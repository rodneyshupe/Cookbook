Chicken Pot Pie
===============

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 25 minutes | Cook Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

Chicken and Gravy
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- 5 chicken thighs
- Salt and Pepper
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons vermouth (could substitute sherry)
- 1 1/4 cup chicken stock
- 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon dried sage
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce

Pot Pie
^^^^^^^

- 3-4 rashers thick cut bacon, diced
- 1 leek, light part only, cleaned and chopped (can substitute 1 small yellow onion diced)
- 2 carrots, peeled and chopped
- 8 ounces cremini mushrooms, quartered
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 sheet pie crust dough

Directions
----------

1. For the chicken: Season the chicken thighs on each side with salt, pepper,
   1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika, and 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder. Heat a large
   skillet over medium high heat, add 1 tablespoon oil, then the chicken. Cook
   until almost done, flipping halfway; it does not need to be fully cooked,
   as it finishes in the oven. Remove from the pan and set aside.
2. For the gravy: Add a tablespoon of oil and the garlic to the empty pan and
   cook until fragrant, 30 seconds. Add the vermouth and reduce by 1/2, then
   add 1 cup chicken broth. Combine the remaining 1/4 cup chicken broth and
   cornstarch in a separate bowl. Stir half the slurry into the gravy, adding
   more until it reaches the desired thickness.
3. For the pot pie: Preheat the oven to 425. Heat a pan over medium heat and
   cook the bacon until just getting crispy. Remove from the pan and drain on
   paper towels. Keep 1 tablespoon of the bacon grease in the pan, add the
   leek, carrots, and mushrooms, and cook, stirring, until the vegetables are
   softened.
4. Add the chicken, sour cream, and gravy and mix well. Pour everything into a
   9 x 9 inch baking dish and cover with the pie crust. Poke 6 small slits
   into the crust and bake until golden brown, about 30 minutes.

Variations
----------

Leftover Chicken and Gravy
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Skip steps 1 and 2, using 3 cups leftover cubed or shredded chicken and 1 cup
gravy. Add 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce and 1 teaspoon dried thyme to the
gravy, then continue the recipe at step 3.
