Pan Seared Striploin Steaks with Sauted Mushrooms
=================================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Yield: 2 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 8 ounces mixed mushrooms, cut into large chunks (a good choice are crimini)
- 1 shallot, minced
- 1 tablespoon butter
- small handful of chives, sliced
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil
- 2 Striploin Steaks, approx. 8 ounces (225 g) each
- 2-4 tablespoons vermouth

Directions
----------

1. Get a pan on very high heat and add the oil. Once it starts to smoke, add
   the mushrooms and toss until golden brown, taking care not to crowd the
   pan. Remove the mushrooms to a bowl, stir in the butter, chopped shallot,
   and chives, and set aside to cool.
2. Preheat the oven to 200F. Line a sheet pan and set a rack inside it.
3. Pour off all but 1-2 tablespoons of oil from the pan and reheat until
   smoking hot. Add the steaks and sear until browned on both sides, about
   3 to 5 minutes.
4. Move the steaks to the prepared pan and roast in the oven, about
   10 minutes. While they roast, deglaze the pan with the vermouth and
   reserved jus.
5. Serve the steaks with the mushrooms and jus spooned on top.
