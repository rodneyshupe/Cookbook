Double-Decker Ground Beef Tacos
===============================

+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Total Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+

Scratch ground beef tacos built double-decker style. A homemade spice
blend and beef broth make a thick, glossy sauce that clings to the meat.
The beef goes into a crispy hard shell wrapped in a soft flour tortilla,
with warm refried beans as the glue that keeps everything from shattering.

Ingredients
-----------

Taco Spice Blend
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- 1 tablespoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper *(optional, for heat)*

Taco Assembly
^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- 1 pound (450 g) lean ground beef *(80/20 or 85/15)*
- 1 tablespoon olive oil or vegetable oil
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- 2/3 cup low-sodium beef broth *(or water)*
- 8-10 crispy taco shells
- 8-10 small soft flour tortillas *(street taco size)*
- 1 can *(15 ounces)* (425 ml) refried beans

Toppings *(Optional)*
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- Sharp cheddar or Monterey Jack cheese, freshly grated
- Finely shredded iceberg lettuce
- Roma tomatoes, finely diced
- Sour cream or Mexican crema

Directions
----------

1. FOR THE SPICE BLEND: Combine the chili powder, ground cumin, garlic powder,
   onion powder, smoked paprika, dried oregano, kosher salt, black pepper, and
   optional cayenne in a small bowl. Set aside.
2. FOR THE TACO MEAT: Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
   Add the ground beef and break it up with a spoon or spatula. Cook until
   completely browned with no pink remaining, about 5 to 7 minutes. If there's
   excessive fat, spoon most of it out, leaving roughly 1 tablespoon in the
   pan.
3. FOR THE TACO MEAT: Reduce the heat to medium. Sprinkle the spice blend and
   the **1 tablespoon of flour** evenly over the meat. Stir vigorously until
   the beef is coated and the spices smell fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes.
4. FOR THE TACO MEAT: Pour in the **2/3 cup of beef broth**. Bring to a
   simmer, stirring frequently. Cook until the liquid combines with the flour
   and reduces into a thick, glossy sauce that coats the beef, 3 to 5 minutes.
   Remove from heat.
5. FOR THE BEAN GLUE: Empty the refried beans into a small saucepan over
   medium-low heat. Stir in a splash of water, a tiny pinch of cumin, or a
   spoonful of the taco meat sauce. Warm through, stirring often, until smooth
   and easily spreadable.
6. FOR THE SHELLS: Bake the hard taco shells at ``350F`` until crisp, 5
   minutes. Separately, warm the soft flour tortillas in a dry skillet for 15
   to 20 seconds per side, or stack them in a damp paper towel and microwave
   until pliable, 20 seconds.
7. FOR ASSEMBLY: Lay a warmed soft tortilla flat on a plate. Spread **1 to 2
   tablespoons** of the warm refried beans evenly across the centre, leaving a
   small clean border around the edge. Set a baked hard shell in the centre of
   the beans, then bring the sides of the soft tortilla up, pressing gently to
   bond them to the hard shell.
8. FILL AND SERVE: Spoon the warm seasoned ground beef into the hard shell
   centre of the double-decker structure. Top immediately with grated cheese,
   shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, and sour cream as desired.
