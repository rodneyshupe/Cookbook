French Onion Pot Roast
======================

+-----------------------+--------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 4 hours | Yield: 6 servings |
+-----------------------+--------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1.5kg chuck roast
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 8 yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1 1/2 teaspoons brown sugar
- 1/4 cup brandy
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- 2 1/2 to 3 cups low sodium beef broth
- 4 thyme sprigs
- 2 bay leaves

Directions
----------

1. Heat your oven to 300F.
2. Set a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Pat the outside of the roast dry
   with a paper towel and season well with salt and pepper. Add the oil and
   sear the roast until golden brown, about three to five minutes per side.
   Move the roast to a rimmed baking sheet or large bowl to catch the juices
   and set aside.
3. Turn the heat to low, add the butter and onions, and season with salt and
   pepper. Cook until the onions are golden, 20 to 25 minutes, stirring
   occasionally. Stir in the garlic and sugar and bump the heat to medium.
   Cook until deeply golden brown and well caramelised, five to 10 minutes,
   stirring frequently.
4. Deglaze with the brandy and let it cook away, one to two minutes. Sprinkle
   the flour over the onions and stir well to combine. Stirring, slowly pour
   in two and a half cups of beef broth. Nestle the roast back in and, if
   needed, add more broth halfway up the roast. Stir in the thyme and bay
   leaves, turn the heat to medium-high, and bring to a simmer.
5. Lid on, then move the roast to the oven until fork-tender, three to three
   hours. When tender, pull it from the oven, leave the lid on, and set aside
   to rest while you make the potatoes.
