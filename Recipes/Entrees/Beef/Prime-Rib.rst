Sous Vide Eye of Prime Rib
==========================

+--------------------------+------------------------------------------------+----------+-------------+
| Prep Time: 1 to 12 hours | Cook Time: 6 hours + 30 minutes finishing time | Yield: 6 | Temp: 132F  |
+--------------------------+------------------------------------------------+----------+-------------+

Equipment
---------
Sous-Vide (immersion circulator)

Ingredients
-----------

- 3-pound (1.35 kg) bone-in beef ribeye roast
- Kosher salt
- 1 tablespoon black peppercorns, coarsely ground
- 1 tablespoon green peppercorns, coarsely ground
- 1 tablespoon pink peppercorns, coarsely ground
- 1 tablespoon dried celery seeds
- 2 tablespoons dried garlic powder
- 4 sprigs fresh rosemary, finely minced
- 2 egg whites
- 1 quart beef stock (low-sodium)

Directions
----------

1. Season the beef generously with kosher salt and refrigerate at least one
   hour, up to 12 hours.
2. Set the sous vide to 132F (55.5C). Seal the beef in a large Ziplock or
   vacuum bag, lower into the water bath, and set the timer for six hours.
3. Preheat the oven to 425F / 218C. Pull the beef from the bag, reserving the
   cooking liquid, and pat dry. Whisk together the peppercorns, celery seeds,
   garlic powder, and rosemary; brush the top and sides of the roast with egg
   white, season with salt, and coat with the spice mixture. Roast on a sheet
   pan fitted with a baking rack until the crust is dark golden brown, 10 to 15
   minutes, then rest the roast 10 to 15 minutes before carving.
4. While the roast rests, bring the sous vide cooking liquid and beef stock to
   a boil in a large saucepan, then reduce the heat and simmer until reduced by
   half. Skim off and discard any solids that float to the top.
5. Carve the roast, trimming off the rib bones and slicing into six equal
   portions. Serve with beef jus.

Notes
-----

Make this a meal by serving with sauteed chanterelle mushrooms,
`Pop Overs <#pop-overs>`__ or
`Sous Vide Garlic Smashed Potatoes <#garlic-smashed-potatoes>`__ and
`Sous Vide Glazed Carrots <#glazed-carrots>`__
