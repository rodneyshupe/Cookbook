Negimaki (Japanese Grilled Steak and Green Onion Rolls)
=======================================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+-----------------------------------------------+
| Prep Time: 45 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Total Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 4 as a main dish, or 8 as an appetizer |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+------------------------+-----------------------------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 pound (900g) flank steak, trimmed
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 3 tablespoons mirin
- 3 tablespoons sake (or substitute chinese rice wine)
- 16 green onions, trimmed and halved crosswise
- 1 tablespoon sesame seeds, toasted

Directions
----------

1. Place the steak on a large plate and freeze until firm, about 30 minutes.
2. Bring the soy sauce, sugar, mirin, and sake to a simmer in a small
   saucepan over high heat, stirring to dissolve the sugar. Reduce heat to
   medium and cook until slightly syrupy and reduced to 1/2 cup, 3 to 5
   minutes. Divide evenly between 2 bowls and let cool. Cover 1 bowl with
   plastic wrap and set aside for serving.
3. Place the steak on a cutting board. Starting at the narrow, tapered end,
   slice the steak 3/8-inch thick on the bias against the grain until the
   width is 7 inches (depending on the size of the steak, you will need to
   remove 2 to 3 slices until it measures 7 inches across). Cut the steak in
   half lengthwise. Continue to slice each half on the bias against the
   grain. You should have at least 24 slices. Pound each slice to 3/16-inch
   thickness between 2 sheets of plastic.
4. Arrange 3 slices on a cutting board with the short side facing you,
   overlapping by 1/4 inch and alternating the tapered ends as needed, to
   form a rough rectangle that measures 4 to 6 inches wide and at least 4
   inches long. Place 4 scallion halves along the edge nearest you, with the
   white tips slightly hanging over the edges of the steak on either side.
   Starting from the bottom edge and rolling away from you, roll into a
   tight cylinder. Insert 3 equally spaced toothpicks into the end flaps and
   through the centre of the roll. Transfer to a platter and repeat with the
   remaining steak and scallions. (Assembled rolls can be refrigerated for
   up to 24 hours.)
5. For a gas grill, turn all burners to high, cover, and heat until hot,
   about 15 minutes, then leave all burners on high. Clean and oil the
   cooking grate. Place the rolls on the grill (over coals if using
   charcoal) and cook until the first side is beginning to char, 4 to 6
   minutes. Flip the rolls, brush the cooked side with glaze, and cook until
   the second side is beginning to char, 4 to 6 minutes. Cook the remaining
   2 sides, glazing after each turn, until all 4 sides are evenly charred and
   a thermometer inserted from the end of a roll into the scallions at the
   core registers 150 to 155F, 16 to 24 minutes total. Transfer the rolls to
   a cutting board, tent with aluminum foil, and let rest for 5 minutes.
   Discard the remaining glaze.
6. Remove the toothpicks and cut the rolls crosswise into 3/4-inch-long
   pieces. Arrange cut side down on a clean platter, drizzle with 2
   tablespoons reserved glaze, sprinkle with the sesame seeds, and serve,
   passing the remaining reserved glaze separately.
