Beef Birria Tacos
=================

+-----------------------+--------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 30 minutes | Cook Time: 3 hours | Yield: 8-10 tacos |
+-----------------------+--------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 4 dried guajillo chiles
- 2 dried pasilla chiles
- 2 cups boiling beef broth
- 3 pounds (1.35 kg) boneless short rib
- Kosher salt and pepper
- 1 yellow onion
- 3 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon oregano
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 2 teaspoons coriander
- 2 chipotle chiles in adobo sauce
- 13.5 fluid ounces (400 ml) can diced fire roasted tomatoes
- Corn tortillas
- Monterey Jack cheese, shredded
- Diced white onion
- Chopped cilantro

Directions
----------

1. Preheat oven to 350. Remove stems and seeds from chiles, put into a
   blender, add the boiling broth and cover.
2. Meanwhile, season the beef well with salt and pepper. Sear in 2 batches in
   a hot ovenproof pot, remove to a plate and repeat with remaining beef. Add
   the onions to the empty pot with a little more oil and cook until softened,
   about 5 minutes. Add the vinegar, scraping up the bits on the bottom, and
   let about 1/2 of the vinegar evaporate. Add garlic and, when fragrant,
   about 45 seconds later, add the beef back in and take off the heat.
3. Add the oregano, cumin, coriander, chipotles, salt and pepper and diced
   tomatoes to the softening chiles. Blend until very smooth and pour over
   beef in pot. The beef should be covered; if not, add a little more broth.
   Cover and place in the oven until fork tender and shreddable, about 2 to
   2.5 hours.
4. For tacos, dip a tortilla in some of the cooking liquid and place on the
   flattop. When it starts getting a little brown, add some beef and shredded
   cheese, fold over and cook until crispy on both sides. Remove, open up
   carefully and add onion and cilantro, and eat.
