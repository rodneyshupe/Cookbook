Beef Rouladen
=============

+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 1 1/2 hours | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 6-8 slices of bacon, chopped fine
- 1 onion, chopped fine
- 2-3 pickles diced (about 5 tablespoons) and 2 tablespoons pickle juice
- 6 pieces Rouladen inside round steak (about 500g)
- 4 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- Salt and pepper
- 2 cups beef broth
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour

Directions
----------

1. Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 325F. Cook bacon in a
   cast iron skillet over medium heat until crisp, 6 to 8 minutes. Transfer
   bacon to a medium bowl and pour off the fat from the pan, reserving 1/4 cup.
   Cook onion in 1 tablespoon bacon fat until browned, about 5 minutes.
   Transfer onion and pickles to the bowl with the bacon.
2. Pat steak dry with paper towels. Brush 1 side of each piece of meat with
   1/2 tablespoon of mustard, then season with salt and pepper. Add 1/6 of the
   bacon mixture to one end of each piece. Roll steak into a tight cylinder and
   tie at 1-inch intervals with kitchen twine.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon reserved fat in the now-empty skillet over medium-high
   heat until just smoking. Brown the rolls on all sides, 5 to 8 minutes. Pour
   broth into the pan, cover the dish tightly, and roast 45 minutes.
4. Transfer rolls to a plate and reserve the pan juices. Heat the remaining
   2 tablespoons fat in the now-empty skillet over medium heat until
   shimmering. Stir in flour and cook until golden and fragrant, about
   2 minutes. Whisk in the pan juices and bring to a boil. Simmer over
   medium-low heat until the sauce is slightly thickened, about 10 minutes.
   Off heat, stir in the remaining 1 tablespoon mustard and the pickle juice.
   Season with pepper. Remove twine from beef.
5. Serve immediately with potatoes or `Buttered Spaetzle <#buttered-spaetzle>`__
   and `Braised Red Cabbage <#braised-red-cabbage>`__
