Braised Short Ribs with Bacon and Mushrooms
===========================================

+-----------------------+--------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 3 hours | Yield: 4-6 servings |
+-----------------------+--------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 3 pounds (1.5kg) beef short rib (5 - 6 bones)
- salt and pepper
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 large head of garlic, cut in half with skin on
- 1 1/2 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 750ml bottle red wine
- 3 - 4 cups beef stock
- 3/4 pound (340 g) thick cut bacon, sliced into lardons about 1/2 cm thick
- 8 ounces Portabellini or chestnut mushrooms, halved
- a handful of fresh Italian parsley, roughly chopped

Directions
----------

1. Preheat the oven to 350F. Slice the beef short rib between the bones into
   5 or 6 separate pieces. Season well with salt and pepper.
2. Heat the oil over high heat in a large metal roasting tray (dutch oven or
   large saute pan). Sear the short rib in the oil, bone side up first, turning
   regularly until nicely browned on all sides.
3. Add the garlic sliced side down and brown it well. Stir in the tomato paste,
   mixing it into the beef and cooking it out until it starts to brown slightly.
   Pour in the red wine, bring to a boil, and reduce its volume by half.
4. Add enough beef stock so the meat sits an inch deep in liquid. Bring to a
   boil, then turn off the heat. Cover tightly with foil or a lid and pop into
   the oven for 2 1/2 hours, until the meat is soft and almost falling off the
   bone.
5. For the garnish, about 10 minutes before the meat is ready, heat a frying pan
   and add the bacon lardons. Fry until crispy, then add the mushrooms. Fry them
   together for a few minutes and set aside.
6. Remove the beef from the oven. Using tongs, place the pieces on a platter.
7. Strain the liquid through a sieve into a bowl, pushing all the soft garlic
   through too. Season the sauce and pour it carefully over the beef.
8. Spoon the crispy bacon and mushrooms over the beef and garnish with a
   sprinkle of chopped Italian parsley.
