Slow Cooker Sloppy Joes
=======================

+-----------------------+--------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 7 hours | Yield: 8 sandwiches |
+-----------------------+--------------------+---------------------+

Equipment
---------
Slow Cooker

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 slices high-quality white sandwich bread, torn into quarters
- 1/4 cup whole milk
- 2 pounds (900g) 85 percent lean ground beef
- Salt and pepper
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 2 onions, minced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 (15-ounce / 425 ml) can tomato sauce
- 1 cup ketchup
- 2 teaspoons brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon hot sauce
- 8 hamburger buns

Directions
----------

1. Mash the bread and milk into a paste in a large bowl with a fork. Mix in
   the ground beef, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and 1/2 teaspoon pepper with your
   hands.
2. Heat the oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Add
   the onions, garlic, and chili powder and cook until the onions are
   softened and lightly browned, 8 to 10 minutes.
3. Stir in the beef mixture, 1 pound at a time, breaking up any large pieces
   with a wooden spoon, and cook until no longer pink, about 3 minutes per
   pound. Stir in 1 cup tomato sauce, scraping up any browned bits, then
   transfer to the slow cooker.
4. Stir the remaining tomato sauce, ketchup, sugar, and hot sauce into the
   slow cooker. Cover and cook until the beef is tender, 6 to 8 hours on low
   or 3 to 5 hours on high.
5. Let the meat mixture settle for 5 minutes, then skim the fat from the
   surface with a large spoon. Break up any remaining large pieces of beef
   with the spoon. Season with salt and pepper to taste, spoon onto the buns,
   and serve.
