Grilled Roast
=============

+----------------------+-------------------+-------------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Cook Time: 1 hour | Total Time: 1 1/4 hours | Yield: 6-8 servings |
+----------------------+-------------------+-------------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 3-pound (1350 g) roast
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt
- 1 tablespoon favourite seasoning blend (montreal steak spice, garlic powder and pepper, etc.)

Directions
----------

1. Set up grill for indirect heat.  Place roast on and grill for 1 minute
   per millimeter of width of the roast.
2. Remove from grill and let rest under foil for 15-20 minutes.
