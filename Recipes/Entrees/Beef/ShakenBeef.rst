Bo Luc Lac (Shaking Beef)
=========================

+-------------------------+-----------------+
| Total Time: 1 1/4 hours | Yield: Serves 4 |
+-------------------------+-----------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 4 teaspoons fish sauce
- 2 teaspoons soy sauce
- 2 teaspoons molasses
- 1 pound (450 g) sirloin steak tips, trimmed and cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- 4 ounces (4 cups) watercress, torn into bite-size pieces
- 1/4 cup lime juice (2 limes)
- 1 teaspoon pepper
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 3/4 teaspoon cornstarch
- 4 teaspoons vegetable oil, divided
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 1 small red onion, sliced thin
- 4 teaspoons maggi sauce (substitute half soy sauce and half worcestershire sauce)

Directions
----------

1. Whisk the fish sauce, soy sauce, and molasses together in a medium bowl.
   Add the beef and toss to coat. Let sit at room temperature for 15
   minutes. Spread the watercress in a shallow serving bowl. Combine the
   lime juice and pepper in a small bowl and set aside.
2. Using tongs, transfer the beef to a second medium bowl, letting as much
   marinade as possible drain back into the first bowl. Add the water,
   garlic, and cornstarch to the marinade in the first bowl and whisk to
   combine; set aside. Add 2 teaspoons oil to the beef and toss to coat.
3. Heat 1 teaspoon oil in a 12-inch nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
   until just smoking. Using tongs, wipe the skillet clean with paper
   towels. Add half of the beef to the skillet, leaving space between
   pieces. Cook, swirling the skillet gently and occasionally to capture any
   fond that collects on the bottom, until the beef is browned on the first
   side, 3 to 4 minutes. Keep cooking, stirring and shaking the skillet
   frequently, until the beef is coated and browned and the centre is just
   pink (remove a larger piece and cut in half to check), 2 to 4 minutes
   longer. Transfer the beef to a clean bowl. Wipe the skillet clean with
   wet paper towels and repeat with the remaining 1 teaspoon oil and beef.
4. Melt the butter in the now-empty skillet over medium heat. Add the onion
   and cook, stirring occasionally, until just beginning to soften, about 1
   minute. Add the reserved marinade and bring to a boil. Cook, stirring
   occasionally, until thickened and glossy, about 2 minutes. Add the beef
   and any accumulated juices and toss to coat. Scatter the beef mixture and
   sauce over the watercress. Drizzle 2 teaspoons lime juice mixture over
   the salad. Divide the remaining lime juice mixture among small bowls for
   dipping and serve with the salad.
