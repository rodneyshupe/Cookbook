Orinara Yaki
============

+-----------------------+----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 5 minutes | Yield: 2 servings |
+-----------------------+----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1/2 prawna
- `Orinara Yaki Sauce <#orinara-yaki-dipping-sauce>`__
- microgreens, cilantro or spring onions
- 1/2 lemon

Directions
----------

1. Butterfly prawns: With prawn belly-side up, cut through middle
   lengthwise from underneath head to tail. Then cut from underneath
   head to top of head. Cut through meat but not shell. Open prawn
   carefully. Lightly season meat side of prawns with salt and pepper.
2. Heat oil in skillet at medium heat for approximately 45 seconds,
   just before smoke point. Place butterflied prawns meat-side down.
   Grill for approximately 1 minute to 1 minute 30 seconds until meat is no
   longer translucent and lightly browned. Do not overcook. Flip to shell
   side. Pour sauce over prawns and reduce for approximately 30 seconds to
   1 minute. Squeeze lemon juice over prawns.
3. Plate prawns on serving dish. Place garnish on top and spoon extra
   sauce over prawns, then serve.
