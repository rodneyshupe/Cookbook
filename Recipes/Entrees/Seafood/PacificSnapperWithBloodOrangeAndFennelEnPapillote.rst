Pacific Snapper with Blood Orange and Fennel En Papillote
=========================================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Yield: 2 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 blood orange
- 1 small fennel bulb, sliced thin
- 2 pacific snapper fillets, halved length wise (to create 2 slim pieces)
- olive oil
- salt and pepper

Directions
----------

1. Preheat oven to around 400F. Cut large square of parchment paper.
2. Segment orange (cut top and bottom off, then use knife to remove skin
   and pith), place pieces in small bowl. Once segmented, squeeze juice
   into bowl.
3. In center of parchment, place some fennel, then some orange segments,
   then one half of filet. Add more fennel, orange, and then other filet
   half. Drizzle with olive oil. Season everything with salt and pepper.
4. Bring two opposite sides of parchment paper together over fish. Fold
   edges down to create seal. Crimp edges for tighter closure. Repeat
   folding and crimping process on remaining sides, stopping to leave
   small opening. Pour a tablespoon each white wine and orange juice into
   packet. Finish crimping closed to create sealed packet. Repeat with
   second fish fillet.
5. Place parchment packet(s) on baking sheet and bake for 15 minutes.
   Carefully transfer packet to plate and open with knife (steam will be
   released, so be cautious).
6. Serve fish directly from parchment paper, enjoying flavourful juices
   and vegetables cooked alongside.
