Chipped Ling Cod (Fish and Chips)
=================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 10 minutes | Yield: 2 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 large ling cod filets
- 3/4 cup panko bread crumbs
- 3/4 cup salted potato chips
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup flour
- salt and pepper

Directions
----------

1. Preheat your oven to 425F and ready a parchment-covered baking sheet.
2. Remove skin from ling cod and cut into 1-inch filets.
3. Season the ling cod with sea salt and cracked pepper.
4. On one plate, combine flour with salt and pepper. On a second plate, crush chips and combine with panko. In a shallow bowl, whisk eggs.
5. Dredge each piece of fish in flour, then egg, and finally in the bread crumb mixture before placing on baking sheet.
6. Bake until golden brown, 10 minutes.
7. Serve with tartar sauce.
