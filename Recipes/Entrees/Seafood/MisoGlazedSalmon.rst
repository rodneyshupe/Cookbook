Miso Glazed Salmon
==================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Marinate Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

Miso Glaze
^^^^^^^^^^

- 1/4 cup miso paste, white/yellow variety
- 1/4 cup rice vinegar
- 2 tablespoons sweetener of choice: honey, maple syrup or brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons reduced-sodium soy sauce
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 teaspoon garlic powder)
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger root, grated (or 1 teaspoon ginger powder)

Salmon
^^^^^^

- 1 pound (450 g) salmon, about 4 filets
- 2 tablespoons prepared miso sauce
- 2 green onions sliced

Directions
----------

1. Liberally mist baking sheet with oil spray and set aside.
2. Pat salmon filets dry, place on baking sheet skin side down, and
   brush each with about two tablespoons miso sauce. Allow to marinate
   for about 30 minutes.
3. Preheat oven to 425F and position rack to top spot. Roast salmon on
   top rack for about 10 to 15 minutes.
4. Set oven to broil and finish under broiler for a few minutes to crisp
   tops if desired. (Cook for about four minutes per 1/2-inch thickness or
   until internal temperature reaches 125 to 130F for medium. Add a few
   more minutes for well done; subtract a few for medium rare.)
5. Alternatively, grill salmon. Ensure fish is at room temperature. Place
   salmon on preheated indoor or outdoor grill skin side down. Cook for
   five to eight minutes depending on thickness. Use wide spatula to
   carefully flip to other side and cook for another four to five minutes
   until desired doneness is reached. (Note: Salmon should release easily
   from grill; if it doesn't, wait another one minute or two before
   flipping.) Most
   salmon filets take about 12 minutes to cook through, so watch closely
   to avoid overcooking.
6. Transfer to serving dish, brush each filet with additional miso sauce
   (using clean brush or fresh spoon), and sprinkle on chopped scallions
   if desired.
