Lingcod Cheeks with Asparagus, Chickpeas, and Kale Chips
========================================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 25 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 head of Kale
- 1 cup diced asparagus
- 1 cup chickpeas
- 1/2 cup quartered red radishes
- 1 tablespoon diced shallots
- 2-3 cheeks per person
- olive oil
- butter (optional)

Directions
----------

1. Preheat oven to 300F. Tear kale into bite-size pieces, toss in olive
   oil, season with sea salt and cracked pepper. Place on baking sheet
   and bake until light golden brown and crispy.
2. In medium-sized fry pan, sauté shallots, chickpeas, diced asparagus,
   and radishes together for a few minutes until tender but with no extra
   colour.
3. Season lingcod cheeks with sea salt and cracked pepper.
4. In pan over high heat, sauté cheeks in olive oil for 1 minute per
   side. Add a little butter to the oil for extra richness, then baste
   cheeks (spooning oil and butter over fish) while they rest in pan for
   a few seconds.
5. Place veggies on plate or platter, lay cheeks on top, and arrange
   kale chips around.
