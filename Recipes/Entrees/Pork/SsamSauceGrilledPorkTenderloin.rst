Ssam Sauce Grilled Pork Tenderloin
==================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 40 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 pound (450g) pork tenderloin, trimmed
- olive oil
- salt and ground black pepper
- onion powder
- 1/4 cup `Ssam Sauce <#ssam-sauce>`__

Directions
----------

1. Preheat grill on high. Coat pork tenderloin with olive oil, then season
   with salt, pepper, and onion powder.
2. Place tenderloin on grill and cook for 2 minutes. Flip and cook the other
   side for 2 additional minutes.
3. Switch to indirect grilling for 15 minutes. Baste with a liberal amount of
   ssam sauce and cook for an additional 20 minutes, until internal temp is
   145F.
4. Slice and serve.
