Crispy Slow-Roasted Pork Belly
==============================

+-----------------------------------------------------+-------------------------+
| Total Time: 4 to 4 1/2 hours, plus 12 hours brining | Yield: 8 to 10 servings |
+-----------------------------------------------------+-------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 (3-pound / 1.35 kg) skin-on center-cut fresh pork belly, about 1 1/2 inches thick
- Kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons packed dark brown sugar
- Vegetable oil

Directions
----------

1. Using a sharp chef's knife, slice the pork belly lengthwise into 3 strips
   about 2 inches wide, then make 1/4-inch-deep crosswise cuts through the
   skin and into the fat spaced 1/2 inch apart. Combine 2 tablespoons salt and
   the brown sugar in a small bowl. Rub the salt mixture into the bottom and
   sides of the pork belly (do not rub into the skin). Season the skin of each
   strip evenly with 1/2 teaspoon salt. Place the pork belly, skin side up, in
   a 13 by 9-inch baking dish and refrigerate, uncovered, for at least 12
   hours or up to 24 hours.
2. Adjust an oven rack to the middle position and heat the oven to 250F.
   Transfer the pork belly, skin side up, to a lightly greased wire rack set
   in a rimmed baking sheet. Roast until the meat registers 195F and a paring
   knife inserted in the meat meets little resistance, 3 to 3 1/2 hours,
   rotating the sheet halfway through roasting.
3. Transfer the pork belly, skin side up, to a large plate. (The pork belly
   can be held at room temperature for up to 1 hour.) Pour the fat from the
   sheet into a 1-cup liquid measuring cup. Add vegetable oil as needed to
   equal 1 cup and transfer to a 12-inch skillet. Arrange the pork belly, skin
   side down, in the skillet (strips can be sliced in half crosswise if the
   skillet won't fit them whole) and place over medium heat until bubbles form
   around the pork belly. Continue to fry, tilting the skillet occasionally to
   even out hot spots, until the skin puffs, crisps, and turns golden, 6 to 10
   minutes. Transfer the pork belly, skin side up, to a carving board and let
   rest for 5 minutes. Flip the pork belly on its side and slice 1/2 inch
   thick (being sure to slice through the original score marks). Reinvert the
   slices and serve.

Notes
-----

This recipe requires seasoning and refrigerating the pork belly for at
least 12 hours before cooking. Be sure to ask for a flat, rectangular
center-cut section of skin-on pork belly that's 1 1/2 inches thick with
roughly equal amounts of meat and fat. Serve the meat in small portions
