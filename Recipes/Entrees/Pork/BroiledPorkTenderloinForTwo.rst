Broiled Pork Tenderloin for Two
===============================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Yield: 2 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 pound (450g) pork tenderloin, trimmed
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 3/4 teaspoon vegetable oil
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper
- 1/8 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 (13 by 9-inch) disposable aluminum roasting pan

Directions
----------

1. Adjust oven rack 4 to 5 inches from broiler element and heat oven to 325F.
   Fold thin tip of tenderloin under about 2 inches to create a uniformly
   shaped roast. Tie tenderloin crosswise with kitchen twine at 2-inch
   intervals, securing the folded tip underneath. Trim excess twine close to
   meat to keep it from scorching under the broiler.
2. Mix salt, oil, and pepper in a small bowl until salt is evenly coated with
   oil. Add baking soda and stir until well combined. Rub mixture evenly over
   pork, then place tenderloin in disposable pan.
3. Turn oven to broil. Immediately place meat in oven and broil tenderloin for
   5 minutes. Flip tenderloin and continue to broil until golden brown and
   meat registers 125F to 130F, 8 to 14 minutes. Remove disposable pan from
   oven, tent loosely with aluminum foil, and let rest for 10 minutes. Remove
   twine, slice tenderloin into 1/2-inch-thick slices, and serve.
