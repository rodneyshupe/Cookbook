Stuffed Pork Loin Roast
=======================

+-----------------------+-------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 30 minutes | Cook Time: 1 hour | Marinate Time: 1 hour | Yield: 6 servings |
+-----------------------+-------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

Marinade
^^^^^^^^

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 teaspoons soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- 2 teaspoons whole-grain mustard
- 1 teaspoon honey
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper

Roast
^^^^^

- 1 3-pound (1.35 kg) Pork Loin Roast
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 2-3 red peppers, sliced to 4 sides pieces.
- 200g mozzarella or jack cheese, grated
- 1/2 cup flat leaf parsley, chopped
- 4 russet potatoes, peeled and sliced in 1/2 inch disks
- 1 onion, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika

Directions
----------

1. Combine marinade ingredients in a small bowl and set aside.
2. Butterfly or roll-cut the roast to create a flat piece about 1/2 inch
   thick. Cover with plastic wrap and pound flat until even. Spread half the
   marinade over one side. Roll the loin into a log and place in the fridge
   for at least 1 hour.
3. While the roast marinates, preheat oven to 400F. Heat a nonstick pan over
   med-high heat. Add 1 tablespoon vegetable oil and place peppers skin side
   down, cooking until the skin starts to blacken. Flip and cover with a lid
   until softened. Remove from the pan, wrap in foil, and let sit for 15
   minutes. Remove from foil and peel the skin off the peppers.
4. Unroll the loin and place the peppers on it. Sprinkle with cheese and
   parsley, then roll the roast back up and tie with butcher twine.
5. On a sheet pan, add potatoes, onions, and garlic. Sprinkle with olive oil,
   salt, oregano, and paprika. Toss to combine.
6. Create a space in the middle of the pan for the roast, then baste the roast
   with half of the remaining marinade. Cover the roast and potato mixture
   with foil and seal tight. Place in the preheated oven and bake for 40
   minutes.
7. Remove the foil, brush with the remaining marinade, and return to the oven
   for 20 minutes.
8. Remove the twine and slice. Serve alongside the potatoes.
