Barbecue Ribs
=============

+-----------------------+------------------------+--------------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 1 1/2 hours | Marinate Time: 1-4 hours | Yield: 4-6 servings |
+-----------------------+------------------------+--------------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 racks pork ribs (I prefer to use side ribs)
- 1 cup `Basic Barbecue Rub <#basic-barbecue-rub>`__
- 2 cups wood chips, preferably apple wood, soaked in 2 cups apple
  cider for 1 hour then drained
- 1 cup `Kansas City Style Sweet and Smoky Barbecue Sauce <#kansas-city-style-sweet-and-smoky-barbecue-sauce>`__

Directions
----------

1. Pull the papery skin off the back of each rack of ribs in a sheet with your
   fingers or a pair of pliers.
2. Sprinkle the ribs on both sides with two thirds of the rub, patting it in
   with your fingers. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for 1 - 4
   hours.
3. Set up the grill for indirect grilling. For a charcoal grill, toss the wood
   chips on the coals; for a gas grill, place the chips in a smoker box. Do
   not grill until you see smoke.
4. Place the ribs in the center of the grill away from the fire. Indirect
   grill for about 1 hour, keeping the temperature around 325F.
5. Brush the ribs with half of the cup of barbecue sauce, setting aside the
   other half for serving at the table.
6. Increase the heat and continue indirect grilling until the ribs are done,
   15-30 minutes. The meat will be very tender and pulled back from the ends
   of the bones.
7. Transfer the ribs to a platter. Sprinkle with the remaining rub and serve
   with the other half cup of barbecue sauce.
