Creamy Sausage Penne
====================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 4-6 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 4 slices raw bacon, diced fairly small
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 3/4 cup panko bread crumbs
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2/3 pound (300 g) spicy Italian sausage, (casings removed if in sausage form)
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 cup whipping cream
- 2/3 pound penne pasta
- 1/3 cup shredded Parmesan cheese
- 1 1/2 tablespoons fresh chopped parsley

Directions
----------

1. Add bacon to a non-stick pan and cook until just getting crispy, then
   remove to a paper towel to drain.
2. Remove the bacon grease, leaving about a tablespoon, then add 1 tablespoon
   of the butter to the pan. Once melted, add the bread crumbs and stir often
   over medium high heat until golden brown. Add back the bacon, stir well to
   combine, and remove to a small bowl, set aside.
3. Heat oil in a medium hot pan, add the sausage and break apart to crumble.
   Cook until no longer pink, then stir in the garlic and cook another minute.
   Add the cream and simmer on low, about 10 minutes.
4. While it cooks, add the pasta to boiling water; when the pasta's ready, so
   is the sausage. Drain the pasta and add to the sausage. Add the remaining
   butter, Parmesan cheese, and parsley, mix well.
5. Serve topped with the bacon/bread crumbs.
