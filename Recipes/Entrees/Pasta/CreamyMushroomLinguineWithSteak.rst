Creamy Mushroom Linguine with Steak
===================================

+-------------------+
| Yield: 4 servings |
+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 stiploin steaks (10 ounces/300g each), cooked to medium rare
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
- 200 grams of chestnut mushrooms
- 1 tablespoon fresh rosemary, chopped, plus extra to garnish
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 200 millilitres of double cream
- 400 grams of linguine
- Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper

Directions
----------

1. Remove the steaks from the fridge 30 minutes before cooking so they come up
   to room temperature.
2. For the creamy mushroom sauce, set a frying pan over medium heat and add
   the oil. Stir in the onion and garlic, and cook for two minutes.
3. Add the mushrooms, season with salt and pepper, and continue cooking,
   tossing every minute or so, for 10 minutes.
4. Add the fresh rosemary and lemon juice, and cook for another minute. Pour
   in the cream, stir, reduce the heat to low, and cook for five minutes. Keep
   warm over low heat.
5. Meanwhile, set a large saucepan of salted boiling water over high heat,
   stir in the pasta, and cook until al dente, 10 minutes. Drain, reserving a
   couple of tablespoons of the cooking water.
6. Leave the steaks to rest for about two minutes before serving so the juices
   drawn to the surface relax back into the meat.
7. Return the cooked pasta to the large saucepan over low heat with the
   reserved pasta cooking water and the creamy mushroom sauce. Toss together
   and serve in warmed dishes.
8. Thinly slice the steaks and serve on top of the creamy mushroom linguine,
   scattered with some chopped rosemary and a twist of black pepper.

Variations
----------

- Prawns: Substitute 1 pound prawns (peeled and deveined) for the steak, add
  them to mushroom mixture with cream to cook.
- Ham/Capicola: Replace mushrooms with 1/2 pound ham or capicola, cubed into
  1/2 pieces. Remove steak, but add 1/2 cup peas (thawed from frozen) to
  sauce right before adding pasta.
