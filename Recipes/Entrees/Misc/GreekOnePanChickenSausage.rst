Greek One Pan Chicken Sausage
=============================

+------------------------------+--------------------------+----------------------------------+--------------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes (est.) | Cook Time: 1 hour (est.) | Marinate Time: 30 minutes (est.) | Yield: 6 servings (est.) |
+------------------------------+--------------------------+----------------------------------+--------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

Marinade
^^^^^^^^

- 1/3 cup extra virgin olive oil, plus more for serving
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 4 cloves garlic - chopped
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon coarse ground black pepper

Chicken & Sausage
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

- 6 bone in chicken thighs or chicken legs, uncooked
- 6 uncooked sausages, any kind your stomach desires - chicken, Italian, lamb
- 1 pound whole small red potatoes, cut in half
- 1 large onion, sliced - red, white, yellow you pick
- 2 peppers, green, red, yellow, pasilla you pick
- 2 tablespoons chopped parsley

Directions
----------

1. Combine all marinade ingredients in a small bowl - mix well to combine.
2. Preheat oven to 400 degrees
3. Put chicken, sausage and vegetables (not the parsley) into a large bowl
   and add marinade - mix well to make sure eveything is covered
4. Arrange on a baking sheet or put in a large oven-proof bowl and bake about
   an hour, or until chicken registers 180 degrees
5. Put on a serving platter, and drizzle with a little more olive oil and
   sprinkle with parsley
