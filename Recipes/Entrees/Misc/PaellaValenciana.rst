Paella Valenciana
=================

+-----------------------+------------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Total Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 3-4 servings |
+-----------------------+------------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 1/4 pounds (600g) chicken leg pieces, rabbit pieces, or a mix
- Salt and pepper
- 1/4 pound (125g) green beans or runner beans, cut into 1 1/2-inch segments
- 1/4 pound (125g) fresh or frozen lima, butter, or fava beans
- A few cloves of garlic, finely minced
- 1 ripe Roma tomato, split in half, the cut-side rubbed through the large holes of a box grater, skins discarded
- 1 teaspoon Spanish smoked paprika
- Pinch saffron threads
- 1 cup (around 400g) short grain rice, preferably Bomba (arborio or any other rice will do fine)
- 2.5 cups (1200ml) water
- 2-3 rosemary sprigs
- Lemon wedges for serving

Directions
----------

1. Heat olive oil in a paella or large carbon steel or cast iron skillet over
   high heat until shimmering. Reduce heat to medium, add chicken and/or
   rabbit, season with salt and pepper, and cook, stirring occasionally, until
   lightly browned on most sides, about 5 minutes.
2. Add the green beans and lima beans and stir into the chicken. Add garlic
   and immediately stir into the other ingredients to prevent burning. When
   the garlic is aromatic, about 1 minute, push everything to the side of the
   pan and add tomatoes directly to the center. Let the tomatoes cook down a
   bit, then stir them into the other ingredients.
3. Push everything to the sides again and add smoked paprika and saffron
   directly to the center. Bloom the spices by stirring them in the olive oil
   and rendered chicken fat until fragrant, about 30 seconds, then stir
   everything together.
4. Add rice and cook, stirring frequently, until lightly toasted and coated in
   the bloomed spice mixture, about 2 minutes. Add water and rosemary sprigs.
   Use a wooden spoon or spatula to scrape up any browned bits from the bottom
   and stir everything together well, making sure the rice is all submerged.
5. Bring to a simmer and cook without stirring until the rice is tender and
   the liquid is absorbed (you may need to add more liquid if it gets too dry
   before the rice is fully cooked; just pour it evenly around the top), 15 to
   20 minutes total.
6. If desired, keep cooking until a crispy crust forms on the bottom of the
   rice (test this by scraping some rice off the bottom with a stiff wooden
   spatula). Serve immediately with lemon wedges.
