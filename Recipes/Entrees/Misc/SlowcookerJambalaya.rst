Slowcooker Jambalaya
====================

+-----------------------+--------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 8 hours | Yield: 6-8 servings |
+-----------------------+--------------------+---------------------+

Equipment
---------
Slow Cooker

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 pound (450 g) andouille sausage, sliced
- 2 stalks celery, thinly sliced
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1 green bell pepper, finely chopped
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon Cajun seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1 can (28 ounces / 828 ml) diced tomatoes
- 2 skinless, boneless chicken breasts, cut into bite size pieces
- 1 cup uncooked long grain rice
- 1 pound (450 g) large peeled and deveined shrimp

Directions
----------

1. Heat a large skillet on medium high with a teaspoon of oil, then add the
   sausage and cook until it starts to brown, 5 minutes. Remove from the
   skillet and add to the slow cooker.
2. In the now empty pan add additional oil if needed, then put in the celery,
   onion and green pepper. Cook until softened and just until they get some
   colour, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the garlic and, when fragrant, 30 seconds, add the tomato paste and
   stir to combine, cooking for 1 minute. Add the Cajun seasoning, cayenne,
   oregano and thyme and stir well to mix through. Cook for about 2 minutes,
   then remove and add to the slow cooker.
4. Deglaze the pan with some of the chicken broth and add the liquid to the
   slow cooker along with the rest of the broth.
5. Add the diced tomatoes and chicken to the slow cooker and set to cook for
   7-8 hours on low (3-4 hours on high), stirring occasionally. *Note: For a
   smokier flavour, grill the chicken before adding it to the slow cooker.*
6. With one hour left in the cooking time, stir in the rice.
7. With 15 minutes left, stir in the raw shrimp.
8. Stir it up and serve.

Variations
----------

- **Seafood Fiesta:** Swap the chicken for a mix of your favourite seafood - think mussels, clams, and crab.
- **Vegetarian Twist:** Skip the meat and load up on veggies like bell peppers, okra, and tomatoes.
