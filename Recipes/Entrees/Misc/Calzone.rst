Calzone
=======

+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 30 minutes | Total Time: 45 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 pound `pizza dough <#pizza-dough>`__
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1/2 cup pizza sauce
- 1/2 cup ricotta cheese
- 1/2 cup pepperoni
- 1/4 cup salami
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 tablespoon water
- 1/4 cup parmesan cheese

Directions
----------

1. Preheat the oven to 475F; line the baking sheet with parchment paper or
   spray it with oil.
2. Divide the dough into 4 equally sized pieces; roll each piece into a 1/4
   inch thick circle.
3. Place the filling on half of each circle (pizza sauce, ricotta, pepperoni,
   salami and shredded mozzarella), leaving the edges clean to allow for
   sealing; combine egg and water and brush the edges with egg wash, then fold
   the side over the filling and crimp the edges.
4. Place calzones on a baking sheet, leaving space apart; brush the top with
   egg wash, cut a small vent on the top of each, then brush the tops with oil
   and sprinkle with parmesan cheese. Bake until golden brown, 15 minutes.
