Vegetable Fajitas
=================

+----------------------+------------------------+----------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Total Time: 15 minutes | Yield: 4 |
+----------------------+------------------------+----------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 green pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 red pepper, thinly sliced
- 1/2 large red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 zucchini, thinly sliced
- 1/2 teaspoon each cumin, garlic powder, oregano & cayenne
- Tortillas
- Sour cream
- Monterey Jack cheese, shredded

Directions
----------

1. Heat olive oil in large skillet.
2. Add both peppers and saute until they begin to soften, about 5 minutes.
3. Add onion and continue, mixing well, another 5 minutes or so, then add
   zucchini.
4. Saute all another couple of minutes, then add about 3 tablespoons water and
   cover pan with a lid for 2 minutes — you want everything soft.
5. Remove lid, add herbs, and mix well.
6. Turn down heat and warm your tortillas.
7. Place a little sour cream, some vegetables, and a little cheese in each
   cooked tortilla and roll up.
