Red Lentil Curry
================

+----------------------+------------------------+----------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Total Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 3 |
+----------------------+------------------------+----------+

A 30-minute red lentil curry.

Ingredients
-----------

- 2/3 cup dry red lentils (thoroughly rinsed in cold water + drained)
- 2 tablespoons coconut oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced (3 cloves yield ~1 1/2 tablespoons)
- 1 teaspoon minced ginger
- 1/2 cup diced carrots
- 3 tablespoons red curry paste
- 1 6 ounces (approximately 156 ml) can tomato paste (a 6-ounce can yields ~1/2 cup)
- 2 cups low-sodium vegetable broth (DIY or store-bought)
- 1 cup water
- 1-2 tablespoons coconut sugar (or maple syrup)
- 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric (plus more to taste)
- 1/3 cup light coconut milk (optional)
- Cooked brown rice
- Fresh chopped cilantro
- Pickled red onion*
- Pita or naan (omit if gluten free / check for vegan friendliness)

Directions
----------

1. Rinse lentils thoroughly in a fine mesh strainer and set aside; if serving
   with brown rice, cook it now using this method from Saveur.
2. Heat a large rimmed skillet over medium heat; once hot, add coconut oil,
   garlic, ginger, and carrots and saute, stirring frequently, 2 minutes.
3. Add curry paste and saute, stirring frequently, 2 minutes.
4. Add tomato paste, vegetable broth, and water and stir to combine, then add
   lentils, coconut sugar, and turmeric and stir.
5. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, then reduce heat slightly to low (or
   medium-low) and gently simmer until lentils are tender, 20 minutes,
   stirring frequently to incorporate flavours and adding more vegetable broth
   as needed if the mixture becomes too thick.
6. Optional but recommended: stir in coconut milk for added creaminess and to
   balance the heat of the curry paste.
7. Taste and adjust seasonings as needed, adding more turmeric for depth of
   flavour, coconut sugar for sweetness/flavour balance, or a pinch of salt
   for saltiness.
8. Divide brown rice and lentils between 2-3 serving bowls and garnish with
   cilantro, pickled onions, and pita or naan (optional).
9. Store leftovers separately in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Reheat on
   the stovetop, adding water as needed for moisture, or in the microwave.
