Dal Makhani
===========

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Equipment
---------
Electric Pressure Cooker

Ingredients
-----------

- 3/4 cup dried red lentils (masoor dal)
- 1/4 cup dried kidney beans (rajma)
- 2 tablespoons butter or ghee
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 2 medium tomatoes, finely chopped or blended into a paste
- 1 teaspoon red chili powder
- 2 teaspoons garam masala
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 1/2 cups water (or vegetable broth for extra flavour)
- Salt to taste
- 2 tablespoons heavy cream (optional)
- Fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish (optional)

Directions
----------

1. Rinse red lentils and kidney beans thoroughly; soak a few hours or
   overnight for faster cooking, but not essential with red lentils.
2. Heat butter or ghee over medium heat in a pressure cooker or Instant Pot;
   add garlic, ginger, and diced onion and saute until onion is translucent,
   about 5 minutes.
3. Stir in red chili powder, garam masala, coriander, cumin, and turmeric;
   cook until fragrant, another minute.
4. Add chopped tomatoes (or tomato puree) and cook until softened and slightly
   caramelised, 2-3 minutes.
5. Add rinsed red lentils and kidney beans with the water (or vegetable
   broth); season with salt to taste.
6. Close the Instant Pot lid and cook on high pressure for 5 minutes; let
   pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then release remaining pressure
   manually.
7. Open the pot and check the lentils and kidney beans; they should be tender
   but not mushy. If needed, simmer on the stovetop a few more minutes.
8. *(Optional)* For a richer texture, mash a portion of the dal against the
   side of the pot with a spoon, or partially puree a small amount with an
   immersion blender.
9. Stir in heavy cream, if using, and adjust salt to taste.
10. Serve hot, garnished with chopped fresh cilantro (optional).

Notes
-----

- Adjust the amount of red chili powder for your desired level of spice.
- To make this recipe vegan, omit the butter or ghee and use coconut milk
  instead of heavy cream.
- Leftovers of Dal Makhani taste even better the next day, as the flavours
  develop further, so make a big batch and enjoy throughout the week.
