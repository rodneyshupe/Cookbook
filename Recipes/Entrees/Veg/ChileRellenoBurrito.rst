Chile Relleno Burrito
=====================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 25 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Yield: 4 burritos |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 4 large poblano peppers
- 1 block Mexican Manchego Cheese (substitute: Monterey Jack)
- 3 eggs, separated
- pinch of salt
- 1 cup all purpose flour
- 1 batch `Mexican Rice <#quick-mexican-rice>`__
- 4 large flour tortillas

Directions
----------

1. Blacken poblanos, then place in a covered bowl 5 minutes; peel and make a
   small incision to remove seeds, then put the cheese block inside.
2. Beat egg whites until beginning to get fluffy, not stiff; beat egg yolks
   with salt, then fold yolks into whites; bread peppers and shallow fry.
3. Layer flour tortilla, rice, pepper, rice, then roll.
