Yogurt-Marinated Grilled Boneless Leg of Lamb
=============================================

+-----------------------+-----------------------+--------------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 20 minutes | Marinate Time: 1-3 hours | Yield: 8 servings |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+--------------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 5 pounds (2.25 kg) boneless leg of lamb
- Salt and pepper
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- Zest and juice of one lemon
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 teaspoons oregano, preferably Greek

Directions
----------

1. Lay the lamb flat on a cutting board and trim the visible pockets of
   interior fat, leaving the fat cap on the exterior; season well with salt
   and pepper.
2. Whisk together the yogurt, lemon zest and juice, olive oil, garlic and
   oregano in a small bowl; rub the marinade all over the lamb and place it in
   a gallon-sized plastic bag, then refrigerate at least one hour and up to 3
   hours.
3. Remove the lamb before cooking and let it come to room temperature; heat
   grill to medium and cook, turning once, until it reaches an internal
   temperature of 125F for medium-rare, about 20 minutes.
4. Rest the lamb loosely tented with foil at least twenty minutes before
   carving, then slice against the grain into thin slices and serve. (Tzatziki
   sauce, harissa or mint jelly all make nice accompaniments.)
