Macadamia Nut Crusted Rack of Lamb with a Merlot Demi-glace
===========================================================

+-----------------------+-------------------+-------------------+
| Prep Time: 25 minutes | Cook Time: 1 hour | Yield: 4 servings |
+-----------------------+-------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 head garlic
- 4 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 2 rack lamb
- kosher salt
- fresh cracked pepper
- 3.5 ounce (100g) macadamia nuts, roughly chopped
- 5 1/4 ounce (150g) bread crumbs
- 5 teaspoons parsley, chopped
- 2 clove garlic, minced

Directions
----------

Lamb
^^^^

1. Preheat oven to 425F; remove the outer papery covering of the garlic
   and slice off the top of the head so most of the cloves are exposed.
2. Place the garlic heads on a baking sheet and drizzle with olive oil,
   then roast in the oven for 45 minutes or until the cloves are
   completely soft and begin to caramelise. Let the garlic cool, then
   squeeze each clove out of the head and mash into a paste with a fork.
3. Place the lamb on a plate and season all over. Heat a skillet, add 2
   tablespoons extra virgin olive oil, then sear the lamb racks fatty
   side first and remove from the skillet.
4. Spread the garlic paste on the fatty side of the racks. Combine chopped
   nuts and bread crumbs, then pack onto the lamb, pressing firmly. (Tip:
   Combine the nuts and bread crumbs in a food processor and chop.)
5. Reduce oven to 375F, place lamb on a baking sheet, and bake until rose,
   approx. 25 minutes. Let rest 5 minutes once out of the oven, then slice
   the racks and drizzle sauce over top.

Merlot demi-glace sauce
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

1. Heat a sauce pan (or use the skillet from above), add olive oil, add
   shallots, season, and sear for 2 minutes; add wine and reduce until
   almost all the liquid is gone, then add the demi-glace and simmer.
2. Just before plating, whisk in whole butter and chopped parsley.
