Herbed Grill-Roasted Lamb
=========================

+--------------------+-------------------+
| Total Time: 1 hour | Yield: 4 servings |
+--------------------+-------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- `Herb Mustard Rub <#herb-mustard-rub>`__
- 1 2-pound (900 g) boneless butterflied leg of lamb

Directions
----------

1. Lay the lamb flat on your work surface (it should be roughly rectangular)
   and trim any visible sinew, but don't trim anything holding sections of the
   leg together. Spread about a third of the rub over the meat, then roll the
   lamb up tightly from one short end to the other and tie snugly at 1-inch
   intervals with kitchen twine; spread the remaining rub all over the rolled,
   tied roast.
2. Heat the grill to medium and set up for indirect grilling, between 325 and
   375F, adjusting as needed. Add the roast and grill about 20 minutes, using
   tongs or a meat fork to turn and rotate it for even cooking, then turn it
   so the side farthest from the fire is now closest, keeping it on the cool
   side of the grill.
3. Cook until the middle of the thickest section reads 125F for medium rare on
   an instant-read thermometer, about 20 minutes after turning the roast. Roll
   the lamb briefly over the hot side of the fire to sear all sides, about 2
   minutes per side, then cover with foil and rest 10 to 15 minutes before
   carving.
