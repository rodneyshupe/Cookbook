Portuguese Francesinha Sandwich
===============================

+----------------------+------------------------+-----------------+
| Prep Time: 5 minutes | Total Time: 30 minutes | Yield: 2 people |
+----------------------+------------------------+-----------------+

Ingredients
-----------

Sauce
^^^^^

- 1/2 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 clove garlic minced
- 1 onion chopped
- 1 large blonde beer
- 1/2 cup beef broth
- 1 cup canned tomato soup concentrate
- 2 tablespoons port wine
- 1 tablespoon piripiri sauce (Portuguese chili)
- 1 tablespoon salted butter
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch

Francesinhas
^^^^^^^^^^^^

- 5-6 ounces (140-170 g) beef striploin
- kosher salt
- black pepper
- 4-6 linguicas (Portuguese sausage) (can substitute chorizo)
- 2 slices of white bread
- 4 slices of ham
- 8 slices of edem cheese

Directions
----------

Sauce
^^^^^

1. Heat the olive oil in a big saucepan and add the garlic and the onions;
   cook them until golden.
2. Pour the beer and simmer until the alcohol evaporates (5 minutes).
3. Pour the beef broth, the tomato soup, the port wine, the Piri Piri
   (chili) and the butter to give shine to the sauce, then add the
   cornstarch slowly avoiding lumps and simmer for a couple of minutes
   until the sauce is thick like in the pictures.

Francesinhas
^^^^^^^^^^^^

1. Preheat the oven to 180 C (350F).
2. Cook the beef chopped in small strings; season with salt and black
   pepper.
3. Cook the linguicas sliced in half lengthwise.
4. Fill the bread with the sausages, the beef and the ham roughly chopped,
   then cover the francesinhas with the cheese and melt in the oven for 5
   minutes.
