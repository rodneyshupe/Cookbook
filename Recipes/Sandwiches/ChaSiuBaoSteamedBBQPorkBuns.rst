Cha Siu Bao
===========

Steamed BBQ Pork Buns

+---------------------+
| Yield: 14 pork buns |
+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- `Steamed bun dough <#bao-buns>`__
- 1/2 cup chicken stock
- 1 tablespoon corn starch
- 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 2 tablespoons Hoisin sauce
- 2 tablespoons Dark soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons Sugar
- 1 tablespoon Shaoxing wine, Chinese cooking wine (Can be substitute with dry sherry or water)
- Small pinch of Chinese five spice
- 2 tablespoons Cooking oil
- 1 Medium size onion, small diced (Approximately 1 cup)
- 1 Clove garlic, minced
- 10 ounces (285 g) Small chopped or ground pork. (You can use pork loin, shoulder or belly)
- 14 pieces 2 1/2" cut parchment paper, cupcake paper cup or coffee filter
- 1/8 teaspoon black pepper

Directions
----------

1. Make the steamed bun dough first, unless making the filling a day ahead.
2. While the dough rises, make the filling: in a mixing bowl combine stock,
   corn starch, oyster, hoisin, and soy sauces, sugar, Shaoxing, and 5 spice,
   then whisk until sugar is dissolved and set aside.
3. Heat a large skillet or wok over high heat; add oil and swirl to coat, then
   add diced onion and garlic and stir fry until onion is softened and
   transparent, 2 to 3 minutes.
4. Add pork and stir fry until pieces are slightly browned, 5 minutes.
5. Add the sauce and bring to a boil, then reduce heat to medium and simmer
   until it thickens, 5 minutes, stirring frequently; transfer to a bowl and
   cover, then let cool in the refrigerator for 1 to 2 hours or in the freezer
   for 30 minutes.
6. While the filling cools, divide dough into 14 equal 2 ounce portions and
   roll each on your palm; place them on a parchment paper/coffee filter lined
   baking sheet or flat surface, cover with plastic wrap or a towel, and let
   rest an additional 20 minutes.
7. When dough and filling are ready, bring one piece of dough to the work
   surface and slightly flatten with your palm, then use a rolling pin to
   flatten into a 3" circle, keeping the center slightly thicker than the
   edge.
8. Put 1 Tablespoon of filling in the center and bring the edge to the top,
   sealing with your index finger and thumb while your left thumb pushes the
   filling inside; place the sealed bun on parchment paper, a cupcake paper
   cup, or a coffee filter.
9. Arrange the buns back on a baking sheet or flat surface, cover with plastic
   wrap or a towel, and let rest an additional 20 minutes.
10. When the buns are well rested, steam them: fill a wok or steamer pot with
    water and bring to a boil over high heat, then place buns into a steamer
    rack with paper underneath. Keep them at least 1" apart, then bring the
    rack to the boiling steamer, making sure the water is not touching the
    buns, and cover and steam until fluffy and fully cooked, 12 to 14 minutes.
11. Tilt the lid a tiny bit for slow air circulation for 2 to 3 minutes before
    opening; if you open the lid right away the fluffy buns will sink down.
    Serve hot.
