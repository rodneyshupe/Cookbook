New York Deli Tuna Salad Sandwich
=================================

+-----------------------+----------------------+----------------------+---------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 5 minutes | Total Time: 12 hours | Yield: 2 sandwiches |
+-----------------------+----------------------+----------------------+---------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

Tuna Salad
^^^^^^^^^^

- 2 tablespoons onion finely diced
- 2 tablespoons celery finely diced
- 1 can 5 ounce (148 ml) tuna drained
- 1/4 teaspoon celery salt
- 1/2 tablespoon sweet relish
- 1/4 teaspoon soy sauce
- 5 tablespoons mayo (1/3 cup)

Sandwich
^^^^^^^^

- 4 slices toasted rye or whole-wheat bread
- 2 slices american cheese
- lettuce or spinach
- sliced tomato (optional)

Directions
----------

1. Finely dice 2 tablespoons onion and 2 tablespoons celery; drain one 5-ounce
   can of water-packed solid white albacore tuna very well, then place it in a
   large bowl with the celery and onion. Add celery salt, sweet relish, and
   soy sauce; mix thoroughly, ideally by hand. Let sit 10 minutes.
2. After 10 minutes, add mayonnaise and mix well; season with salt and pepper
   to taste.
3. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate overnight. Note: You can eat it
   right away, but it is way better after overnight.
4. Put half the tuna mixture on a slice of toasted bread, top with a slice of
   cheese, and broil until melted, 2-3 minutes; top with lettuce, tomato, and
   the second slice of bread.
