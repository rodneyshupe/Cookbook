Homemade Jerky
==============

+-----------------------+---------------------------+------------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Marinate Time: 8-24 hours | Drying Time: 6-8 hours |
+-----------------------+---------------------------+------------------------+

Equipment
---------
Dehydrator

Ingredients
-----------

- 2 1/4 pounds (1kg) Beef, flank, top round or any other cheaper non-fatty
  type works best
- Marinade

Directions
----------

1. **Meat**: Trim as much of the fat off as possible, then slice with the
   grain into 3-6 mm (1/8" - 1/4") thick strips. Any cut works, but meat
   with a high fat content will become rancid faster; top round steak or flank
   steak work well, and eye of round rouladin cut saves time. To make slicing
   easier, partially freeze the meat for 2-3 hours. For tough cuts or wild
   game, pound with a mallet.
2. **Marinade**: Combine the marinade ingredients, coat the strips of meat,
   and place in a glass or plastic (non-metallic) container (a 1 gallon
   ziplock bag works well); cover and refrigerate overnight.

Dehydration
-----------

1. Remove the meat strips from the refrigerator, place on oiled smoker
   racks, and air dry for at least one hour.
2. If the marinade uses liquid smoke, dehydrate in the smoker or oven at
   approx. 70C (160F). In the smoker, keep the vent fully open and no
   water in the drip bowl; in the oven, leave the door open slightly with
   a wooden spoon to let moisture escape.
3. If smoke is required, use the smoker as above with hickory flavour
   bisquettes for 2-3 hours, then just heat for the remaining time.
4. Dehydration can take approximately 6 to 8 hours; rotate the racks
   every hour.
5. Jerky is ready when you can tear the strips along the grain — pliable
   but not soft, fairly stiff but not brittle.
6. Store in a clean, dry container lined with a paper towel and a loose
   fitting lid. Jerky is shelf stable for about 2 weeks at room
   temperature and one month in the refrigerator.
