Teriyaki Beef Jerky
===================

+-----------------------+----------------------------------+
| Prep Time: 15 minutes | Yield: about 1 1/2 cups marinade |
+-----------------------+----------------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 3/4 cups `teriyaki sauce <#teriyaki-sauce>`__
- 3/4 tablespoon hickory flavour liquid smoke
- 3/8 cups brown sugar
- 1/2 tablespoon onion powder
- 1/2 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1/2 tablespoon pepper (optional)

Directions
----------

1. Mix the teriyaki sauce and liquid smoke in a medium pot; heat until hot to
   the touch, then add the brown sugar and stir until dissolved, about 5
   minutes. Cool to room temperature.
