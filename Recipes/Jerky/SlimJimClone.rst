Slim Jim Clone
==============

+-----------------------+----------------------+-------------------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Drying Time: 5 hours | Yield: about 3/4 pound sticks |
+-----------------------+----------------------+-------------------------------+

Equipment
---------
Dehydrator

Ingredients
-----------

- 3/8 teaspoons curing salt (prague powder #1)
- 2 tablespoons cold water
- 1 tablespoon liquid smoke (hickory)
- 2 1/2 tablespoons paprika
- 1 3/4 tablespoons ground mustard
- 1/4 teaspoons celery seed
- 1/4 teaspoons black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoons white pepper
- 1/4 teaspoons red pepper
- 1 tablespoon corn syrup
- 2 pounds (900 g) of ground lean meat

Directions
----------

1. Dissolve Prague Powder in water and liquid smoke, then blend together with
   other dry ingredients, corn syrup, and meat.
2. Using jerky gun, create sticks using round tip.
3. Dehydrate 4 1/2 - 5 hours on high setting.
4. Using a jerky gun, load the gun with the ground beef mixture and shoot 6"
   sticks, using round tip, onto a dehydrator tray or on a baking sheet.
5. Dehydrate at 160F for 4 1/2 to 5 hours.
