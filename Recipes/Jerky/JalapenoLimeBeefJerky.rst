Jalapeno Lime Beef Jerky
========================

+-----------------------+----------------------------------+
| Prep Time: 20 minutes | Yield: about 2 1/2 cups marinade |
+-----------------------+----------------------------------+

Ingredients
-----------

- 1 1/3 cup orange juice
- 1/2 cup lime juice (freshly squeezed)
- 4 tablespoons honey
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon garlic
- 4 diced jalapenos (seeds removed)
- 3/8 teaspoons Curing Salt (Prague Powder #1)(optional)

Directions
----------

1. Blend the orange juice, lime juice, honey, ground cumin, salt, garlic, and
   diced jalapenos until the jalapenos are cut small and the honey is evenly
   mixed, then transfer to a bowl or ziplock bag.
